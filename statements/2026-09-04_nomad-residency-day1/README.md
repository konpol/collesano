## Summary — speaker by speaker

**Gioacchino Di Garbo (Madonie Smart Tourism coordinator).** The festival sits inside the two-year "Madonie Smart Tourism" project **led by the Municipality of Collesano** with Castelbuono. Achievements presented: eight Mediaset "Planet" episodes (two on the Targa Florio), a professional rally simulator built from half a real car, and the "Bell'Italia" TV segment whose first episode was filmed on the Madonie. He invites nomads to share the slow, relationship-rich life of the villages.

**Tiziana Cascio (mayor of Collesano, lead municipality).** Stresses the teamwork behind promoting the whole territory and calls hosting the festival in Castelbuono a "mature choice" given its operational coworking (2,300 attendances). Digital nomads and new ways of working are "the new key for the communities to be reborn" — a bet embraced by all the Madonie municipalities together with SO.SVI.MA.

**Mario Cicero (mayor of Castelbuono).** The territory can show national and European politics an alternative to funneling people into polluted metropolises. He backed the coworking idea immediately during Covid; beyond visitor numbers, people — including Northern-European professionals — have chosen to **move and live** in the area, Collesano included. The Madonie model: reject heavy industry, bet on agri-food and clean environment (Fiasconaro's panettone as proof).

**Alessandro Ficile (SO.SVI.MA.).** Temporary residents, starting with digital nomads, can regenerate fragile inner areas; this event opens a series of actions planned for at least three years, in a "polycentric" logic where Castelbuono is the most mature node but the whole district participates.

> **Why this matters for the Observatory:** these are the same administrators criticized in the political statements — here presenting their strongest public narrative. The opposition's counterpoint (simulator container placement, events vs structural projects) is tracked in [governance.md](../../topics/governance.md) and [nomad-residency.md](../../topics/nomad-residency.md).
