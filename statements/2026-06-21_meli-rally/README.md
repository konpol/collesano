## Summary of the speech

Meli's core message: *"a real mayor tells the truth, accepts mistakes, and doesn't push blame onto others"* — and this administration, which he himself helped elect, failed that test on the town's strategic projects:

- **Depuratore:** the administration "lied" saying there was no funding — DM 191/2022 opened €61.6M for Sicily; Collesano could have had a ~€1.4M plant at zero local cost, and now pays ~€250k for upgrades itself.
- **Casa di Comunità:** the most serious loss — only two were lost in all Sicily, Collesano one of them. €500k was allocated; if insufficient, the mayor should have fought at the Region for more instead of letting the funds be redistributed.
- **PRG:** ~10 years of work and ~€100k lost by missing the 31-12-2025 deadline despite his public warning of 11 Sept 2025; the town must now restart with the PUG (≥€150k more).
- **Attribution:** many works the administration claims (lighting, roads, sports field, Opera Pia ~€2M) were prepared under his mandate; a €3.5M hydrogeological project was neglected until he raised the alarm.
- **Betrayal:** he campaigned for this team expecting continuity, was then shut out and had to warn them "via videos, posts and the minority councillors."
- **Priorities:** too many events and ceremonies (Targa Florio, Carnival) while structural projects died; people are "afraid to speak or even like posts."
