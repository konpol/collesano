## Summary of the speech

Cascio's framing: she answers "with documents", accepts one responsibility explicitly, and rejects the rest of the narrative:

- **Depuratore:** the plant is old (1975) but legal and functioning; the 2021 design was **not build-ready** (missing authorizations). The services conference was concluded positively in March 2024, the project updated and submitted for funding. **"There was no lost financing decree"** — only a call for project proposals.
- **Designer dispute:** hiring a lawyer was normal caution; settled for ~€19k (vs ~€28k asked) and the updated project was obtained.
- **PRG:** she **accepts political responsibility** for the missed 31-12-2025 deadline — but rejects "did nothing": the update began in 2012 *under Meli*, and her administration immediately launched the PUG (resolution, new urban-planning office, notices, regional funding request).
- **Casa di Comunità:** no direct municipal funding was ever assigned — implementation belonged to the ASP; the ~€342k could not adapt the building (structural/seismic issues) and cancellation was **technically motivated, not negligence**. She secured an alternative: a **35-year free-use agreement with ASP Palermo** with essential services confirmed by Dr. Casiglia.
- **Attribution:** she distinguishes *funded* from *merely ranked* projects, lists her administration's works (nursery ~€560k PNRR, roads, water network, ceramic museum ~€760k SNAI…), and **apologizes** for one campaign claim ("funded" vs "admitted to ranking"). "He was not mayor alone, just as she is not mayor alone."
- **Democracy:** she objects to being called "less democratic than Putin"; anyone truly pressured should file a formal complaint. Cultural events (Targa Florio, Carnival) are legitimate governance, not a distraction.
