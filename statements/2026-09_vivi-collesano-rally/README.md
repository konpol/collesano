## Summary — speaker by speaker

**Michele Testaiuti [?] — the three failures and the council climate.** The rally exists because the mayor declined (on the last of 15 days given) a formal request for a public face-to-face debate. He rejects the "Meli directs the opposition" narrative as deliberate disinformation. The verdict: on the three big themes the administration failed with no mitigating circumstances — **PRG:** everything was ready, only a digital upload to the Region was missing by 31-12-2025; records access revealed a **two-year communication void** (July 2023 → Nov 2025) with the Region, whose own conduct was "impeccable" (last solicitation 22 December). **Casa di Comunità:** €342,123 allocated; the mayor "raised the white flag" instead of fighting for a re-modulation like Palermo, Termini Imerese and Godrano — of 38 municipalities only Collesano and Santa Flavia were "cancelled" (the decree's word). **Depuratore:** ~€1.75M lost over minor technical issues. Plus the San Basilio leak: 18 months ignored ("it's Cerda's line" — decommissioned years earlier), fixed 10 days after their Facebook video. In council: every amendment voted down 8-to-4, speaking time cut, allies (Meli, Peppe Peri's group) cynically cut out after victory. "Nothing personal — light-years apart politically."

**Rosario Sapienza — three years of opposition work.** "Not against someone, but for something": dozens of records-access requests and formal questions (oversight), dozens of amendments almost all rejected (proposals), and constant presence. Concrete results claimed: pressure that helped resolve the family-doctor vacancy, the cemetery regulation, a win on the revenue regulation. Full activity log 2023–2026: schools question (Sept 2023), SP9 road clarifications (unanimously approved), school-transport amendment €17,658.46 (rejected), castle reopening questions, kennel motion (~€27k/yr for 14 dogs — rejected), Madonna della Neve trail, market area, waste centre, urban-décor motion (approved unanimously, then ignored), blocking tax-collection outsourcing while keeping the €20k grant. The nursery is welcome but risks being a "cathedral in the desert" without any policy against demographic decline.

**Group leader — the PRG dossier in forensic detail.** Politics by gossip ("an external group leader directs them") is "the shortcut of those who have no arguments." Why the PRG matters: construction economy, artisan areas unlocking innovation funding (Caltavuturo got ~€800k), historic-centre planning. The record: ~€75k of assignments wasted; the administration stopped answering the Region in Nov 2023, resumed only after the opposition's records-access visit, sent knowingly deficient documentation "to shut the opposition's mouth" — including **two missing digital signatures** — and completed it only on 23 December, days before the deadline. "The cherry on the cake": in June 2025 the Region had warned the depuratore upgrade depended on PRG homework. Everything is minuted in council: "I could not lie even if I wanted to."

**Maria Candida Cascio — closing indictment and valedictory.** Cefalù's mayor used its updated plan as leverage against the regional landscape plan — Collesano lost that weapon. On the Casa di Comunità: "no file even existed at the town hall"; the mayor explained herself only after a year of pressure, offering "smoke in the eyes." The America trip was decided in the majority's WhatsApp group — "an institutional trip is not a private party"; opposition voters "are not second-class citizens." Schools: the technical office (2023) and the school principal (June 2026) certified the same deficiencies in writing — three years, nothing changed; the Aug 19 works decision right before school opening is "navigating by sight" to avoid Facebook comments, "their kryptonite." The simulator container: they like the museum and the simulator, not the placement — and the unanimously approved urban-décor regulation was deliberately delayed to protect it. Closing: "We tried to be an alternative… We have another idea of politics, perhaps slower but more precise. Thank you for listening."
