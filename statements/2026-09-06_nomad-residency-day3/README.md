## Summary — speaker by speaker

**Alberto Mattei (President, Associazione Italiana Nomadi Digitali) — keynote `04:45–37:37`.** Italy has no integrated offer for "temporary community citizens": 93% of would-be nomads prefer small towns (his association's reports — the only scientific research on the topic in Italy), yet there is no national portal, the 2024 digital-nomad visa can take 2 years of procedures for 1 year of validity, and ~4M remote workers sit in a legal void (Law 81/2017 covers only "smart working"). His proposals: visa reform, a remote-work law, a new "temporary community citizen" legal status, and a national steering committee — otherwise local experiments remain "islands in the ocean." In Q&A: multi-month stays need co-living with flexible contracts, not B&Bs.

**"Il diritto a restare" — Campobello di Licata `46:37–1:07:21`.** The story of **Peppe Cati**, who chose to *stay* at 20 as a political act, became a shepherd of Girgentana goats, and died at 22 in a workplace accident — seeding the Centro Studi Giuseppe Cati, a 1,393-student survey (*Terra di Futuro*) and the *Patto per Restare* network (60 civic organizations). Three warnings for the nomad debate: people choose **communities, not territories**; beware the "Airbnb mentality" of consuming places — someone must remain and take responsibility; and you cannot welcome affluent remote workers while rejecting migrants. Quotes Danilo Dolci: *"Each one grows only if dreamed."*

**Gonçalo Hall — the Ponta do Sol results `1:20:00–1:23:41`.** Madeira's nomad village: returning emigrants opening businesses, 80% growth in registered tech businesses, weekly events deliberately half locals / half nomads. When Portuguese TV came hunting for anti-nomad stories, the locals defended the project. Later on Castelbuono: the €20–30/year coworking and its young managers are the town's "most incredible asset" — "nothing but the locals themselves stands between Castelbuono and becoming the digital nomad village that lifts all of Sicily."

**Panel: rights, South Working, Sicily as laboratory `1:23:43–1:56:19`.** Elena Militello (South Working): the 2020–21 remote-work window was squandered by control culture and return-to-office; the moment to reclaim it is now. **Dina Ravera (Destination Italia):** 100k nomads staying 6 months ≈ 2.5M classic tourists; **Sicily should be Italy's pilot laboratory with the Madonie as lead destination** — one central governance, connectivity, coworking, youth retention.

**SO.SVI.MA. closing analysis `1:56:23–2:04:49`.** Digital nomads are one driver inside broader inner-areas policy: a project with Fondazione CON IL SUD for a **territorial community cooperative** (property recovery for nomads, migrants, over-65s); the Madonie has led Sicily's National Strategy for Inner Areas since 2018 ("Madonie Resilienti"). Hardest infrastructure: **social capital**.

**Media talk (Mediaset journalist) `2:06:18–2:26:27`.** Unknown towns become famous only through tragedy (Garlasco, Avetrana, Lampedusa) or get reduced to postcards (Birra Messina ad); against both, Calvino's *Invisible Cities* — and the new territorial storytelling of podcasts and pages like *Storie di Brand* and *Ti ci devo portare*.

**Results & Manifesto `2:26:27–2:40:17`.** First edition: **211 registered, 155 participants (>70% conversion), 330 attendances**, 121 Sicilians (60 from the Madonie), 25 international from 12 countries; 18 sessions, 15 speakers; 4–5 startups expected from contacts. Self-criticism: too few women on innovation panels. **The manifesto:** continuity 365 days/year; prepare the territory; a connected ecosystem (companies, universities, banks); concrete projects; **one coworking space in each of the 21 Madonie municipalities**, starting with residency pilots, community hubs and community enterprises. Roadmap: activate now → experiment through 2027 → report at the third edition in **2028**.

**Closing ceremony `2:40:17–3:08:16`.** Ficile (SO.SVI.MA.): three-year territorial-pact horizon; the **Italian Episcopal Conference** backs the effort with a commission of three bishops. Mayor Cicero: regional/national/EU politics must now pick up the challenge. **Mayor Tiziana Cascio (Collesano, lead municipality): "the intuitions were confirmed by the facts — a project of the whole Madonie territory,"** pledging political support so "the territory grows and people return." Dina Ravera pledges to mobilize MEPs and business contacts (Palermo airport routes). Toni's tributes close with Gioacchino Di Garbo and *"Thank you Castelbuono — see you soon, we hope."*

> **Why this matters for the Observatory:** Day 3 contains the administration-side commitments in their most explicit form (manifesto, 21-coworking pledge, 2028 accountability date) and Mayor Cascio's public claim of vindication — the natural counterpart to the opposition's [end-of-term audit](../2026-09_vivi-collesano-rally/) delivered twelve days later.
