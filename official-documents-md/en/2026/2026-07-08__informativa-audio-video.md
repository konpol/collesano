# Information notice on the processing of personal data (images and audio/video) collected during participation in the Collesano school camp

(EU Reg. 2016/679 - "General Data Protection Regulation")

**Autonomous Data Controllers**

The Autonomous Controllers of the processing of personal data are:

- ☐ the Civil Protection Department, in the person of its legal representative pro tempore, located in Rome, via Ulpiano, 11;
- ☐ the Municipality of Collesano and the municipal group of civil protection volunteers, via vittorio emanuele 2, Collesano

The Controllers, in relation to the processing activities carried out autonomously by each, undertake to process the personal data provided according to the principles laid down by the GDPR (lawfulness, fairness and transparency of processing, necessity, relevance and minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, confidentiality and accountability).

**1) Type of data, legal basis and purposes of their processing**

The processing will concern the data of the minor collected during participation in the Collesano school camp, including the publication and use of the images and audio/video recordings made by the Controllers.

The type of personal data of the minor requested and acquired is the following:

- Identifiers: name;
- Image: photos and videos.

For the sole purpose of validating the consent to the processing of the minor's data, the name and the codice fiscale [personal tax code] of the parents/guardians will be processed.

The Controllers do not carry out profiling activities, either in automatic form or in any other form, on the data collected.

The legal basis of the processing activity is the consent expressed by the parents/guardians of the minor concerned.

**2) Methods of data processing**

The processing will be carried out mainly with the aid of electronic means and may consist of any operation or set of operations among those indicated in art. 1 paragraph 2 letter b) of the European provision referred to above [reference as printed in the original]. The data will be processed by authorised and trained technical staff.

**3) Data retention period**

Personal data will be kept until the expiry of the limitation period of any rights connected with the publication of the images and, therefore, for no more than 10 years, except for archival needs or needs of documentation of the event.

**4) Provision of data or possible refusal**

The provision of personal data is necessary for the performance of the activity and purposes described above. Failure to provide the data will not allow any use and/or publication of the images.

**5) Communication of data**

Personal data will not be communicated to further parties.

**6) Dissemination of data**

The data collected will be disseminated online on the institutional platforms of the Comune di Collesano and on the institutional website of the Civil Protection Department.

**7) Rights of the data subject**

At any moment, the data subject may exercise, vis-à-vis the Controller, the rights provided for by arts. 15-22 of the Regulation, that is, by way of example, he/she may request:

- the revocation of consent (which will not affect processing activities that are impossible to interrupt or already carried out);
- access to his/her personal data and to the information relating to them;
- rectification in the event of their inaccuracy or the integration of incomplete data;
- the erasure of the data upon the occurrence of the conditions provided for by art. 17, paragraph 1, of the Regulation and in compliance with the exceptions provided for in paragraph 3;
- the restriction of their processing in the cases indicated in art. 18, paragraph 1 of the Regulation.

The aforesaid rights may be exercised by written communication to be sent to the addresses of the Data Controllers, as reported above, or by sending an email

- for the Civil Protection Department, to the address dipartimento.protezione.civile@certmail.regione.sicilia.it
- for the Comune di Collesano, to the address protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it

In the communication, please indicate the site of publication of the recordings or images, the image or video referred to and, in the case of video recordings, the reference timestamp, in as much detail as possible.

The data subject will have the right to lodge a complaint with the Autorità Garante per la Protezione dei dati personali [Italian data protection authority] according to the procedures specifically indicated on the institution's website: www.garanteprivacy.it.
