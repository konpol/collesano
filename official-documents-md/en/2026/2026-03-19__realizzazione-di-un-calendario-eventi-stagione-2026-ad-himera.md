**SUBJECT: Creation of an Events Calendar for the 2026 season at Himera, a site belonging to the Archaeological Park of Himera, Solunto and Iato. Active involvement of the Associations operating in the reference territory.**

To the Associations

- As is known, the Archaeological Park of Himera, Solunto and Iato is engaged, as every year, in the creation of a calendar of cultural events covering various fields of knowledge, with events ranging from open-air theatrical performances (Teatri di Pietra) to book presentations and conferences on various themes, which see the sites of Himera as protagonists in animating the territory, especially ("sopratutto" [text as printed]) in the summer period;

- These initiatives have found broad approval among the local populations and the active collaboration of both associations and local Authorities (Municipalities, G.A.L., Parks);

- Since this year too the intention is to continue in the furrow of what has already been profitably achieved in past years, therefore, with this letter a courteous invitation is extended to take part in the planning meeting with the Associations of the territory which will be held at the Himera site, Pirro Marconi Museum, on **Saturday 28 March 2026 at 17:30**;

- The Associations addressed, for a better organisation of the meeting, may send a note of Participation to the Park's offices, no later than **19/03/2026**, to the E-mail address parco.archeo.himera@regione.sicilia.it;

- The contact person is Dott. Roberto Tedesco, Consultant of the Park of Himera, Solunto and Iato, Mobile 3387326589;

- The procedure manager is Mr Provino Domenico, employee of this Park, reachable at the following E-mail address parco.archeo.himera@regione.sicilia.it and Tel. Mobile 3927597140.

Thanking you in advance for your welcome participation, best regards.

Cordially.
