## AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

### <u>Formation of the ranking list for access to the home-assistance service</u>

### Project "ATS-Servizi e Infrastrutture Sociali di Comunità – Azioni e Servizi per l'Inclusione" [ATS — Community Social Services and Infrastructure — Actions and Services for Inclusion]

Notice is hereby given that the time limits are open for the submission of applications for inclusion in the ranking list of beneficiaries of the home-assistance service provided for within the project **"Servizi e Infrastrutture Sociali di Comunità – Azioni e Servizi per l'Inclusione"**.

Applications may be submitted by citizens resident in Collesano who meet the required conditions, with particular reference to:

- persons aged 65 or over;
- persons with disabilities;
- persons at risk of social exclusion;
- persons without an adequate supporting family network.

**Only one application per family unit** is admitted.

Inclusion in the ranking list **does not entail the immediate activation of the service**. Beneficiaries will be admitted progressively, in the order of the ranking list and within the limits of the places available within the project.

Applications must be submitted no later than **Friday 14 August 2026**, using the appropriate form available at the municipal offices and on the institutional website of the municipality, by hand at the ufficio protocollo [filing office] or by certified email to the address protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it

**The ranking list will remain valid for the entire duration of the project and will be used for the inclusion of those entitled as new places become available**. For further information, you may contact the social services office during public opening hours.
