with offices in the Municipality of Petralia Soprana (PA)

# PUBLIC NOTICE

## Request for activation of a Service Pact for persons with disabilities aged 18 or under

The Union of the "Madonie" Municipalities, in its capacity as implementing entity of the National Strategy for Inner Areas (SNAI) of the Madonie prototype area, and specifically of the project intervention called **"Network of home social-health services for persons with disabilities"**, which will make it possible to implement the service-voucher system to facilitate the access of households with differently-abled members to the network of day-cycle social-health services, in order to foster an increase in the quality of services both in terms of services provided and of extension of time slots and integration of the chain, in close coordination with the three Social-Health Districts (DSS 33, DSS 35 and DSS 37) operating in the territory, has decided to adopt, in the perspective of subsidiarity, transparency, participation and support of private commitment in the social function, **the accreditation system**, through which the participation and active involvement of third-sector organisations has been promoted in the prevention, elimination or reduction of situations of need and difficulty of the person and in the achievement of the social objectives of improving the quality of life.

In this sense, persons with disabilities aged 18 or under and resident in the 21 municipalities of the Area Interna Madonie (Alimena, Bompietro, Castellana Sicula, Gangi, Petralia Soprana, Petralia Sottana, Blufi, Geraci Siculo, Polizzi Generosa, Castelbuono, Collesano, Gratteri, Isnello, Pollina, San Mauro Castelverde, Aliminusa, Caccamo, Caltavuturo, Montemaggiore Belsito, Sclafani Bagni, Scillato) may submit an application at the Protocol Office of their municipality of residence for the activation of the **Service Pact (as provided by art. 3 paragraph 4 lett. b) of D.P. n. 589/2018), which identifies forms of assistance through the provision of territorial services.**

**The form for submitting the application, which must take place no later than 27 March 2026,** can be collected at the social-services offices of the Municipality of residence of the person with disabilities or downloaded from the institutional website of SO.SVI.MA. Spa: <u>www.sosvima.com</u>.

<u>Applications must be accompanied by:</u>

1) a photocopy of the identity document and tax code of the beneficiary and/or applicant;
2) a Social-Health ISEE certificate (of the head of family) issued by the authorised offices and bodies in the current year for the previous year;
3) the commission's report attesting the disability and/or equivalent documentation;
4) a substitute declaration attesting the presence of the following information:
   a) attendance allowance;
   b) presence of other persons with disabilities in the cohabiting household;
   c) use of assistance vouchers;
4) *(numbering repeated in the original text)* any measure of appointment as support administrator or guardian in the case of a delegate.

Following the submission of the request, the conditions of dependency and the degree of non-self-sufficiency of the person with disabilities will be ascertained by the Multidimensional Evaluation Unit, and the person will be taken into care and a personalised plan drawn up showing any other forms of direct and/or indirect assistance.

From this derives, in order to avoid duplication of interventions or overlaps, the assignment of a project budget clearly showing the type of intervention planned and the resources prepared, as well as the resources of which the person with disabilities is a beneficiary by reason of his/her condition.

Applications received after the set deadline or not accompanied by the required documentation will be excluded.

The social cooperatives which, following a similar notice published in 2022 and therefore being updated and/or confirmed, have successfully passed the accreditation phase are the following:
