# COMUNE DI COLLESANO

***Città Metropolitana di Palermo***

## AVVISO [public notice]

### MEETING ON THE PROVINCIAL LANDSCAPE PLAN

Following the adoption of the **Provincial Landscape Plan**, the municipal administration invites stakeholders to take part in a discussion meeting aimed at examining the contents of the Plan in depth, assessing its effects on the municipal territory and gathering any shared observations and proposals.

The invitation is addressed, in particular, to **architects, engineers, surveyors, agronomists, technical experts, professionals of the sector**, as well as to all interested parties.

📅 **Wednesday 5 August 2026** 🕔 **17:00** 📍 **Council Chamber of the Town Hall**

The meeting will take place with the participation of the **municipal technical staff**, in order to encourage discussion and provide the necessary clarifications.

Participation is important in order to contribute, through constructive discussion, to the protection and development of our territory.

**THE HEAD OF THE UTC [municipal technical office]** ***Ing. Liborio Biundo*** — **THE MAYOR** ***Dott.ssa Tiziana Cascio***
