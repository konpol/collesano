# ADECCO NOTICE

# JOB OFFER — Category B drivers – Municipality of Collesano

#### PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

#### COLLESANO, Palermo

We are looking for 2 drivers with a category B driving licence for a client company operating in the public sector.

The initial contract will be for 3 months with the possibility of subsequent extensions.

The position is open in the province of Palermo, more precisely in the municipality of Collesano.

Preferential requirements:

- Possession of a category B driving licence for at least 48 months;
- Previous experience of at least two years driving minibuses or vans;
- Upper-secondary school diploma;

Responsibilities of the candidate:

- Carrying out accompaniment activities to local public and private facilities for care and recreational activities.

The ideal candidate should possess the following soft skills: ability to work in a team, excellent communication skills, motivation, optimism and energy, flexibility, task orientation and planning ability.

Place of work: Municipalities of Collesano ["Comuni" plural as printed], province of Palermo.

Working hours: part-time, 18 hours per week.

If you are a motivated person with solid experience in the sector and the required skills, apply for the offer.

Time availability: Part Time morning, Part Time afternoon

CCNL [national collective labour agreement]: Local Authorities — Contract level: B1 Level B1
