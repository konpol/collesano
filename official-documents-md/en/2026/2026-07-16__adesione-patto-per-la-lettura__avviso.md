## AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

## JOINING THE "READING PACT" OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

In execution of the Deliberazione [resolution of the council or town board] of the Giunta Comunale [town board] n. 97 of 13.07.2026

### NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN

that it is possible to submit one's application for membership for the establishment of the "Reading Pact" of the Municipality of Collesano, an instrument of territorial collaboration aimed at the promotion of books and reading.

Applications may be submitted by interested public and private entities as specified in more detail in the Pact attached to this notice, by transmitting the duly completed form to the PEC address protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it or delivering it by hand to the Ufficio Protocollo [filing office] of the Municipality of Collesano, stating in the subject line the wording "Modulo di adesione – Patto per la lettura del Comune di Collesano" by **12:00 on 31 July 2026.**
