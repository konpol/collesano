## Public notice concerning criteria and methods of payment of the contribution of € 3.000,00, aimed at access to rented housing, under art. 57 of Regional Law n. 1/2026

In application of art. 57, paragraph 1 of regional law 05 January 2026, n. 1, in order to facilitate access to rented housing on the private market through the reduction of rent, the Regional Department for Family and Social Policies, within the limits of the available allocation of € 5.000.000,00, provides for the award of an annual contribution of € 3.000,00 to households composed of at least three people, with an ISEE [equivalent economic situation indicator] not exceeding € 10.000,00 based on income for the year 2024. In addition, paragraph 3 of the same article of law provides for an annual increase of € 200,00 for each further child of the household record holder beyond the first.

**1. RECIPIENTS OF THE CONTRIBUTION**

The intervention is addressed to Italian citizens, or citizens of a state belonging to the European Union, or holders of an EC residence permit for long-term residents or of a valid residence permit, pursuant to the legislation in force, provided they do not hold ownership or usufruct rights. **An application for the grant of the contribution may be submitted by the holder of the household register record** (record holder) **of a registered household composed of at least three members,** in possession of the following requirements:

**-citizenship** Italian or EU or, in the case of a non-EU person, possession of an EC residence permit for long-term residents or of a valid residence permit, pursuant to the legislation in force, provided they do not hold ownership or usufruct rights;

**-residence** in the territory of the Sicilian Region for at least two years at the time of submission of the application;

**-I.S.E.E. indicator** of the household not exceeding € 10.000,00, based on income for the year 2024. All members of the household contribute to the determination of this indicator pursuant to the provisions in force on the matter.

**2. APPLICATION**

The application must be submitted to the Social Services Office of the Municipality of residence and must be drawn up on the specific template prepared by this Regional Department (Annex *B*), in the form of a substitute declaration of affidavit, pursuant to art. 46 et seq. of D.P.R. 28 December 2000 n. 445. The following documentation must be attached to the application:

- a photocopy of the applicant's valid identity document, pursuant to art. 38 of D.P.R. 445/2000;
- in the case of a non-EU person, a copy of the EC residence permit for long-term residents or of the valid residence permit;
- an I.S.E.E. indicator certificate issued by the authorised Offices based on income for the year 2024, currently valid;
- a regular residential lease contract, relating to a property used as the main dwelling corresponding to the applicant's registered residence, duly registered and in order with the annual payment of registration tax, unless the flat-rate "cedolare secca" option applies, pursuant to art. 3 of D.Lgs. 14 March 2011, n. 23, as amended and supplemented.

**Households with lease contracts for public residential housing, or in cadastral categories A1, A8 and A9, are excluded from the contribution.** **The contract may also be in the name of another member of the household,** <u>provided the property corresponds to the applicant's registered residence and main dwelling.</u>

The submission of the aforesaid documentation is obligatory for admission to the benefit. The applications with their documentation will be retained and kept at the competent municipal Office.

**3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE MUNICIPALITIES**

Each Municipality is responsible for verifying the documentation submitted and the truthfulness of the declarations contained in the application; for this purpose it may carry out the sample checks provided for by the legislation in force. The Municipalities are required:

- to verify that the applicant citizen possesses the registry requirements required by art. 57 of regional law 05 January 2026, n. 1, namely residence in the regional territory for at least two years, as well as a household composed of at least three members including the applicant;
- to provide citizens with adequate information on the intervention, using both formal publicity means and other suitable tools;
- to indicate a deadline for the submission of applications that is compatible with the deadline, indicated below, for transmission to this Department.

The Municipalities, having carried out the due verifications and checks on the applications received, must send to this Regional Department for Family and Social Policies:

- a specific request signed by the Manager of the competent sector, accompanied by the list of applicant citizens;
- certification of residence and family status of each of the applicant citizens.

The aforesaid request must arrive no later than ……… *(deadline not filled in in the text)* BY PEC [certified email] to the address <u>dipartimento.famiglia@certmail.regione.sicilia.it</u> and to the address: <u>raffaella.patti@regione.sicilia.it</u> <u>Applications addressed to other email boxes will not be accepted.</u>

**4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE REGION**

The contribution of € 3.000,00, referred to in the first paragraph of the provision in question, will be paid to households composed of at least three people, regardless of the presence of one or more children. The increase of 200,00, provided for by the third paragraph of the provision in question, will be recognised for each child of the record holder after the first.

**Examples:**

1. household composed of the record holder plus two siblings: is a beneficiary of the contribution of € 3.000,00, always within the limits of the ISEE value provided for by the provision and of the relative position in the ranking list;
2. household composed of the record holder plus two siblings and 1 child of the record holder: is a beneficiary of the contribution of € 3.000,00, without increase, always within the limits of the ISEE value provided for by the provision;
3. household composed of the record holder plus two siblings, 1 child and one grandchild of the record holder: is a beneficiary of the contribution of € 3.000,00, without increase, always within the limits of the ISEE value provided for by the provision;
4. household composed of the record holder plus two siblings and 2 children of the record holder: is a beneficiary of the contribution, always within the limits of the ISEE value provided for by the provision, with the increase of € 200,00.

By Decree of the Director General of this Department, two ranking lists will be approved:

- Annex A: regional ranking list of applicants according to the household I.S.E.E. indicator in ascending order;
- Annex B: regional ranking list of beneficiaries according to the household I.S.E.E. indicator in ascending order, within the limits of the available budget allocation.

The distribution and assignment of the sums to the requesting Municipalities will proceed according to the order of the ranking list and within the limits of the available regional Budget allocation. The contribution will be paid to the beneficiaries directly by the assignee Municipalities. The benefit does not constitute income for tax and social-security purposes and may be combined with similar provisions or allowances.
