# Facilitated settlement (definizione agevolata) of municipal revenues

The Municipality of Collesano informs the citizens that, in implementation of the **Regulations for the facilitated settlement of revenues**, approved with **Resolution of the Municipal Council n. 12 of 22 April 2026**, it is possible to submit an application to access the facilitated settlement of municipal taxes.

**Deadline for the submission of applications: 31 July 2026**.

The facilitated settlement allows taxpayers to regularise their debt position with the exclusion of penalties and interest.

The procedure is structured in the following phases:

1. Submission of the application to join the facilitated settlement by 31 July 2026, with a choice between payment in a single instalment or in instalments;
2. Verification of the application by the Municipality;
3. Communication to the taxpayer of the amount of the sums due, with the amount and deadlines of any instalments;
4. Closure of the position in the event of regular payment, or restoration of the original situation in the event of non-fulfilment.

## It is specified that, based on the subject and content of the regulations, the taxes eligible for the facilitation are:

- **IMU and TARI assessment notices for tax year 2020;**
- **Collection notices entrusted to the Agenzia delle Entrate-Riscossione [revenue collection agency], for IMU-TASI and TARI, for tax years up to 2015.**
