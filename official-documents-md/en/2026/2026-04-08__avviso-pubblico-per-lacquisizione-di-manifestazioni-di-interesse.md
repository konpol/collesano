# METROPOLITAN CITY OF PALERMO — ASSETS DIRECTORATE

# PUBLIC NOTICE FOR THE ACQUISITION OF EXPRESSIONS OF INTEREST FOR THE PURCHASE OF REAL-ESTATE ASSETS OWNED BY THE METROPOLITAN CITY OF PALERMO, INCLUDED IN THE PLAN OF DISPOSALS AND REAL-ESTATE ENHANCEMENTS FOR THE YEAR 2026/2028.

Notice is given that the Metropolitan City of Palermo (hereinafter also referred to as the "Administration") issues this public notice for the acquisition of expressions of interest from parties intending to purchase the following state-property buildings:

* FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE (casa cantoniera) located in the municipality of BLUFI (PA) (in the cadastre at map sheet 63, parcel 363, sub. 1-2-3-4);
* FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE located in the municipality of SAN MAURO CASTELVERDE (PA) (in the cadastre at map sheet 3, parcel 480, sub. 1-2-3);
* FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE located in the municipality of COLLESANO (PA) (in the cadastre at map sheet 21, parcel 1088, sub. 1-2-3-4).

## WHEREAS:

- in execution of the Authority's Plan of Disposals and Real-Estate Enhancement for the three-year period 2026/2028, together with the D.U.P. [single programming document], approved by Resolution of the Metropolitan Council n° 08 of 02/02/2026 and by Managerial Determination n° 952 of 24/02/2026 approving the notice in question, in conformity with the principles of transparency, equal treatment and non-discrimination, the Metropolitan City of Palermo intends to acquire expressions of interest from parties interested in purchasing the real-estate assets included in the list just above;

- the properties in the above List, Former Road-keeper's Houses — State Property, have been declassified under art. 829 paragraph II of the Civil Code to the Authority's Available Assets;

- this public notice is preliminary to the possible negotiated procedure, according to the criterion of the best price upwards of the amount established as the auction base, which will be carried out with those who submit an expression of interest in purchasing the assets in question, in the ways and times established below.

## GIVES NOTICE

- to all parties interested in submitting proposals of expression of interest in the purchase of properties owned by the Metropolitan City of Palermo included in the Plan of Disposal and Real-Estate Enhancement for the year 2026/2028, and specifically with reference to those indicated in art. 1 of this notice.

## Art. 1 — LIST AND DESCRIPTION OF THE REAL-ESTATE ASSETS

The list and details of the properties covered by this notice are given below, with indication of the cadastral data and of the relative estimated value, which constitutes the base price for the disposal:

**LOT 1)** FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE located in the Municipality of BLUFI (PA) (in the cadastre at Sheet 63, Parcel 363, sub. 1-2-3-4) — ESTIMATED VALUE: € 22.000,00 (twenty-two thousand/00);

**LOT 2)** FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE located in the Municipality of SAN MAURO CASTELVERDE (PA) (in the cadastre at Sheet 3, Parcel 480, sub. 1-2-3) - ESTIMATED VALUE: € 24.000,00 (twenty-four thousand/00);

**LOT 3)** FORMER ROAD-KEEPER'S HOUSE located in the Municipality of COLLESANO (PA) (in the cadastre at Sheet 21, Parcel 1088, sub. 1-2-3-4) - ESTIMATED VALUE: € 10.000,00 (ten thousand/00).

## Art. 2 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

- This Notice does not constitute a contractual proposal and does not bind the Metropolitan City of Palermo in any way, which, for its part, is free to start other procedures or to interrupt at any time, for reasons of public interest, the procedure started, without the requesting parties being able to claim anything, including the payment of brokerage commissions and/or any consultancy charges;
- this notice constitutes only an invitation to express interest and does not represent an invitation to make an offer to the public pursuant to art. 1336 of the Civil Code; *(wording as printed)*
- the submission of the expression of interest does not give rise to any title, right or legally relevant interest to demand the continuation of the procedure;
- all parties who have submitted a valid expression of interest through this notice will be invited to take part in the subsequent auction procedure, aimed at identifying the best offer;
- conditional offers or offers expressed in an indeterminate manner will not be admitted;
- the assets put up for sale are included in the list of properties to be disposed of under the Authority's Plan of DISPOSALS and REAL-ESTATE ENHANCEMENT for the three-year period 2026-2028, which, following its publication, produces a declaratory effect of ownership as well as the effects provided for by art. 2644 of the civil code (effects of the transcription of acts relating to real property) and of the registration of the asset in the cadastre;
- the assets covered by this procedure will be sold as a whole (a corpo) and not by measure, in the lots indicated, to be awarded separately, in the state of fact and law in which they are found; including any active and passive charges, active and passive easements, continuous and discontinuous, apparent and non-apparent, appurtenances and accessions, uses, actions and claims, in any case free of mortgages and attachments and without any obligation on the part of the Metropolitan City of Palermo for further works of completion, improvement, maintenance, clearance, disposal of waste and materials of any kind present on site for any reason;
- if only one expression of interest is received for one or more lots, the Metropolitan City of Palermo reserves the right to proceed by direct negotiation for the sale of the individual lots;
- the costs of any subdivisions and all the costs inherent and consequent to the signing of the deed of sale (including cadastral searches) will be entirely borne by the purchaser;
- the expression of interest may be submitted for one or more lots. In the case of participation in several lots, an expression of interest must be submitted for each individual lot;
- the properties will be sold in the state of fact, law and urban planning in which they are found. The sale is not subject to the rules concerning the warranty for defects or lack of quality, nor may it be revoked for any reason. Consequently, the existence of any defects, lack of quality or non-conformity of the thing sold, charges of any kind — including, by way of example only, urban-planning charges or those deriving from the possible need to bring installations into line with the laws in force — for any reason not considered, even if hidden and in any case not highlighted in this notice and/or in the documents of the procedure - may not give rise to any compensation, indemnity or reduction of the price, this having been taken into account in the valuation of the assets and in the determination of the price;
- the Administration, at its unquestionable discretion, may suspend, extend the deadline, not follow up or revoke this procedure.

To view the documentation relating to the assets and for further information, you may contact Ing. ## Art. 4 — SUBJECTIVE REQUIREMENTS OF PARTICIPANTS

To submit the expression of interest, and to be admitted to any subsequent phases (including the auction in the event of several expressions of interest for the same property), interested parties, together with the application (Declaration of expression of interest "Annex A"), must submit a duly signed declaration (where applicable by the legal representative in the case of a company), accompanied by a photocopy of a valid identity document of the signatory, attesting:

a) to possess the requirements to contract with the public administration and to be in order with the rules in force on anti-mafia matters;
b) not to have pending disputes with the Metropolitan City of Palermo and to be in order with the payment of the tariffs and fees due to the Authority on any basis;
c) to have read all the conditions governing this procedure, and to expressly accept them;
d) to have taken note of and to accept the general characteristics (having consulted the cadastral documents) of the properties for which the expression of interest in purchase is submitted;
e) to be aware that the sale takes place as a whole and not by measure;
f) to authorise the Administration to process personal data, pursuant to European regulation 2016/679 (General data protection regulation, GDPR) on the protection of personal data, for the purposes connected with the regular conduct of this procedure;
g) of the criminal responsibilities assumed, pursuant to article 76 of D.P.R. 28 December 2000, n. 445, for falsity in documents and untruthful declarations.

## Art. 5 — METHOD OF SUBMISSION OF PROPOSALS OF EXPRESSION OF INTEREST

The proposal of expression of interest must be submitted, under penalty of exclusion, by PEC [certified email] to the address: patrimonio@cert.cittametropolitana.pa.it specifying in the subject the wording: "MANIFESTAZIONE DI INTERESSE ALL'ACQUISTO DI IMMOBILI DI PROPRIETÀ DELLA CITTÀ METROPOLITANA DI PALERMO INSERITI NEL PIANO DELLE ALIENAZIONI E VALORIZZAZIONI IMMOBILIARI ANNO 2026/2028" no later than 30 (thirty) days from the date of publication of the notice.

## Art. 6 — DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED

The expression of interest must be drawn up according to the facsimile template attached to this notice and must contain:

- the declaration of full acceptance of everything provided for in this notice;
- contact details at which the interested Party declares that he/she wishes to receive communications relating to this notice;
- a photocopy of the signatory's identity document and, in the case of a legal person, an updated chamber-of-commerce record and the identity document of the legal representative.

## Art. 7 — SALE PROCEDURE

Following the expression of interest, the applicants admitted to participate will be invited to submit an irrevocable purchase offer.

When depositing the irrevocable purchase offer, the offeror must at the same time attach the non-interest-bearing security deposit equal to 10% of the price offered, by non-transferable Cashier's Cheque made out to the Metropolitan City of Palermo with the wording "acquisto lotto n .... ex casa cantoniera" [purchase of lot n .... former road-keeper's house].

The declaration of commitment, under penalty of loss of the security deposit, to proceed with the signing of the deed of sale and therefore to settle the price no later than 60 days; the express declaration of knowing the factual and legal status of the asset for which the offer is submitted, that the sale is as a whole and not by measure, and expressly holding the Metropolitan City of Palermo harmless for any defects. *(so in the original, syntactically incomplete sentence)*

The award is made in favour of the competitor who submits the highest economic offer with respect to the price set as the basis of the negotiation (which is equal to the estimated value indicated above for each lot). Offers equal to or higher than those set as the basis of the negotiation are admitted, while offers below are not admitted.

In the case of a single party interested in purchasing a lot, the disposal will take place following direct private negotiation with that party.

If the highest economic offer is submitted in identical amount by two or more participants, they will be invited to proceed with upward bids. Where none of the offerors wishes to improve the offer and thus proceed with the auction, the awardee will be chosen by drawing lots.

## Art. 8 — CONTRACT - PAYMENT OF THE PRICE

The sale is completed with the signing of the deed of sale in the forms and ways provided for by the Civil Code. The sale contract is drawn up by a notary chosen by the purchaser. The property sale contract is signed within a maximum period of 60 days running from the definitive award.

Failure to sign the contract within the prescribed period entails forfeiture of the award, with the right of the Metropolitan City of Palermo to retain the security deposit and without prejudice to the Authority's right to act in the appropriate fora for compensation of any greater damage.

The award price, to be understood net of all tax, notarial and cadastral charges, must be paid in a single instalment, by cashier's cheque, at the signing of the contract. Deferred payments or instalments are not admitted. All contractual and tax expenses consequent to the signing of the contract (notarial fees, searches, registration, mortgage and cadastral taxes, etc.) are borne by the purchaser.

## Art. 9 — COMPETENT COURT

For any dispute concerning this procedure, the Court of Palermo will have exclusive jurisdiction.
