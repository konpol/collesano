# COMUNE DI COLLESANO

*Città Metropolitana di Palermo*

*****************

## AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

### FOR THE ACQUISITION OF MANIFESTAZIONE DI INTERESSE [expression of interest (a first, non-binding application)] ADDRESSED TO THE

### COMMERCIAL BUSINESSES PRESENT ON THE MUNICIPAL TERRITORY FOR

### JOINING THE "ECO-IES" PROJECT

### THE HEAD OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE AREA

This notice is of an exclusively exploratory nature and does not launch any tender procedure, nor does it provide for ranking lists or the awarding of scores. With this notice, the Municipality of Collesano intends to carry out a market survey aimed at acquiring expressions of interest from **economic operators** in order to encourage the process of recovery and recycling of PET (polyethylene terephthalate) bottles in line with European directives. For this purpose the municipality has purchased 1 eco-compactor and has arranged its installation in via Mora. This will allow citizens to deposit PET bottles that have contained food liquids, so that they are separated, compacted, collected and transported to authorised facilities for recycling. The initiative provides for the direct participation of citizens and a reward system in their favour: indeed, depositing PET waste at the "Mangiaplastica" eco-compactor will allow the virtuous citizen to accumulate "*IES value*", verifiable by means of a coupon issued by the machine, to be spent at the commercial businesses that are partners of the project.

With this instrument the intention is not only to reward the virtuous citizen, but also to encourage zero-kilometre purchases in local shops and businesses. In view of all the above,

## THE COMMERCIAL BUSINESSES ARE INVITED

— those present on the municipal territory — to express their specific interest in the formation of a list of partner entities of the "ECO-IES" project meeting the requirements specified in more detail below.

### 1. SUBJECT OF THE NOTICE

The commercial businesses that intend to join, if they meet the requirements specified in more detail below, will form a list of operators at which the virtuous citizen may spend the digital vouchers. The economic operators, in turn, will be reimbursed by means of a refund invoice issued to the platform entrusted with the service on behalf of the municipality.

### 2. ENTITIES ADMITTED TO PARTICIPATION

Registration in the list of project partner entities by commercial businesses is admitted following the positive verification of the required conditions and provided they have submitted an application in the manner defined further below and using the attached membership form.

### 3. DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED FOR REGISTRATION IN THE LIST

For the purposes of registration in the list of partner entities, the economic operators that intend to join must send a specific application (FORM "A") declaring, pursuant to the DPR 445/2000:

**a**. That they have read the notice of expression of interest in the "ECO-IES" project;

**b**. That the business is registered with the C.C.I.A.A. [chamber of commerce];

**c**. that the business is not in a state of bankruptcy, compulsory liquidation or preventive composition with creditors, and that no proceedings are under way for the declaration of one of those situations;

**d**. that there are no convictions that have become final, or criminal conviction decrees that have become irrevocable, or judgments applying a penalty upon request pursuant to art. 444 of the C.P.P. [code of criminal procedure], for serious offences against the State or the Community affecting professional morality; e. that there is no pending proceeding for the application of one of the prevention measures referred to in art. 3 of Law n° 1423/53 or one of the disqualifying grounds provided for by art. 10 of Law n° 575/65;

**e**. that they have not committed serious violations, definitively ascertained, of the obligations relating to the payment of taxes and duties, according to Italian legislation;

**f**. that they have not committed serious violations, definitively ascertained, of the rules on social security and welfare contributions, according to Italian legislation.

### 4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED FOR REGISTRATION IN THE LIST

The entities interested in being included in the list of commercial businesses willing to become partners of the project, thereby being prepared to accept the vouchers that will be issued to citizens by the eco-compactor machines called "Mangiaplastica", must submit their application, drawn up according to the attached form, signed by the legal representative, owner and/or attorney of the business, complete with a valid identity document, on pain of exclusion from the procedure, exclusively by certified email to the address **protocollo@comune.collesano.pa.it**, stating in the subject line the following text: "ADESIONE AL PROGETTO ECO-IES" **by 12.00 on Monday 03 August 2026;**

### 5. FORMATION, VALIDITY, UPDATING OF THE LIST AND WITHDRAWAL

The applications received in the manner indicated will be subjected to the relevant examination to verify their regularity and completeness, including for the purpose of requesting any additions and/or clarifications to allow the inclusion in the list of the commercial businesses that will form the aforesaid list. It remains in any case the responsibility of the participating businesses to communicate any changes and/or updates to the information relating to the business.

### 6. PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

By registering in this list, consent is given to the processing of the personal data and information provided in compliance with the provisions of European Regulation n° 2016/679, exclusively for the purposes of this procedure and of any subsequent phases.

### 7. PUBLICITY OF THE NOTICE AND SOLE PROJECT MANAGER

The person responsible for the establishment and keeping of the list referred to in this notice is Dott.ssa Antonella Porcello of the Administrative Area.
