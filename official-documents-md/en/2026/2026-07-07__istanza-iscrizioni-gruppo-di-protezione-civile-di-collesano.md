**REQUESTS**

to be admitted as a volunteer member of this Municipal Group.

To this end he/she declares:

- That he/she is not in the conditions provided for by paragraph 1 of art. 15 of L. 19/03/1990, n° 55 and subsequent amendments and additions, and is not subject to prevention measures;
- That he/she is not aware of the existence, against him/her, of proceedings in progress for the application of prevention measures;
- That he/she has no pending charges nor criminal convictions;
- That he/she knows, shares and respects the Founding Act, the Statute and the Regulations of the Gruppo Volontari Protezione Civile of Collesano
- That he/she consents to the processing of the personal data, both ordinary and sensitive, concerning him/her.

The consent is conditional on compliance with the provisions of the legislation in force (art. 10 L.675/96-privacy);
