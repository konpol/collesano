# Transport of persons with disabilities to rehabilitation centres

The Municipal Administration of Collesano has activated the transport service for minors with disabilities who attend rehabilitation centres. The intervention is aimed at supporting attendance at the rehabilitation facility, fostering inclusion and participation in social life, in conformity with what is provided:

✓ by art. 13 of D.lgs. n. 267 of 18.08.2000
✓ by art. 26 of law 104 of 05.02.1992
✓ by art. 6 paragraph 2 lett. c of Sicilian Regional Law n. 68 of 18.08.1981

## Who it is for

The transport service is addressed to minors with disabilities, resident in the Municipality of Collesano, beneficiaries of multidisciplinary therapies provided by rehabilitation centres authorised by the ASP [provincial health authority].

## Description

The free municipal transport service to and from the Rehabilitation Centres provides support to families with minors with disabilities, recognised pursuant to Law n. 104/1992 art. 3 - paragraph 3, who must travel to the Rehabilitation Centres authorised by the ASP.

## How to apply

The application form can be downloaded from the institutional website in the services section – browse by category – health, wellbeing and assistance, or can be requested at the Social Services Office.

It must be duly completed in every part, accompanied by all the documentation indicated. The application may be submitted by the applicant in the following ways:

- Pec: protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it
- by hand to the Protocol Office of Collesano

## What is needed

Documents to attach to the application:

- assessment report pursuant to Law 104/92 attesting the type and degree of the handicap, issued by the competent health commission of the ASP, currently valid;
- Therapeutic plan containing the number of sessions and the relative type of treatment to be carried out, issued by the ASP, currently valid;
- Photocopy of the applicant's valid identity document.

## What you get

Free transport of minors with disabilities who must access the Rehabilitation Centres authorised by the ASP.

## Timing and deadlines

Admission to the service takes place on the existence of the required requirements, subject to verification by the Professional Social Service. A waiting list may be provided. The validity of the request is annual. The application may be renewed annually if the access requirements persist.
