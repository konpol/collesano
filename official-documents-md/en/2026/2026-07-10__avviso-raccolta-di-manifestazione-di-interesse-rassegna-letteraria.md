## AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

AIMED AT THE COLLECTION OF MANIFESTAZIONI DI INTERESSE [expressions of interest (a first, non-binding application)] FOR PARTICIPATION IN THE LITERARY SERIES "UN BORGO DI LIBRI – FESTIVAL DIFFUSO DEL LIBRO DI COLLESANO", FOR THE PROMOTION OF READING

Literary series "Un Borgo di Libri – Festival Diffuso del Libro di Collesano " [A Village of Books — Widespread Book Festival of Collesano]

Reading paths, authors and community

Expression of interest for participation in the municipal literary series "Un Borgo di Libri – Festival Diffuso del Libro di Collesano "

**1. Purposes**

The Municipality of Collesano promotes the cultural series "Un Borgo di Libri – Festival Diffuso del Libro di Collesano ", aimed at enhancing reading, publishing output and the authors of the territory.

The initiative provides for a cycle of public meetings at the Municipal Library to be held in the period between September and May of each year, through events dedicated to the presentation of literary works, cultural discussion and the promotion of dialogue between authors and readers.

The series will culminate in the month of May with the Festival del Libro di Collesano, organised within the national campaign "Il Maggio dei Libri" [The May of Books].

The initiative forms part of the reading-promotion activities aimed at the possibility of putting the Municipality of Collesano forward as a **Città che Legge** [City that Reads].

## 2. Eligible applicants

Applications may be submitted by:

- authors resident in the Municipality of Collesano;
- authors resident in the municipalities of the Madonie or in any case authors resident in the other municipalities of Sicily;
- publishing houses;
- cultural associations;
- authors of literary works;
- entities promoting literary works in agreement with the authors.

The following will constitute preferential qualifications:

- residence in the Municipality of Collesano;
- works dedicated to the history, culture, traditions and identity of the territory;
- works that promote reading and the cultural growth of the community.

**3. Eligible works**

Works published in the last five years belonging to any literary genre are eligible, including:

- fiction;
- poetry;
- non-fiction;
- local history;
- cultural and scientific popularisation;
- children's and young adults' literature;
- comics and graphic novels.

Works will not be admitted that contain:

- discriminatory messages;
- political or religious propaganda;
- offensive or defamatory content;
- apology of violence, racism, homophobia or any form of discrimination;
- content contrary to constitutional principles and to the fundamental rights of the person.

**4. How the events take place**

The meetings will take place at the Municipal Library or in other cultural spaces identified by the municipal administration. The selected authors will present their work through a conversation with a moderator, a speaker or an interlocutor identified by themselves or agreed with the Municipality. The meetings will be included in the official calendar of the series. Participation is free of charge and does not entail any remuneration or reimbursement of expenses in favour of the authors or speakers.

## 5. Submission of candidatures

Interested parties must transmit, together with their application for participation;

- a brief biography of the author;
- a literary curriculum;
- a descriptive sheet of the work;
- any promotional material;
- a digital and/or paper copy of the cover of the volume.

The selected authors must also deliver a copy of the work to the Municipal Library, intended for the consultation and lending service.

## 6. Selection criteria

The candidatures will be assessed for the purposes of drawing up the annual calendar of meetings, taking account:

- of the cultural quality of the proposal;
- of the variety of the themes covered;
- of the interest for the local community;
- of the enhancement of the territory;
- of the overall balance of the programming.

The municipal administration reserves the right to schedule further meetings and cultural activities within the series.

**7. Festival del Libro di Collesano**

The authors taking part in the series may be involved in the initiatives of the **Festival del Libro di Collesano**, which will take place in the month of May within the national campaign "Il Maggio dei Libri". The Festival will represent the concluding moment of the programme and will include presentations, meetings with authors, activities for schools, workshops, moments of shared reading and initiatives dedicated to the promotion of books and culture.

## 8. Information

For information, you may contact the office of the assessorato for social and cultural services of the Municipality of Collesano or write an email addressed to protocollo@comune.collesano.pa.it.

**9. Procedures for the submission of project proposals**

The promoting entities may, on pain of exclusion, transmit their project proposal to the PEC address protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it or deliver it by hand to the Ufficio Protocollo [filing office] of the Municipality of Collesano, stating in the subject line the wording "**Manifestazione di interesse per la partecipazione alla rassegna letteraria comunale "Un Borgo di Libri – Festival Diffuso del Libro di Collesano "** by 12:00 on 19/08/2026.

Pursuant to and for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and of the D.Lgs. n. 196/2003 as amended, the data requested by this bando [call for applications] and by the attached forms will be used exclusively for the purposes provided for by the call itself and will be kept for the period of time necessary to achieve the purposes for which they are collected and processed, and in ways suitable for guaranteeing the security and confidentiality of the data and every other right belonging to the data subject.
