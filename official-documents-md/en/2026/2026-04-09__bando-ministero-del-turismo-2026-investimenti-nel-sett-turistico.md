## Subject: Ministry of Tourism Call (2026) – Investments in the Tourism Sector - Communications.

Dear operator,

we inform you that the Decree of the Ministry of Tourism has been published defining the implementing rules for the incentives intended for tourism enterprises, within the framework of the 2026 Budget Law. The measure, managed by Invitalia, aims to strengthen and modernise the national tourism offering and has a total endowment of 109 million euros.

The benefits provided are of a mixed nature, namely ("soni" for "sono" [text as printed]):

- A non-repayable grant of up to 30% of the investment and up to a maximum threshold of 4,5 million;
- Subsidised financing for the remaining 70%.

Eligible investments must have a minimum amount of 1 million euros and a maximum of up to 15 million euros, and must be completed by 30/09/2028.

The programme intends to achieve the following strategic objectives:

1. De-seasonalisation of tourist flows;
2. Innovation and digitalisation of facilities;
3. Environmental sustainability and energy efficiency;
4. Promotion of investments compliant with ESG criteria.

Enterprises in the tourism sector (hotels, restaurants, B&Bs, holiday villages, etc.) active for at least 3 years may take part in the call.

The types of fundable interventions concern:

* Energy efficiency (insulation, window frames, smart air conditioning);
* Renewable energy (photovoltaic, solar thermal, storage systems);
* Digitalisation (cabling, home automation, AI infrastructure);
* Facility upgrading (wellness, swimming pools, conference centres, accessibility);
* Intangible investments (software, patents, licences).
