*Model n. 7 / REF — Public poster convening the Municipal Electoral Commission for the appointment of poll clerks (scrutatori)*

# CONSTITUTIONAL REFERENDUM

#### OF SUNDAY 22 AND MONDAY 23 MARCH 2026

### CONVOCATION

**OF THE MUNICIPAL ELECTORAL COMMISSION**

**FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF THE POLL CLERKS**

# MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

#### THE MAYOR

Having regard to article 19, first paragraph, of law 25 May 1970, n. 352, laying down rules on the *referendums* provided for by the Constitution and on the people's legislative initiative;

Having regard to article 2 of law 22 May 1978, n. 199, as replaced by article 2 of law 7 May 2009, n. 46;

Having regard to article 9 of law 23 April 1976, n. 136, laying down rules on the reduction of time limits and the simplification of the electoral procedure;

Having regard to article 6 of law 8 March 1989, n. 95, laying down rules for the establishment of the register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station clerk;

Having regard to article 1, paragraphs 1 and 3, of decree-law 27 December 2025, n. 196;

Having regard to the decree of the President of the Republic of 13 January 2026, by which the electoral assemblies were convened for Sunday 22 and Monday 23 March 2026, and the decree of the President of the Republic of 7 February 2026, by which the question was specified for the holding of the following ***CONSTITUTIONAL REFERENDUM***

«Do you approve the text of the law revising articles 87, tenth paragraph, 102, first paragraph, 104, 105, 106, third paragraph, 107, first paragraph, and 110 of the Constitution, approved by Parliament and published in the *Gazzetta Ufficiale* [Official Gazette] of 30 October 2025 with the title "Rules on the judicial system and the establishment of the Disciplinary Court"?»;

## GIVES NOTICE

that the Municipal Electoral Commission is convened at the Municipality's seat in public session on 27 FEBRUARY 2026, at 10.30, to proceed with the appointment of the poll clerks to be assigned to the section offices for the constitutional *referendum* called for Sunday 22 and Monday 23 March 2026.

*Collesano, 25 February 2026*

**THE MAYOR**
***D.ssa Tiziana Cascio***
