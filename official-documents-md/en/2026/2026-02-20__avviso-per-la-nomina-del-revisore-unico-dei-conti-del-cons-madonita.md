# PUBLIC NOTICE FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF THE SOLE AUDITOR (Revisore Unico dei Conti) OF THE CONSORZIO MADONITA PER LA LEGALITA' E LO SVILUPPO [Madonie Consortium for Legality and Development] FOR THE THREE-YEAR PERIOD 2026/2029

## THE HEAD OF THE FINANCIAL AREA

**Whereas**

- By resolution of the Assembly of Mayors n. 02 of 27/01/2023, enforceable pursuant to law, the board of Auditors was elected for the three-year period 2022/2025;
- the above engagement ran from the date of enforceability of the appointment resolution, without prejudice to the prorogatio period provided for by art. 235, paragraph 1, of D. Lgs. 267/00;
- the prorogatio period under art. 3 of D.L. 16 May 1994, n. 293 (rules on the extension of administrative bodies), converted with amendment by art. 1, paragraph 1, of law 15 July 1994, n. 444, transposed by the Sicilian Region with art. 1 of L.R. 28 March 1995, n. 22, is set at a maximum of forty-five days;
- it is necessary to proceed with the renewal of the said Auditing Body for the subsequent three-year period 2026/2029;

**Having regard to**

- articles 234 to 241 of D. Lgs. n. 267/2000, concerning the economic-financial auditing of Local Authorities;

**Considering that**

- by Decree of the Ministry of the Interior of 15/02/2012 n. 23, the regulation implementing art. 16, paragraph 25, of D.L. 13 August 2011, n. 138, converted, with amendments, by law 14 September 2011, n. 148, was approved for the establishment of the register of Auditors of Local Authorities and the method of choosing the economic-financial auditing body;
- by circular F.L. n. 7/2012 of the Ministry of the Interior - Department for Internal and Territorial Affairs - the first indications were issued regarding the new system for appointing local-authority auditors provided for by the aforesaid art. 16, para. 25 of D.L. n. 138/2011, converted, with amendments, by L. n. 148/2011;
- the Sicilian Region legislated on the application of the above rule with art. 10 of L.R. 17 March 2016 n. 3, as replaced by art. 6 of L.R. 11 August 2016 n. 17, in turn subsequently amended by art. 39 of L.R. n. 16 of 11 August 2017, which provides verbatim: *"article 10 of regional law 17 March 2016, n. 3 is replaced by the following:*

*1. In the local authorities of the Region, economic-financial auditing is carried out by a board of auditors composed of three members, chosen in the manner set out in this article. In municipalities with a population of up to 5.000 inhabitants and in unions of municipalities, economic-financial auditing is carried out by a single auditor.*

*2. In accordance with the provisions of article 16, paragraph 25, of decree-law 13 August 2011, n. 138, converted, with amendments, by law 14 September 2011, n. 148, local-authority auditors are chosen by drawing lots among professionals resident in Sicily, enrolled in the register of statutory auditors under legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39, as well as among members of the order of chartered accountants and accounting experts, who have applied to take part in the procedure for choosing the local authority's auditing body and who possess the following requirements:*

**a) band 1 – Municipalities with a population of up to 5.000 inhabitants:**

1) *Enrolment for at least two years in the register of statutory auditors or in the order of chartered accountants and accounting experts;*
2) *Attainment, in the preceding year, of at least 10 training credits for having taken part in training courses and/or seminars on public accounting and the economic and financial management of territorial authorities.*

**b) band 2 – Municipalities with a population of more than 5.000 and up to 15.000 inhabitants:** *omissis.*

**c) band 3 – Municipalities with a population of more than 15.000 inhabitants:** *omissis.*

3) *For the purpose of choosing the auditor or the board of auditors, each local authority, within two months before the expiry of the auditing body, issues a notice to be published on the local authority's institutional website and on that of the regional Department of Local Autonomies. In the event of renunciation or cessation, for any reason, of the engagement of the auditor or of a member of the board, the municipality issues the notice referred to in this paragraph within 15 days of the cessation of the engagement.*

4) *The drawing of lots is carried out publicly, in the presence of the municipal secretary, in a session of the council or assembly body to be held within 45 days of the expiry date of the auditing body.*

5) *Failure to observe the time limits referred to in paragraphs 3 and 4 entails, following formal notice with a deadline for compliance, the appointment of a commissioner "od acta" ["ad acta" misprinted in the original] pursuant to article 24 of regional law 3 December 1991, n. 44 as amended and supplemented.*

6) *In boards of auditors, the functions of chair are exercised by the member who has held the greatest number of auditor engagements at local authorities and, in the event of a tie, those functions are assumed by the one who exercised them in the authority with the largest population.*

7) *Each auditor may not take on more than eight engagements. This incompatibility must be declared when submitting the application to take part in the procedure for choosing the local authority's auditing body*

8) *On first application, pending the effective start of the procedure referred to in this article, in place of the training credits referred to in letters a), b) and e) ["e)" as printed, for "c)"] of paragraph 2 for all local authorities, applicants must have attained at least 10 training credits recognised by the competent professional orders or by associations representing them, for having taken part in training courses and/or seminars on public accounting and the economic and financial management of territorial authorities";*

## GIVES NOTICE THAT

- the procedure is launched for the appointment of the Sole Auditor of the Consorzio Madonita per la legalità e lo sviluppo for the three-year period 2026/2029, running from the date of enforceability of the appointment resolution;
- for the purposes of the above election, Auditors resident in the Sicilian Region are required to have been enrolled, for at least two (2) years, in the register of Statutory Auditors under legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39 or in the Order of Chartered Accountants and Accounting Experts, and to have attained, in the preceding year, at least 10 training credits for having taken part in training courses and/or seminars on public accounting and the economic and financial management of territorial authorities;
- the duration of the engagement, the causes of cessation, incompatibility and ineligibility, the functioning, the limits on the assignment of engagements, the functions and the responsibilities of the Auditor are established by artt. 234-241 of D. Lgs. n. 267/2000, as well as by art. 6 of L.R. 17/2016, amended by art. 39 of L.R. n. 16/2017;
- the basic annual remuneration due to the auditor is established on the basis of the provisions in force and art. 241 of D. Lgs. n. 267/2000 and is parameterised according to what is established by the Ministerial Decree of 21 December 2018;
- the auditor must not be in the conditions of incapacity referred to in art. 2382 of the civil code, nor in the situations of incompatibility or ineligibility referred to in art. 236 of D. Lgs. 267/2000, according to which:

1) *the incompatibility cases of the first paragraph of art. 2399 of the civil code apply to Auditors, with "administrators" meaning the members of the local authority's executive body;*
2) *the engagement of economic-financial auditing may not be exercised by members of the local authority's bodies and by those who held such an engagement in the two years preceding the appointment, by members of the regional control body, by the secretary and by employees of the regions, provinces, metropolitan cities, mountain communities and unions of municipalities, with regard to the local authorities included in the territorial district of competence;*
3) *members of accounting auditing bodies may not take on engagements or consultancies at the local authority or at bodies or institutions dependent on it or in any case subject to its control or supervision;*

- for the limits on the assignment of engagements and the prohibition of accumulation, the state and regional legislation in force applies;
- payment of the remuneration will be arranged by the Head of the Economic-Financial Area by his own determination.

## APPLICATION FOR PARTICIPATION

The application for participation, according to the template annexed to this notice, complete with personal data, residence in a Municipality of the Sicilian Region, domicile, VAT number (if held) and tax code, must contain a declaration of acceptance of the conditions of this notice, a list of the Local Authorities at which the function of Auditor is or has been performed, and authorisation for the processing of personal data (D.Lgs. 30/6/2003, n. 196 as amended and GDPR Reg. EU 2016/679) limited to the procedure in question, and must be accompanied by the following documentation:

a) Certification of enrolment, for at least two years, in the Register of Statutory Auditors under legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39, or in the Order of Chartered Accountants and Accounting Experts, or a substitute declaration made pursuant to D.P.R. n. 445/2000 showing the enrolment number and year;

b) Certification attesting the attainment of at least 10 training credits in the year 2025 recognised by the competent professional orders or by associations representing them, for having taken part in training courses and/or seminars on public accounting and the economic and financial management of territorial authorities, or a substitute declaration made pursuant to D.P.R. n. 445/2000;

c) A substitute declaration of affidavit made pursuant to D.P.R. n. 445/2000 by which the competing party declares:

1. that they are not in the conditions of incompatibility referred to in art. 236, paragraph 1, D.Lgs. n. 267/00, according to which: *"3. The incompatibility cases of the first paragraph of article 2399 of the civil code apply to auditors, with 'administrators' meaning the members of the local authority's executive body".*
2. that they are not in one of the conditions of ineligibility referred to in art. 236, paragraphs 2 and 3, D.Lgs. n. 267/00, according to which: *"2. The engagement of economic-financial auditing may not be exercised by members of the local authority's bodies and by those who held such an engagement in the two years preceding the appointment, by members of the regional control body, by the secretary and by employees of the local authority at which the economic-financial auditing body is to be appointed, and by employees of the regions, provinces, metropolitan cities, mountain communities and unions of municipalities with regard to the local authorities included in the territorial district of competence. 3. Members of accounting auditing bodies may not take on engagements or consultancies at the local authority or at bodies or institutions dependent on it or in any case subject to its control or supervision".*
3. that there are no cases of ineligibility and incompatibility established by law, or the commitment, if elected, to eliminate any cause of incompatibility within the times and in the ways set out in D.Lgs. n. 39/2013;
4. that they are not subject to prevention measures and have no final convictions for offences against the Public Administration;
5. that they have not been removed or dispensed from employment at a public administration and have not forfeited a previous employment;
6. the absence of conflicts of interest with the engagement to be assumed;
7. that they undertake, without any reservation, if the engagement is conferred, to perform it in accordance with all the conditions, methods, prescriptions, clauses and anything else provided for by artt. 235, 239 and 240 of D.Lgs. n. 267/00;
8. that they are aware of the obligation of prompt communication regarding the arising of one of the causes of non-conferrability and incompatibility referred to in D.Lgs. 39/2013;
9. compliance with the limits on taking on engagements under art. 6, paragraph 7, of L.R. n. 17/2016, as amended by art. 39 of L.R. n. 16/2017, according to which each auditor may not take on more than eight engagements;
10. a declaration of acceptance of the office, in the event of appointment, by drawing of lots;
11. a non-authenticated copy of a personal identity document;
12. a detailed curriculum vitae in European format, dated and signed.

The engagement will last three years, running from the date of enforceability of the appointment resolution.

## APPOINTMENT PROCEDURE

On expiry of the deadline for submitting applications, the formal verification of the applications will be carried out. Subsequently, the admitted applications will form a list in chronological order of arrival, numbered in ascending order, which will be annexed to the proposed council appointment resolution. The Assembly will be convened to make the appointment by drawing lots from the list thus formed, carried out publicly. The names drawn will be subjected to verification of the declarations made in the application. The appointment is subject to the automatic condition of forfeiture for untruthfulness of one or more declarations made in the application for participation.

## REMUNERATION

The basic annual remuneration due to the economic-financial auditing Body is established by the Assembly in the same appointment resolution, taking account of the provisions of the Decree of the Ministry of the Interior, in agreement with the Ministry of Economy and Finance, of 21 December 2018 and of art. 241 of D. Lgs. n. 267/2000.
