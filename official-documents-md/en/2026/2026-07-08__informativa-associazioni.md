# Information notice on the processing of personal data (registry data, etc.) collected in relation to participation in the Collesano school camp

(EU Reg. 2016/679 - "General Data Protection Regulation")

**Data Controller**

The Controller of the processing of personal data is the Comune di Collesano, in the person of its legal representative pro tempore, located in Collesano, Via Montegrappa. The Controller undertakes to process the personal data provided according to the principles laid down by the GDPR (lawfulness, fairness and transparency of processing, necessity, relevance and minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, confidentiality and accountability).

**1) Type of data, legal basis and purposes of their processing**

The processing will concern the registry data of the minor collected to allow participation in the Collesano school camp.

The type of personal data of the minor requested and acquired is the following:

- Identifiers: name, date of birth;
- Special categories: food intolerances, medical conditions, etc.

For the sole purpose of validating the consent to the processing of the minor's data, the name and the codice fiscale [personal tax code] of the parents/guardians will be processed.

The Controller does not carry out profiling activities, either in automatic form or in any other form, on the data collected.

The legal basis of the processing activity is the consent expressed by the parents/guardians of the minor concerned.

**2) Methods of data processing**

The processing will be carried out with the aid of electronic and paper means and may consist of any operation or set of operations among those indicated in art. 1 paragraph 2 letter b) of the European provision referred to above [reference as printed in the original]. The data will be processed by authorised and trained staff.

**3) Data retention period**

Personal data will be kept, for tax reasons, legal obligations or for the protection of rights, for no more than 10 years, unless further needs arise.

**4) Provision of data or possible refusal**

The provision of personal data is necessary for the performance of the activity and purposes described above. Failure to provide the data will not allow participation in the school camp.

**5) Communication of data**

Personal data will not be communicated to further parties, except for those parties in relation to whom a legal obligation to that effect exists (public security authorities, etc.).

**6) Dissemination of data**

The data collected will not be disseminated.

**7) Rights of the data subject**

At any moment, the data subject may exercise, vis-à-vis the Controller, the rights provided for by arts. 15-22 of the Regulation, that is, by way of example, he/she may request:

- the revocation of consent (which will not affect processing activities that are impossible to interrupt or already carried out);
- access to his/her personal data and to the information relating to them;
- rectification in the event of their inaccuracy or the integration of incomplete data;
- the erasure of the data upon the occurrence of the conditions provided for by art. 17, paragraph 1, of the Regulation and in compliance with the exceptions provided for in paragraph 3;
- the restriction of their processing in the cases indicated in art. 18, paragraph 1 of the Regulation.

The aforesaid rights may be exercised by written communication to be sent either to the contact addresses of the Data Controller, as indicated above, or by sending an email to the address protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it

The data subject will have the right to lodge a complaint with the Autorità Garante per la Protezione dei dati personali [Italian data protection authority] according to the procedures specifically indicated on the institution's website: www.garanteprivacy.it.
