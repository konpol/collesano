Collesano, 19 March 2026

To the Municipalities of Collesano and Scillato

**Subject**: Request for suspension of the school canteen service for the **REFERENDUM.**

Notice is given that, in view of the handover of the school premises for the confirmatory referendum of 22 and 23 March 2026, the suspension of the canteen is requested starting from 20 March 2026 and for the days of 23 and 24 March 2026 for the Primary school buildings of Collesano and for the Primary–Secondary of Scillato; teaching activities will resume regularly on 25 March 2026. *(single run-on sentence as in the original)*

Thank you for your cooperation.

The School Principal
*Prof. Biagio Ferro*
