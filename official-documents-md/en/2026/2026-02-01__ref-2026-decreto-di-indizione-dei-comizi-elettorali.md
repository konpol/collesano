### SUBJECT: Referendum elections 22-23 March 2026. Decree calling the electoral colleges.

# Annex — THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC

Having regard to articles 138 and 87 of the Constitution;

Having regard to law 25 May 1970, n. 352, containing «Rules on the referendums provided for by the Constitution and on the people's legislative initiative», and subsequent amendments;

Having regard to art. 1, paragraph 1, of decree-law 27 December 2025, n. 196, containing «Urgent provisions for the electoral and referendum consultations of the year 2026»;

Having regard to the text of the constitutional law approved, in second vote, by absolute majority, but less than two thirds of the members of each Chamber, containing: «Rules on the organisation of the judiciary and the establishment of the Disciplinary Court», published in the Official Gazette n. 253 of 30 October 2025;

Having regard to the order of the Central Office for the referendum, established at the Supreme Court of Cassation, issued on 18 November 2025, filed and communicated on the same date, by which the request for a popular referendum was declared legitimate and admitted, pursuant to art. 138, second paragraph, of the Constitution, for the approval of the aforesaid text of the constitutional law;

Having regard, in particular, to art. 15 of the aforesaid law n. 352 of 1970, which provides that the referendum be called within sixty days of the communication of the order admitting it, and that it take place on a Sunday between the fiftieth and the seventieth day following the issue of the calling decree;

Deeming it therefore appropriate to proceed with the calling in compliance with the deadline of 17 January 2026;

Noting that, for voting purposes, the dates of 22 and 23 March 2026 fall at the expiry of the seventieth day following the issue of the calling decree;

Having regard to the resolution of the Council of Ministers adopted at the meeting of 12 January 2026;

On the proposal of the President of the Council of Ministers, in agreement with the Ministers of the Interior and of Justice;

Issues the following decree:

The confirmatory popular referendum is called on the following question:

«Do you approve the text of the constitutional law concerning "Rules on the organisation of the judiciary and the establishment of the Disciplinary Court", approved by Parliament and published in the Official Gazette n. 253 of 30 October 2025?».

The related electoral colleges are convened for Sunday 22 March and Monday 23 March 2026.

This decree will be published in the Official Gazette of the Italian Republic.

Given in Rome, on 13 January 2026

### MATTARELLA

Meloni, President of the Council of Ministers
