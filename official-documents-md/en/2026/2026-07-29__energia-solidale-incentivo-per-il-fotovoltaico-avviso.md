Subject: Solidarity energy, the regional financing for photovoltaics — Communication.

Dear all, we inform you that the Regione Siciliana has published a notice granting zero-interest financing of up to 19.920 euro for the installation of photovoltaic systems and storage systems intended for self-consumption. From 12:00 on 14 July last, applications may be submitted for the regional measure against energy poverty, provided for by article 36 of regional law n. 1 of 5 January 2026, managed by Irfis FinSicilia and promoted by the regional department for energy. The online desk will remain open until 12:00 on 14 October.

It is a measure designed to help Sicilian families who, through self-production of energy, will be able to cut the amount of their bills.

An intervention that combines the social aspect with the need to promote the energy transition towards renewable and sustainable forms of production. A simple instrument to implement, which reduces bureaucratic steps and covers the expenses incurred for the installation of the systems.

With this initiative the Regione Siciliana positions itself as an advanced laboratory of energy policies, affirming a model that combines environmental sustainability, social equity and administrative innovation. The contents of the measure were in fact the subject of discussion with the Sicilian Episcopal Conference and with the Caritas organisations, with a view to activating a network that guarantees the maximum effectiveness of the intervention.

The overall budget is 12 million euro.

Access is open to natural persons belonging to family units with a valid ordinary ISEE [household means-test indicator], resident in a property located in Sicily over which they hold a right in rem. The financing covers 100 per cent of the eligible expenses, has a duration of ten years, including one year of pre-amortisation, and does not require third-party guarantees.

The amounts vary according to the power of the system and the capacity of the storage system: from 15.400 euro for a 3 kW system with a 5 kWh battery up to 19.920 euro for a 6 kW system with 10 kWh storage.

Eligible for financing are, among other things, panels, inverters, batteries, necessary works, grid connection, design, testing and a multi-risk insurance policy.

<u>The systems must be new and have a power between 3 and 6 kW.</u>

Applications will be assessed through a ranking list that will take account above all of the ISEE and, to a lesser extent, of the power of the installation. The mere order of submission will therefore not be decisive.

Applications must be sent on the online platform using SPID level 2 [public digital identity system], the National Services Card or the Electronic Identity Card. Only one application per family unit is admitted.

The full notice is available on the institutional website of Irfis, reachable through this link. <u>https://www.irfis.it/poverta-energetica/</u>

The platform through which applications are to be submitted is accessible from this link https://incentivisicilia.irfis.it/index.html?app=FISI&idt=ZnIMo88a5fAwqsYc
