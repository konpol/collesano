## MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

***Metropolitan City of Palermo***

## ADMINISTRATIVE AND SOCIAL-WELFARE AREA — ASSETS SERVICE

# PUBLIC NOTICE OF EXPRESSION OF INTEREST

### Enhancement of an area owned by the municipality through concession to third parties.

#### The RUP [procedure manager]

Having regard to the Determination of the Head of the Assets Service n° 263 of 25/02/2026 approving the public notice of expression of interest for the assignment in concession of land encumbered by civic use and the application for Participation - *"Expression of interest – Enhancement of Municipal Land in C/da Drinzi"*;

# GIVES NOTICE

that citizens interested in obtaining the assignment in concession of the municipal land located in c/da Drinzi and encumbered by civic use are invited to submit their expressions of interest by presenting a specific application.

This notice is exclusively exploratory in character and does not open any auction procedure, nor does it provide for ranking lists or the award of scores.

With this notice, the Municipality of Collesano intends to promote the recovery and enhancement of the land located in Contrada Drinzi, registered in the Land Cadastre at Sheet N. 38, Parcels n. 96 (Ha 0.13.60) and n. 120 (Ha 0.36.80). Given that the aforesaid land is encumbered by civic uses *ab origine* with a "pasture" destination and has been unused for decades, the Municipal Administration intends, respecting the destination in favour of the community, to promote projects with positive effects in the social, cultural, environmental or sports field. With this notice, the Administration does not start an auction procedure but a fact-finding survey aimed at collecting proposals and project ideas. It is understood that the expression of interest does not constitute proof of possession of the general requirements required for the award of the works, which must be declared again by the interested parties and verified by the Municipality in the subsequent procedure. The Authority reserves the right to suspend, modify or cancel the procedure relating to this exploratory notice and not to proceed with the opening of the subsequent procedures for the award of the works to realise the shared project.

#### ART. 1 (PURPOSES)

The Municipality of Collesano intends to promote the recovery and enhancement of the land located in Contrada Drinzi, registered in the Land Cadastre at Sheet N. 38, Parcels n. 96 (Ha 0.13.60) and n. 120 (Ha 0.36.80).

Notice is given that the area covered by this notice is encumbered by a Right of Civic Use, per the list approved by the Commissioner for the liquidation of civic uses with measure n. 470 of 22/02/2006; therefore, every proposal for use must be rigorously compatible with the **collective nature of the asset** and aimed at the benefit of the local community, in compliance with the legislation in force (Law n. 1766/1927 and Law n. 168/2017).

The objective is to transform this area into a resource for the community, favouring projects with positive effects in the social, cultural, environmental or sports field.

The actual concession of the land **remains subject** to the possible procedure for the change of use or to the compatibility verification by the competent regional offices.

The proposer releases the Municipality from all liability if the regional authorisation process for the civic use is not successful.

### ART. 2 (DESCRIPTION OF THE AREA)

- **Approximate surface:** 5.000 m²
- **Current state:** Uncultivated green area
- **Constraints:** Pasture destination

### ART. 3 (WHO CAN PARTICIPATE)

The following may submit their "Expression of Interest" and the related concept proposal:

- Individual or associated citizens;
- Non-profit associations and APS [social-promotion associations];
- Third-Sector Entities;
- Enterprises and start-ups (with particular attention to youth and green entrepreneurship).

### ART. 4 (CONTENTS OF THE PROPOSAL)

Various types of use may be proposed, by way of example:

- **Urban or educational gardens** for the promotion of biodiversity.
- **Equipped areas** for recreational-sports activities or leisure.
- **Open-air exhibition or cultural spaces.**
- **Urban regeneration projects** with social purposes.

Proposers must send a brief report (maximum 3 sides) illustrating:

1. **The project idea** and its purposes.
2. **The reference target** (who the project is addressed to).
3. **The sustainability** of the management (how the area is to be maintained).

### ART. 4 BIS (DESTINATION CONSTRAINTS AND COMPATIBILITY)

Given the nature of the asset, proposals will be evaluated primarily on their capacity to:

- **Guarantee collective use:** The area may not be closed off exclusively, but must remain accessible to the citizenry.
- **Environmental protection:** Projects providing for the conservation of the landscape, biodiversity and local traditions.
- **Absence of permanent works:** The building of concrete or permanent structures that definitively alter the state of the places is not permitted, except for what is strictly necessary and authorised by the competent bodies.

### ART. 5 (METHODS AND DEADLINES FOR SUBMISSION OF THE EXPRESSION OF INTEREST)

Proposals must arrive **no later than Wednesday 18/03/2026** at 12:00, by a specific duly signed declaration transmitted to the PEC address: <u>protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it</u>, and must bear in the subject the wording "*Manifestazione di interesse – Valorizzazione Terreno Comunale in C/da Drinzi*", with a plain photocopy of a valid identity document of the signatory attached.

Expressions of interest arriving after the indicated peremptory deadline will not be taken into consideration.

### ART. 6 – EVALUATION CRITERIA FOR PROPOSALS

The evaluation of the expressions of interest received will be carried out by a specially appointed Technical Commission, which will assign a maximum score of 100 on the basis of the following parameters:

#### A) Social Value and Public Interest (Max 30 points)

- Capacity of the proposal to involve the citizenry, schools or disadvantaged persons.
- Type of services offered free of charge to the community.
- Coherence with the purposes of social aggregation and promotion of the territory.

#### B) Compatibility with the Civic-Uses Regime (Max 25 points)

- Level of guarantee of collective use of the area (absence of exclusive closures).
- Absence of impacts that could prejudice the local community's rights over the asset.

#### C) Environmental and Landscape Sustainability (Max 20 points)

- Use of green-building techniques or natural materials for any furnishings (removable).
- Projects for the protection of biodiversity, planting of native species and water saving.
- Minimum visual impact and compliance with the landscape constraint.

#### D) Maintenance and Management Plan (Max 15 points)

- Completeness of the plan for cleaning, mowing and upkeep of the area.
- Capacity of the proposer to guarantee surveillance and safety of the site without costs for the Municipality.

#### E) Experience and Local Roots (Max 10 points)

- Documented previous experience in similar sectors (social, agricultural, environmental).
- Historical presence of the proposer in the municipal territory.

**Admissibility threshold**: Proposals that do not reach a minimum overall score of 60/100 will not be taken into consideration.

### ART. 7 (SAFEGUARD CLAUSE AND SUSPENSIVE CONDITION)

Notice is given that the concession of the area, being encumbered by civic uses, is subject to the special legislation in force in the Sicilian Region. Therefore, the effectiveness of the concession measure and of the related agreement remains expressly subject to obtaining the favourable opinion, clearance or approval of the Commissioner for the liquidation of civic uses for Sicily and/or of the competent Regional Department, as provided for by Law 1766/1927 as amended and by the regional sector legislation.

The Municipality reserves the right not to proceed with the assignment if the protection bodies cited above express a negative opinion or prescribe conditions incompatible with the proposal submitted.

The proposer, by signing the application for participation, declares awareness of this condition and waives as of now any claim for compensation or indemnity against the Municipal Administration in the event of failure to complete the act for reasons attributable to the civic-uses protection bodies.

### ART. 8 DURATION

The concession will last 10 years with the possibility of renewal for a further 10 years;
