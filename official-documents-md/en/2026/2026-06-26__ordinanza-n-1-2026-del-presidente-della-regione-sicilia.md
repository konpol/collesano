## ITALIAN REPUBLIC

## Ordinance n. 01 of 12/06/2026

*Contingent and urgent ordinance [ordinanza contingibile e urgente: an emergency order] in the matter of hygiene and public health, pursuant to article 32 of law 23 December 1978, n. 833. Provisions for work activities in the agricultural and flower-nursery sectors, on building sites and similar, in quarries and their external appurtenances, as well as in the logistics sector, including those who carry out delivery of goods on behalf of others in urban areas with the aid of bicycles or two-wheeled motor vehicles, in conditions of direct and prolonged exposure to high temperatures and exposure to solar radiation.*

## The President of the Region

## Having regard to:

- art. 32 of the Constitution [text as printed: "Costituzionea"]
- the Statute of the Sicilian Region;
- article 32 of law 23 December 1978, n. 833, "Establishment of the national health service", which provides: "The Minister of Health may issue ordinances of a contingent and urgent nature, in the matter of hygiene and public health and of veterinary policing, with effect extended to the entire national territory or to a part of it comprising several regions.(…) In the same matters, ordinances of a contingent and urgent nature are issued by the president of the regional government and by the mayor, with effect extended respectively to the region or to a part of its territory comprising several municipalities, and to the municipal territory.";
- Legislative Decree 9 April 2008, n. 81;
- article 650 of the Criminal Code;
- having regard to the "Guidelines for the protection of workers from heat and solar radiation" of 19/06/2025, approved by the Conference of the Regions and Autonomous Provinces, which provide an overall view of the elements that characterise the path leading to the achievement of healthy and safe working conditions, in relation to the risk constituted by high temperatures and solar radiation

## Considering:

**that** the high temperatures typical of the summer season in Sicily make the performance of work activities risky in the sectors in which work is carried out mainly outdoors;

**that** prolonged exposure to the sun represents a danger to the health of workers exposed for long periods of time to solar radiation, causing heat stress and heat strokes with outcomes that are at times even lethal;

**that** work in the agricultural and flower-nursery sector, on building sites and similar, in quarries and their external appurtenances, as well as in logistics, including the delivery of goods on behalf of others in urban areas with the aid of bicycles or two-wheeled motor vehicles (so-called "riders"), is carried out essentially outdoors without any possibility for the workers to shelter from the sun and the heat in the moments of the day characterised by a considerable rise in temperature;

**that** INAIL [national institute for insurance against accidents at work], within the worklimate project (Inail-CNR), has made available on the website <u>www.worklimate.it</u> the national forecast maps of the risk of heat exposure, in order to contain the exposure risk of workers;

## Deeming:

- it necessary, for all the areas or zones of the territory of the Sicilian Region concerned by the performance of work activity in conditions of prolonged exposure to the sun in the agricultural and flower-nursery sector, on building sites and similar, in quarries and their external appurtenances, as well as in logistics, including the delivery of goods on behalf of others in urban areas with the aid of bicycles or two-wheeled motor vehicles, to issue a measure for the protection of health and public hygiene aimed at reducing the impact of environmental heat stress on the health of the workers engaged in such activities and avoiding the resulting consequences on health and, therefore, the risks to which the relevant personnel are exposed;
- therefore, for workers exposed to risks deriving from rising temperatures, in particular on building sites, in agriculture and flower-nursery work, in quarries and their appurtenances, as well as in logistics, including the delivery of goods on behalf of others in urban areas with the aid of bicycles or two-wheeled motor vehicles (so-called "riders"), to order, with immediate effect and until 31 August 2026, subject to subsequent measures, a work prohibition across the whole Sicilian territory between 12.30 and 16.00, on the days on which the risk map published on the website www.worklimate.it/scelta-mappa/sole-attivita-fisica-alta/ referring to: "workers exposed to the sun" with "intense physical activity" at 12.00, signals a "HIGH" risk level;
- it necessary not to apply the prohibition of this Ordinance to Public Administrations, to public-service concessionaires, to their contractors, in the case of interventions of public utility, of civil protection or of safeguarding public safety, without prejudice in any case to the adoption by employers of suitable organisational and operational measures necessary to bring the risk of workers' exposure to high temperatures back to an acceptable level according to the risk assessment conducted in compliance with the provisions of Legislative Decree 9 April 2008, n. 81 on safety at work;
- that the conditions and prerequisites of art.32 of law 23 December 1978, n.833 exist for the adoption of an Ordinance in the matter of hygiene and public health;

## Noting that:

- the measures of the Mayors relating to the municipal territory remain unaffected, where they do not conflict with this Ordinance, as do the obligations assigned to the Employer for the protection of workers' health and safety at work;
- This Ordinance does not affect any specific company Agreements aimed at protecting workers' health in the face of such a risk, where the latter improve on the content of the Ordinance in question.

## ORDERS

With immediate effect and until 31 August 2026, subject to subsequent measures, with reference to the regional territory of Sicily:

1. work in conditions of prolonged exposure to the sun is prohibited in the agricultural and flower-nursery, building and similar sectors, in quarries and their external appurtenances, as well as in logistics, including the delivery of goods on behalf of others in urban areas with the aid of bicycles or two-wheeled motor vehicles (so-called "riders"), from 12.30 to 16.00, on the days and in the areas in which the risk map published on the website http://www.worklimate.it/scelta-mappa/sole-attivita-fisica-alta/ referring to: "workers exposed to the sun" with "intense physical activity" at 12.00, signals a "HIGH" risk level;
2. the above prohibition does not apply to Public Administrations, to public-service concessionaires, to their contractors, in the case of interventions of public utility, of civil protection or of safeguarding public safety, without prejudice in any case to the adoption by employers of suitable organisational and operational measures necessary to bring the risk of workers' exposure to high temperatures back to an acceptable level according to the risk assessment conducted in compliance with the provisions of Legislative Decree 9 April 2008, n. 81 on safety at work;
3. employers, in any case, shall take care to adopt suitable organisational and operational measures necessary to bring the risk of workers' exposure to high temperatures back to an acceptable level according to the risk assessment conducted in compliance with the provisions of Legislative Decree 9 April 2008, n. 81 on safety at work;
4. non-compliance with this measure is punished pursuant to art. 650 of the Criminal Code, if the act does not constitute a more serious offence.

## DIRECTS

## The transmission of this ordinance:

- to the Regional Department of Health
- to the Regional Assessore for Productive Activities
- to the Regional Department of Local Autonomies and the Public Service for the notifications to ANCI and to the Sicilian Municipalities
- to the Regional Department of the Family, Social Policies and Labour for the follow-up and the notifications within the Region's competence, and it has in any case the value of legal notification.
- to the Regional Department of Agriculture, Rural Development and Mediterranean Fishing, also for subsequent forwarding to the agricultural professional organisations of Sicily
- To the Regional Territorial Directorates of INAIL and INPS

Publication in the Official Gazette of the Sicilian Region, as well as on the website of the Presidency

GIVES NOTICE that pursuant to art. 3 of law n. 241/90, against this measure a judicial appeal to the territorially competent TAR [regional administrative court] is admitted within 60 days from the date of its notification, or an extraordinary appeal to the President of the Italian Republic within 120 days from the aforesaid date of notification.

THE PRESIDENT SCHIFANI
