## To the interested Economic Operators

SUBJECT: Self-protective revocation (revoca in autotutela) of the measure and of the procedure for the selection of the managing entity of the logistics platform supporting a stable network among micro-enterprises operating in the sectors of production, processing and marketing of agricultural and agri-food products, crafts and local traditions.

## Having regard to

- art. 21 quinquies of law n. 241 of 7/08/1990

**Recalling**

- the resolution of the Board of Directors of 22.12.2025 approving the Notice of the Notice *(duplication as printed)* — the public notice for the selection of the managing entity of the logistics platform in question;
- the public notice for of selection *(wording as printed)* of the managing entity of the logistics platform in question

**Taking note** of the questions and objections raised by some economic operators regarding certain participation requirements.

**Considering**

1. that Gal Isc Madonie operates with the intention of adopting acts aimed at the best realisation of the public interest, in total transparency and safeguarding maximum participation;
2. that the questions and objections raised cannot be remedied and/or answered with simple clarifications (FAQ) but require the necessary technical-legal analyses aimed at guaranteeing the protection of competition, of transparency and of *favor partecipationis* [the principle favouring participation] and at preventing potential litigation

**Sharing** the view that it is appropriate to proceed with a requalification of the procedure by self-protection, the above-mentioned reasons of public interest existing.

**Noting** that the administrative measure and the procedure may be revoked by self-protection where there is a public interest in the correct conduct of the selection procedure and the correct identification of the managing entity. It is specified that the revocation renders the revoked measure unfit to produce further effects.

**Considering** that the adoption of a self-protective measure represents a power and duty of Gal Isc Madonie to be exercised at any time during the public-evidence procedure, in the presence of needs to protect the principles *(wording as printed)* of impartiality, good performance and good administration, competition, favor partecipationis and transparency.

**Considering**, as regards the "reasonable time" for the revocation, the absence of consolidation of a set of interests, given that the management contract has not yet been concluded

**IT IS ORDERED BY SELF-PROTECTION**, for the reasons expressed in the preamble, the revocation of the administrative measure and of the selection procedure in question and of all documents attributable to it. It is specified that the revocation renders the revoked measure unfit to produce further effects.

The undersigned reserves the right, by a subsequent dedicated act, to launch a new procedure which will be published on the website www.madoniegal.it.
