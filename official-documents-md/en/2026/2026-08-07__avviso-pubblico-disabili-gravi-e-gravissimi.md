# PUBLIC NOTICE — Persons with Severe Disabilities

**FNA 2020 – DECREE 1256/2026: SEVERE DISABILITY, INTENSIVE SUPPORT**
**FNA 2024 – DECREE 3770/2024: SEVERE DISABILITY, INTENSIVE SUPPORT**
**FNA 2025 – DECREE 4036/2025: SEVERE DISABILITY, INTENSIVE SUPPORT**

## DEADLINE FOR THE SUBMISSION OF APPLICATIONS: 07/09/2026

**Having regard to** art. 9, paragraph 1, of the L.R. [regional law] n. 8 of 9 May 2017, which established the "Regional fund for disability and for non-self-sufficiency";

**Having regard to** paragraph 3 of the same article, which specifies that "the fund finances social-assistance and social-health services and benefits, pursuant to the legislation in force, taking specific account of the needs of minors affected by disability";

**Having regard to** the D.P.R. [presidential decree] n. 589/Gab of 31/08/2018, which governs the modalities and criteria for the delivery of financial interventions in favour of disability;

## NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN

**that** pursuant to the D.P.R. n. 589/GAB of 31 August 2018, which governs the modalities and criteria for the delivery to the Distretti Socio-Sanitari [social-health districts] of the resources coming from the National Fund for "Non-Self-Sufficiency", the Assessorato della Famiglia, delle Politiche Sociali e del Lavoro [regional department for family, social policies and labour] of the Regione Siciliana orders the assignment to DSS 33 of the following allocation sums:

- FNA 2020 DECREE 1256/2026 (Severe disability, intensive support): resources amounting to € 93.710,35
- FNA 2024 DECREE 3770/2024 (Severe disability, intensive support): resources amounting to € 22.781,25
- FNA 2025 DECREE 4036/2025 (Severe disability, intensive support): resources amounting to € 21.181,53

## PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS

An application requesting assistance may be submitted by all citizens resident in the municipalities of the Distretto Socio-Sanitario D.33: Cefalù, Pollina, San Mauro Castelverde, Castelbuono, Isnello, Collesano, Gratteri, Campofelice di Roccella and Lascari.

**RECIPIENTS OF THE INTERVENTION**

The beneficiaries of the intervention are minors with severe disability who need intensive support. The regulatory framework refers to Law 104/1992, today supplemented by the reform introduced by Legislative Decree n. 62 of 2024. That decree superseded the previous terminology of "handicap in a situation of severity" (former art. 3, paragraph 3), officially replacing it with the expression "person with a need for intensive support" (divided by the law into a high or very high level).

## MODALITIES AND TIME LIMITS FOR SUBMISSION

Applicants are invited to submit an application at the Ufficio Protocollo [filing office] of their municipality of residence <u>no later than 12:00 on 07/09/2026</u>, on pain of inadmissibility. The request must be submitted using the appropriate form and must be accompanied by:

1. Copy of the identity document of the disabled person;
2. Copy of the identity document of the caregiver;
3. Report of the commission attesting the disability in accordance with the legal provisions in force, placed in a sealed envelope bearing the wording "<u>Contains sensitive data</u>".

The forms for submitting the application may be collected at the social services offices of one's municipality of residence, downloaded from the institutional website of the municipalities of DSS 33, or requested at the social secretariat desk of the lead municipality (tel. 0921/924169, Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 12:00).

**ASSESSMENT AND DELIVERY OF THE SERVICE**

Following the submission of the request, the Multidimensional Assessment Unit (social-health) will proceed to define the **Personalised Plan**, indicating the type of service to be delivered. This plan will be agreed with the user and/or with his or her family contact person through the signing of a Service Pact. The pact will identify forms of assistance through territorial services – as provided by art. 3, paragraph 4, letter b) of the D.P.R. n. 589/2018 – such as the Home Assistance Service **(SAD)** and/or the Home Educational Service **(SED)**. Each user meeting the requirements will be assigned an hourly assistance voucher. The value of the voucher will be determined in relation to the number of eligible applications received and to the resources available for each decree. Applications received after the set deadline or lacking the documentation indicated above will be strictly excluded.
