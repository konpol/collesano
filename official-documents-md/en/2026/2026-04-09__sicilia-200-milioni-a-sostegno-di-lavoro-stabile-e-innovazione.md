## Subject: Sicily, 200 million in support of stable employment and innovation - Communication.

Dear Sirs, we inform you that starting from tomorrow Irfis will proceed with the publication of the first two notices of a package worth almost 240 million. The opening of the platforms for the submission of applications will begin 30 days after publication.

Two hundred million euros for those who hire on permanent contracts and for those who invest: -150 million intended for enterprises that choose stable employment, -50 million for those that focus on new investments and innovation.

The two measures are the heart of an overall plan of 239 million defined by the regional government, structured into six lines of intervention.

The first measure is worth 150 million and aims to support enterprises that hire on permanent contracts, with the objective of reducing precariousness and employment fragility, strengthening family income and encouraging young people and skills to remain in Sicily.

Provided for by article 1 of the budget measure, it concerns private enterprises with at least one production unit on the Island. The contribution can reach up to 10% of the annual staff cost, with an increase to 15% for companies that adopt welfare tools or ESG criteria, invest in safety or reduce working hours to 35 hours with equal pay, and also for the hiring of women and of over-50s unemployed for at least two years.

The second measure, of 50 million, looks instead at companies that launch projects of <u>new investments</u> in Sicily.

In this case the contribution, again up to 10%, is calculated on the salary costs of the new jobs created thanks to the investment over a period of two years. Here too, bonuses are provided for company welfare, sustainability, safety, reduction of working hours and hiring of categories considered more fragile. The logic is to accompany growth and development programmes, with particular attention to increasing production capacity, technological transformation and the creation of new employment.

On the State-aid front, the framework is not identical for the two calls:

- the one on permanent hiring remains, for the moment, under the de minimis regime,
- the one on initial investment projects has been structured as regional investment aid pursuant to the GBER regulation.

In the following weeks, notices and decisions are also expected for the remaining 39 million of the package, distributed across four other lines:

- 18 million for south working, designed to attract or bring back to Sicily workers who work remotely;
- 12 million for energy solidarity, with the objective of mitigating the impact of energy costs on the most exposed;
- 5 million for targeted building interventions;
- 4 million for the publishing fund, intended to support informational pluralism and the local information chain.
