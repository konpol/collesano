# PUBLIC NOTICE FOR ADMISSION TO THE "EXPERIMENTAL PROJECT — SOLIDARITY MOBILITY AND ACTIVE CITIZENSHIP"

## GIVES NOTICE

The Municipal Administration announces that, by Municipal Executive Resolution n. 13 of 28/01/2026, it intends to facilitate access for less well-off fellow citizens, the elderly and/or those in conditions of illness, disability and fragility, guaranteeing 2 transport runs per week to be carried out in the time slots from 9.00 to 13.00: on Wednesdays (Cemetery Shuttle Service) — Objective: visiting the deceased; on Fridays (Town Market Shuttle Service) — Objective: weekly shopping and socialisation, in support of mobility within the municipal territory.

## 1. Purpose of the Project

The Service does not constitute a scheduled public transport service or a commercial activity, but a free social-assistance intervention reserved for users identified by the Social Services.

The project aims to combat the social isolation of the elderly and persons with disabilities, guaranteeing them autonomy in essential journeys.

## 2. Beneficiaries of the Service

- Resident citizens over 65 years of age.
- Persons with disabilities (able to board/alight from the vehicle autonomously or accompanied).
- Citizens without a family network or their own means of transport.

## 3. Operational Organisation

The service will be active two days a week, strategically chosen to meet the community's needs:

- Wednesday (Cemetery Shuttle Service): Objective: visiting the deceased
- Friday (Town Market Shuttle Service): Objective: weekly shopping and socialisation.

Indicative hours: from 9.00 to 13.00

## 4. Planned stops

**Cemetery Shuttle Service**

1. Via Montegrappa (municipal sports field)
2. Piazza Santa Maria di Gesù
3. Via Isnello (junction)
4. Piazza Mazzini
5. Piazza Zubbio
6. Piazza Quattro Cannoli
7. Piazza Garibaldi
8. Piazza Rosario Gallo

**Municipal Market Shuttle Service**

1. Piazza San Calogero
2. Piazza Rosario Gallo

6. Piazza Zubbio

9. Viale delle Fiere

## 5. Methods of submitting applications

For the purpose of accessing the benefit, applicants possessing the above requirements must submit an application using the dedicated forms available at the Social Services offices of the Municipality of Collesano and on the institutional website, to be delivered by hand to the Municipality's protocol Office or to the following certified email address (PEC): protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it

## 6. Contacts

For information on the public notice, the Social Services Office can be contacted on the following numbers: 0921/661158 – 0921 782305.
