# COMUNE DI COLLESANO

Città Metropolitana di Palermo

## READING PACT

PREAMBLE

Reading constitutes a fundamental instrument for the cultural, social and economic development of the community; it fosters personal growth, the exercise of conscious citizenship, intercultural dialogue and social cohesion. Law 13 February 2020, n. 15 "Provisions for the promotion and support of reading" recognises Reading Pacts as permanent instruments of collaboration between public institutions and private entities aimed at the diffusion of books and reading. The Centro per il Libro e la Lettura (CEPELL), an autonomous institute of the Ministry of Culture, promotes Reading Pacts within the national programme "Città che legge" [City that Reads]. The participating municipalities of the Basse Madonie, the school institutions, the Diocese, the libraries, the cultural associations and the other signatories share the conviction that books and reading represent a common good and a citizenship right to be promoted through coordinated and continuous actions. All this being premised, this Intermunicipal Reading Pact of the Basse Madonie is agreed and entered into.

**ART. 1 – PURPOSES**

The Pact pursues the following purposes:

a) to promote reading as a widespread practice accessible to all citizens;
b) to foster the pleasure of reading from early childhood;
c) to combat educational and cultural poverty;
d) to support the growth of linguistic and critical skills;
e) to enhance libraries, schools, archives, bookshops and places of culture;
f) to foster the active participation of the community through shared initiatives;
g) to create a stable network of collaboration between the entities of the territory.

**ART. 2 – PARTICIPATING ENTITIES**

The following may join this Pact:

✓ School institutions;
✓ Public and school libraries;
✓ Diocese, parishes and ecclesial bodies;
✓ Cultural and social-promotion associations;
✓ Foundations and cultural institutions;
✓ Bookshops, publishers and operators of the sector;
✓ Public and private bodies interested in the promotion of reading;
✓ Other entities that share the purposes of this Pact.

Membership is free of charge and open.

**ART. 3 – COMMITMENTS OF THE SIGNATORIES**

The participating entities undertake to:

✓ collaborate in the realisation of the activities provided for by the Pact;
✓ share information, projects and good practices;
✓ foster the participation of citizens in the initiatives promoted;
✓ identify opportunities for collaboration and co-design;
✓ promote the Pact through their own institutional and associative channels.

**ART. 4 – PRIORITY ACTIONS**

The signatories identify, among the priority actions:

✓ territorial literary festivals and series;
✓ meetings with authors and illustrators;
✓ reading groups;
✓ reading-promotion activities in schools;
✓ read-aloud initiatives;
✓ projects dedicated to early childhood;
✓ enhancement of municipal and school libraries;
✓ activities addressed to the elderly and to the fragile segments of the population;
✓ initiatives in places of social and religious gathering;
✓ participation in regional, national and European calls concerning the promotion of reading.

**ART. 5 – COORDINATION TABLE**

The Coordination Table of the Pact is established, composed of one representative for each member and/or representatives of the other signatories. The Table:

✓ plans the annual activities;
✓ fosters coordination between the participating entities;
✓ monitors the state of implementation of the Pact;
✓ promotes participation in calls and funding opportunities;
✓ handles the tasks necessary for obtaining and subsequently maintaining the "Città che legge" qualification.

**ART. 6 – SUBSEQUENT MEMBERSHIPS**

This Pact remains open to the membership of further public and private entities that share its principles and purposes. Membership takes place by signing a specific declaration of membership.

**ART. 7 – DURATION**

This Pact has a duration of three years from the date of its signing. On expiry it is automatically renewed unless the signatories decide otherwise.

**ART. 8 – FINAL PROVISIONS**

The signing of this Pact does not entail direct financial obligations for the participating entities. The activities will be carried out through collaboration between the signatories and through any resources deriving from public or private funding.

Read, approved and signed.
