## Regione Siciliana – Presidency

## CIVIL PROTECTION DEPARTMENT

The Director General

Prot. [filing number] n. 33956 /DG/ DRPC Sicilia of 13 July 2026

## ADDRESSES ATTACHED

## SUBJECT: Extraordinary civil protection communiqué — Pre-alert for the forecast of a marked increase in temperatures with risk of heat waves and the ignition of fires.

The first significant heat wave is forecast for the Sicilian region starting from Wednesday 15 July, characterised by the sub-tropical Algerian anticyclone, with very high temperatures persisting until Sunday, beginning in the western sectors (Trapani area) and extending to the central-eastern sectors where the maximum values will occur, locally reaching even 42-44 degrees.

The presence on the land of large quantities of biomass will raise the capacity for the spread of fires, especially in interface areas.

## Recommendations:

Considering that this is the first significant heat wave over the island, pending the issue of the daily regional civil protection notices, although extreme situations are not forecast, it is considered necessary to carry out a preventive alert of all the components of the regional civil protection system and, in particular, of the operational structures, in order to allow everyone adequate preparation for the possible occurrence of more severe events deriving from interface/urban and forest fires as well as from heat waves.

The Mayors, in their capacity as the local civil protection authority, are therefore invited to implement, making use of their own municipal civil protection structure, the local police, the volunteer organisations and the competent operational structures, everything provided for in their own Municipal Civil Protection Plans and, in particular, to:

- verify the full functionality of the C.O.C. [municipal operations centre], of the hardware and communication tools, of the equipment and vehicles and of their own air-conditioned buildings, updating the emergency telephone lists, ensuring the preparation of the staff charged with carrying out standby or emergency-management activities, scheduling their shifts especially in the hours with statistically the highest frequency of emergency events.
- verify the accessibility and functionality of the water supply points for firefighting vehicles:
- monitor the evolution of the phenomena and communicate, in the most critical phases, with the SOUR [regional unified operations room], the Prefectures, the Metropolitan Cities, the Free Consortia, the Fire Brigade commands and the Forestry Corps.
- Identify in advance (where not already done) the most fragile persons (the elderly, people with debilitating chronic illnesses, pregnant women and newborns, the bedridden) and provide or prepare adequate assistance services;
- verify the availability of air-conditioned spaces (climate shelters) to receive the most fragile citizens in the hottest hours;
- prepare for the possible distribution of small bottles of water in cases of necessity;
- pay particular attention to all fire-risk situations on the territory, in particular along urban and extra-urban roads close to land with large quantities of biomass at risk of combustion, parks and reserve areas, also in order to be able to promptly guard the sites and prohibit passage and use in the event of imminent dangers for the population.
- Assess the possible suspension of activities entailing direct or indirect risks for the population.

The Mayors are therefore invited to bring to the knowledge of the population, by suitable means, the risks of heat waves and fire on the days indicated above and the following main recommendations:

- Suspend the highest-risk outdoor activities; in any case avoid remaining outdoors in the hottest hours of the day;
- for fragile persons, in the hottest hours, remain in air-conditioned premises and, if identified, in the "climate shelters";
- secure goods, materials, equipment and vehicles in areas at risk of fire;
- report any emergency situation to no. 112 or, for fires, to 115 or 1515 or, for heat waves, to no. 1500.

Finally, it is requested that receipt of this extraordinary communiqué be confirmed to the SOUR with the names and contact details of:

- the Municipal Civil Protection Manager
- the Commander of the Local Police
- the Presidents of the pre-alerted or activated volunteer organisations (OOdV).

The Volunteer Organisations (OOdV) are pre-alerted as of now and invited to promptly verify the functionality and efficiency of their vehicles, to make contact with the Municipality and to act in coordination with the DRPC (provincial directors and NOPI), referring to the Mayor as the local civil protection authority.

The main activities of each structure forming part of the national and regional civil protection system remain unchanged, within their competence, as also summarily indicated in the daily regional civil protection notice for fire and heat-wave risk, namely:

a) implement the OPERATIONAL PHASES contemplated in their own civil protection and emergency documents and plans in relation to the ALERT LEVELS;
b) assess the activities to be carried out, including preventive ones, and the measures to be adopted, in relation to the risks due to high temperatures (heat waves and fires)
c) constantly verify the organisation and the activation capacity of their own alert and emergency management system;
d) promptly disseminate the notice to their own structures;
e) constantly inform the operations rooms about the evolution of the situation.
