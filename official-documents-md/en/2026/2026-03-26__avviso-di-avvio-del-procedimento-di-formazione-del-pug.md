**SUBJECT: Notice of the start of the procedure for the formation of the PUG (Piano Urbanistico Generale [General Urban Plan])**

*Sicilian Region – Regional Department of Territory and Environment – Decree of 7 July 2021. Guidelines for the drafting of the Municipal General Urban Plan, under art. 25 paragraph 7 of Regional Law 13 August 2020 n. 19, G.U.R.S. n°34 of 06/08/2021*

Pursuant to art. 26 of Regional Law 19/2020, which provides that the participation process must be started even before the design phase of the plan, thereby signifying the fundamental importance that the legislator wished to assign to this procedure,

## NOTICE IS GIVEN

that pursuant to Resolution n°39 of 25/03/2026 of the Municipal Executive concerning: "Direction act for the drafting of the PUG pursuant to art. 26 of Regional Law 19/2020", for the obligations under art. 26 of regional law 13 August 2020 n°19, the procedure for the formation of the PUG has been started, with attached directives

and, therefore,

## THOSE INTERESTED ARE INVITED

within a period of thirty days, to put forward proposals and formulate suggestions, which will be evaluated in the drafting of the Preliminary Document of the PUG, sending them by email to the following dedicated address: <u>ufficiopug@comune.collesano.pa.it</u>.

The Resolution of the Municipal Executive n°39 of 25/03/2026 and its attachments are attached to this notice.
