## COMUNE DI COLLESANO

# NOTICE

### FOR CITIZENS HOLDING A PAPER IDENTITY CARD

Decree-Law 26 June 2026, n. 108 (article 11) has been published which, as announced in recent weeks, redefines the terms of validity of paper identity cards, initially destined for total cessation by 3 August 2026. On the basis of the European regulations, a transitional regime differentiated by fields has been introduced and the introduction of a provisional paper document has been provided for.

Paper identity cards will therefore no longer expire all at once on 3 August 2026, as previously established: if the paper identity card has not yet expired, it remains valid, only within the national territory, within the limits of the provisions described below.

### Validity in existing contractual relationships

For contracts, both public and private, entered into by 3 August 2026 in which the parties were identified by means of a paper identity card, the document maintains its contractual validity until the natural expiry of the contract itself, limited to the obligations linked to that specific relationship.

### General use within the national territory (until 31 January 2027)

Pending the issue of the CIE (Carta d'Identità Elettronica, electronic identity card), unexpired paper documents may be used in Italy until 31 January 2027 exclusively for: the exercise of fundamental rights; access to health, social-security and insurance services; in relations with the Public Administration and with private entities providing public services (pursuant to art. 35 of D.P.R. n. 445/2000); the delivery of post and judicial acts, the withdrawal or deposit of money at banks and institutions providing financial or postal services.

### The paper "Provisional Document" (issuable until 31 December 2027)

In the event of justified urgency or temporary impossibility of issuing the CIE, the provision allows for the issue of a provisional paper identity document: we are awaiting the ministerial circular for operational guidance on the times and methods of issue. That document, when it is made available to municipalities for issue, may be issued until 31 December 2027 and will be valid for travel abroad. However, the border authorities of some foreign States might not recognise its validity. The provisional paper document will have a temporary validity of no more than 6 months and must obligatorily be returned to the municipal counters at the moment of collection or delivery of the definitive CIE. To plan a trip abroad, it is in any case recommended to obtain the CIE or a Passport.

The organisational arrangements to facilitate the replacement of paper identity cards remain valid; we invite the persons concerned to get in contact with the Ufficio Anagrafe [registry office], on the number 0921 664749, or the switchboard, on the number 091/661158, to make an appointment for the replacement of their document
