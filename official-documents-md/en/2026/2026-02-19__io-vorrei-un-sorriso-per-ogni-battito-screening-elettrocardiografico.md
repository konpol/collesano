# Document 1 — "PICCOLI BATTITI" poster

# ELECTROCARDIOGRAPHIC SCREENING

### Prevention of sudden death and early diagnosis of heart diseases, by Dott. Calogero Comparato

### director of the Paediatric Cardiology Unit (UOC), ARNAS CIVICO PALERMO

## SUNDAY 29 MARCH 2026, FROM 9 AM TO 1 PM, AT THE TOWN HALL OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO - VIA VITTORIO EMANUELE, 2

The initiative provides a free electrocardiographic screening, aimed at the prevention and **early diagnosis of possible heart conditions in paediatric age. The screening is addressed to a *maximum of 50 children aged 0 to 16*, who will be selected according to the order of arrival of the requests. Information can be requested at the Social Services office of the Municipality of Collesano, where the forms needed for the screening can also be collected**
