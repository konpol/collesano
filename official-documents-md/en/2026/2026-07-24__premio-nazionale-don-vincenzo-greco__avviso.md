# COMUNE DI SAN MAURO CASTELVERDE

## CITTÀ METROPOLITANA DI PALERMO

#### NATIONAL PRIZE

### "DON VINCENZO GRECO"

The Municipality of San Mauro Castelverde organises the fourth National Prize dedicated to its fellow citizen ***Don Vincenzo Greco***, in which artists of Sculpture and of Painting and Graphic Arts may compete.

Don Vincenzo Greco was a priest born in San Mauro Castelverde in 1597 who died in Rome in 1687. He completed his ecclesiastical studies in Monreale and at the age of 27 moved to Rome, where he was Rector of the Collegio di Propaganda Fide and subsequently Ordinary Confessor at the Church of San Silvestro in Capite and Beneficiary of Santa Maria Maggiore, where he is buried. He was also Chaplain of the Church of Santa Maria in Porto Salvo alla Marina in Palermo, Prior of the Sanctuary of Gibilmanna, Dean of the Chapter of the Cathedral of Cefalù and Honorary Citizen of Messina. To him is owed the arrival in San Mauro of a great many works of art of the Baroque age and beyond, kept in our innumerable churches.

All adult artists may take part in the competition, without distinction of sex, religion or political belief. Each artist may take part with a maximum of three works whose subject is "San Mauro Castelverde and the Madonie". The dimensions of sculptural works must not exceed cm 40 x 100, while paintings or graphic works must not exceed cm 70 x 100.

#### The established prizes will be:

- First prize € 1.000,00
- Second prize € 500,00
- Third prize € 200,00

The works, which will be displayed in a collective exhibition, must be sent or delivered by hand to the secretariat of the Municipality of San Mauro Castelverde [Piazza Municipio, n° 11] starting from 24.08.2026 and no later than 04.09.2026. Outward and return shipping costs are borne by the artist, and the postmark within the deadline will be taken as proof. The works must be accompanied by: the participation form attached to this notice, a brief presentation and the artistic curriculum of the author (containing biographical and residence details, and a telephone contact). Each work must have a title. The award ceremony will take place at the conclusion of the exhibition, which will be held by 31.10.2026. All artists will receive a certificate of participation. The winning works will remain the property of the Municipality of San Mauro Castelverde; all other works may be collected at the end of the exhibition. The jury will be composed of the Sindaco [mayor] or a delegated Assessore [member of the town board] who chairs it, and three experts in the art world appointed by the mayor. The judgement of the members of the jury is final.
