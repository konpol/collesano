# "GIORNI DA VIVERE" [Days to Live]

(Project code: PTXSU0018925013862NMTX)

Sector and area of intervention: Assistance / Adults and elderly in conditions of hardship

| Project duration | Service hours and days | Monthly allowance |
|---|---|---|
| 12 MONTHS | 25 hours per week over 5 days | 519,47 € |

## Advantages

✓ Recognition of a 15% reservation of places in public competitions;
✓ Tutoring period (up to 3 months) to facilitate entry into the world of work;
✓ Places reserved for Young People with Fewer Opportunities (GMO) - economic difficulties - ISEE equal to or below the threshold of 15.000 Euros.

Young people who have reached their eighteenth and not passed their twenty-eighth year of age at the date of submission of the application (28 years and 364 days) may take part in the selection. For all other requirements, consult articles 2 and 3 of the Call (pages 6 - 7 - 8).

## SUBMISSION OF APPLICATIONS
