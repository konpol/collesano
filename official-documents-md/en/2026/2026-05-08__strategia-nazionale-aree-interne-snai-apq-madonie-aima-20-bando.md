## ART. 1 - SUBJECT OF THE NOTICE

This Notice governs the methods of submission of candidatures, the access requirements, the selection procedures and the conditions of participation in the integrated pathway provided for by the AIMA 20 project "Food plan and dynamic mapping of land", called "GenerAzioni Madonie — Skills, enterprise and territory for the food system".

The project is addressed to 40 recipients and provides for activities of guidance, training, traineeship in enterprise and support for business creation, according to what is provided for by the reference public notice and the approved Executive Plan.

The pathway does not constitute an employment relationship, nor does it entail a promise or guarantee of employment, but is aimed at strengthening skills, professional autonomy, the capacity to build an entrepreneurial project and direct knowledge of business contexts.

## ART. 2 - PURPOSES OF THE PROJECT

The AIMA 20 project forms part of the framework of interventions of the Area Interna Madonie and pursues the purpose of strengthening local human capital, enhancing the recipients' skills and promoting pathways of working autonomy and self-entrepreneurship. The intervention is consistent with the objective of supporting active labour-policy pathways, with particular reference to sectors presenting growth prospects and to pathways of support for business creation and self-employment.

In this perspective, the project intends to offer participants a progressive and structured pathway, capable of combining basic training, practical experience in enterprise, specialist support and the development of entrepreneurial ideas linked to the territory, the food system, the enhancement of local resources and the opportunities connected with the production chains of the Madonie.

## ART. 3 - NUMBER OF AVAILABLE PLACES

The total number of available places is 40 trainees, distributed as follows for the guidance and training activities:

- 15 trainees for the Alte Madonie territorial area with reference to the Gangi venue;
- 15 trainees for the Basse Madonie territorial area with reference to the Castelbuono venue;
- 10 trainees for the Madonie Imerese territorial area with reference to the Caltavuturo venue.

The aforesaid venues constitute the reference points for the performance of the guidance and classroom-training activities.

The subsequent traineeship phase will be carried out at host enterprises identified by the implementing entity within the project's reference territory, on the basis of the characteristics of the participant's profile and of business availability.

The assignment of trainees to the guidance and training venues will take place according to the merit ranking list and any preferences expressed by the candidates, respecting the planned distribution of places.

Admission to the pathway will be ordered on the basis of the verification of the access requirements and the outcomes of the selection procedure.

Any replacements, scrolling of the ranking list or variations in the assignment of participants may be ordered exclusively within the limits and in the ways provided for by the legislation in force and in relation to the organisational needs of the project.

## ART. 4 - RECIPIENTS

Applications for participation may be submitted by persons who, at the date of submission of the candidature, possess the requirements provided for by the reference public notice. The recipients of the intervention are unemployed persons, beneficiaries of income-support instruments, workers at risk of unemployment, workers seeking new employment, as well as further categories provided for by the Notice, provided they possess the required territorial, age and educational requirements.

For unemployed persons, this condition must be proven by possession of the Dichiarazione di Immediata Disponibilità [declaration of immediate availability for work], as provided for by art. 19 of D.Lgs. n. 150/2015.

## ART. 5 — ADMISSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

To be admitted to the selection, candidates must be resident or domiciled in the territory of the Area Interna Madonie for at least six months at the moment of candidature, be aged between a minimum of 16 and a maximum of 56 completed years, have fulfilled the education and training obligations, and not have had, in the past, any employment relationship with the enterprise hosting the traineeship.

Non-EU citizens may also be recipients, provided they hold a long-term residence permit or asylum or subsidiary protection on Italian territory for at least twelve months, as provided for by the reference public notice.

The lack of even one of the required requirements entails the inadmissibility of the application or exclusion from the pathway, even at a later stage, if the absence of the requirement is ascertained after the start of the activities.

## ART. 6 - STRUCTURE OF THE PATHWAY

The project pathway is structured into four main actions, integrated with one another and functional to the achievement of the project's purposes: guidance and selection, training, traineeship in enterprise and support for business creation.

The first action concerns guidance and the definition of the training pathway aimed at the selection of participants. This phase provides for activities of presentation of the project on the territory, verification of the admissibility requirements, collection of documents, a structured guidance interview and the definition of the ranking list of those admitted according to objective and verifiable criteria.

The second action concerns training. According to the approved Executive Plan, the training pathway has a duration of 40 hours and is structured into modules on hygiene and safety at work pursuant to D.Lgs. n. 81/2008, computer literacy and English language. The Executive Plan also provides for an additional informational/training phase on the themes of the green and digital transition, aimed at raising participants' awareness of skills and strategic scenarios for the development of the territory.

The third action consists of a traineeship in enterprise lasting five months. The traineeship represents the operational heart of the pathway and allows trainees to engage with real productive contexts, acquire practical experience, understand business dynamics and develop greater awareness of their own prospects of professional and entrepreneurial autonomy.

The fourth action concerns support for business creation. This activity provides for targeted meetings and individual consultancy aimed at maturing the entrepreneurial idea, learning the basic notions of business activity, business culture, sources of financing, the formalisation of the idea and support for the start-up and management of the enterprise.

## ART. 7 - TRAINING AND VENUES

The training activities will take place at the authorised venues identified in the project, namely at the Castelbuono venue (Palazzo Failla), Via Garibaldi n. 67, at the Caltavuturo venue (Palazzo Bonomo), Piazza Papa Giovanni XXIII n.5, and at the Gangi venue, I.C. "F.P. Polizzano" G. Vazzano building, Via Castello n. 1/3.

The classes will be organised in coherence with the territorial distribution provided for by the project, with reference to the areas of the Basse Madonie, the Alte Madonie and the Madonie Imerese.

The activities will be scheduled by the implementing entity and communicated to the admitted trainees. Unless organisational needs dictate otherwise, the activities will be carried out in the afternoon, according to the calendar that will be made known to those concerned before the start of the pathway.

## ART. 8 - TRAINEESHIP IN ENTERPRISE

Trainees who have completed at least 70% of the training activity will be started on the traineeship phase in enterprise, as provided for by the Executive Plan.

The traineeship will last five months and will be carried out at enterprises that have signed a specific agreement with the proposing ATI. During the traineeship period, the communications to the competent bodies will be carried out, INAIL cover will be activated and a specific third-party civil liability policy will be guaranteed.

For each trainee there will be a mentoring activity by the host enterprise, through a company tutor, and a monitoring activity by a tutor identified by the proposing entity.

The traineeship constitutes a concrete and qualifying occasion to enter into relation with the world of enterprise, get to know organisational and productive processes from the inside, test one's own abilities and build the foundations for a possible pathway of self-enterprise or self-employment.

## ART. 9 - ALLOWANCES AND BENEFITS FOR TRAINEES

Participation in the project provides for the recognition of specific allowances, in compliance with the conditions provided for by the Notice and the applicable legislation.

For the training phase, an attendance allowance of 0,80 euros per hour/trainee of actual presence is provided, as per the Executive Plan.

For the traineeship phase, an allowance of 1.000,00 euros gross per month is provided for each trainee, for a total duration of five months. The Executive Plan in fact provides, for 40 trainees, an allowance cost of 1.000,00 euros for 5 months, for a total amount of 200.000,00 euros.

The payment of the allowances is subject to actual participation in the activities, regular registration of attendance and compliance with the minimum attendance thresholds provided. The recognition of the traineeship allowance represents one of the qualifying elements of the project, since it allows trainees to carry out a practical experience in enterprise for five months while benefiting, at the same time, from significant economic support during the pathway.

## ART. 10 - SUPPORT FOR BUSINESS CREATION

The business-creation support action is aimed at supporting trainees in the construction of a possible entrepreneurial pathway.

According to the Executive Plan, this action has a total duration of 40 hours and is structured into a first phase of collective meetings, lasting 20 hours, dedicated to themes such as the entrepreneur, legal notions, business culture, the birth of the entrepreneurial idea and sources of financing, and a second phase of individual consultancy, lasting 20 hours per beneficiary, aimed at drawing up a business project sheet and at supporting the formalisation of the idea, the start-up and the management of the enterprise.

This phase constitutes the concluding and strategic moment of the pathway: the trainee is not left alone in the definition of his/her own idea, but is accompanied with specialist support, concrete tools and targeted consultancy.

## ART. 11 - METHOD OF SUBMISSION OF THE APPLICATION

The application for participation must be drawn up exclusively using the model Annex A — Application for participation, duly completed in every part and signed by the candidate.

A copy of a valid identity document must be attached to the application. An updated curriculum vitae and any further documentation considered useful for the verification of the requirements and the evaluation of the candidature may also be attached.

The application must arrive no later than 23:59 on 22 May 2026.

Applications may be transmitted by ordinary email to the address segreteria@itsmadonie.it or by certified email to the address protocollo@pec.itsmadonie.it.

The subject of the communication must state the following wording: "Domanda di partecipazione — Progetto AIMA 20".

Applications arriving after the indicated deadline, lacking a signature, lacking an identity document or not complying with the provisions of this Notice may be declared inadmissible.

## ART. 12 - SELECTION PROCEDURE

The selection procedure will be structured into a first phase of verification of the admissibility requirements and of the documentation submitted and a subsequent phase of guidance interview.

The interview will be aimed at evaluating the candidate's motivation, availability to take part in the entire pathway, previous experience, personal aptitudes and the coherence of the profile with the project's purposes.

The selection will be carried out according to objective and verifiable criteria, in conformity with what is provided for by the Executive Plan, which identifies in the verification of the requirements, in the structured guidance interview and in the definition of the ranking list the essential steps of the procedure.

## ART. 13 - INFORMATIONAL AND GUIDANCE OPEN DAYS

In order to guarantee the widest dissemination of information relating to the project and to foster a correct knowledge of the opportunities offered, the implementing entity organises three informational and guidance open days in the territory of the Madonie.

The open days will take place in the week of 18 May 2026, according to the following indicative calendar:

- 18 May 2026 at 17:00 at the Caltavuturo venue (Madonie Imerese Territorial Area);
- 19 May 2026 at 15:30 at the Castelbuono venue (Basse Madonie Territorial Area);
- 20 May 2026 at 15:30 at the Gangi venue (Alte Madonie Territorial Area).

The open days represent moments of information and guidance addressed to candidates, aimed at presenting the project, its purposes, the methods of participation and the opportunities provided by the pathway.

In the course of the meetings, operational guidance may be provided for the correct completion of the application for participation and for understanding the phases of the pathway.

The times and any organisational details will be communicated to candidates through the institutional channels of the implementing entity.

The implementing entity reserves the right to modify the calendar, the venues or the manner of holding the open days for proven organisational or logistical needs, giving prompt notice to those concerned.

## ART. 14 - START OF THE ACTIVITIES

The start of the activities is planned from 3 June 2026.

The activities will be organised according to the approved schedule and the calendar drawn up by the implementing entity. The project, according to the Executive Plan, provides for its conclusion no later than 30 November 2026.

Admitted trainees will be required to respect the communicated calendar, to sign the attendance registers and to take part regularly in the planned activities.

## ART. 15 - OBLIGATIONS OF THE TRAINEES

Trainees admitted to the project are required to take part continuously and punctually in the planned activities, to respect the organisational provisions communicated by the implementing entity, to sign the required documentation, to complete any sheets, registers or monitoring tools and to promptly communicate any variation relating to the data declared at candidature.

The minimum attendance required for access to the subsequent phases of the pathway and for the recognition of the allowances is at least 70% of the planned activities, as established by the reference public notice and the Executive Plan.

Failure to comply with the attendance obligations, unjustified abandonment of the pathway or loss of the requirements may entail exclusion from the project and non-payment of the planned allowances.

## ART. 16 - CERTIFICATIONS AND ATTESTATIONS

At the end of the activities, the attestations and certifications provided for by the project will be issued, in relation to the modules attended, the tests taken and the activities actually carried out.

The Executive Plan provides for certifications relating to the computer, English language and safety modules, in coherence with the structure of the approved training pathway.

## ART. 17 - GROUNDS FOR EXCLUSION

The following constitute grounds for exclusion from the selection procedure or from the pathway, where ascertained even after admission: the lack of the required requirements, the submission of untruthful declarations, the failure to sign the application, the absence of the identity document, the submission of the application after the deadlines, the failure to take part in the selection, as well as any other condition incompatible with participation in the project.

The implementing entity reserves the right to carry out checks, including sample checks, on the truthfulness of the declarations made by candidates pursuant to D.P.R. n. 445/2000.

## ART. 18 - PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

The personal data provided by candidates will be processed in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and D.Lgs. n. 196/2003 as amended and supplemented, exclusively for the purposes connected with the management of the selection procedure, the realisation of the project, and administrative, management, monitoring, control and reporting obligations.

The submission of the application entails acknowledgement of the privacy notice and authorisation to process the data for the purposes indicated above.

## ART. 19 - PUBLICITY AND COMMUNICATIONS

This Notice, Annex A and any communications relating to the selection will be made available through the institutional channels of the implementing entity and/or of the bodies involved. Communications to candidates may be made by email, publication on the indicated website, telephone contact or other means suitable for guaranteeing knowledge of the information.

It is the candidate's responsibility to indicate correct contact details and to consult the indicated communication channels regularly.
