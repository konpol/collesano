# SO.SVI.MA SpA

*Administrative Office: Viale Risorgimento 13B Castellana Sicula (PA)* *Tel 0921/563005 – Fax 0921/563006 – Website:* <u>www.sosvima.com</u> *E-mail* <u>sosvima@gmail.com</u>*; sosvima.agenzia@pec.it*

<u>Public notice for the selection of 1 expert for the award of self-employment engagements</u>

Public notice for the selection of 1 expert for the award of self-employment engagements under art. 7, paragraph 6 of d.lgs. n. 165/2001, for the performance of support activities for the Company.

## Art. 1 – Subject of the engagements and professional requirements

1.1 This Notice, accompanied by Annexes 1 - "Criteria for the evaluation of experts", 2 - "Application for participation", 3 – "Description of the required professional profiles", governs the selection procedures, the award methods and the conditions of the engagements that SO.SVI.MA. Spa *(www.sosvima.com)* intends to confer on an expert in possession of qualified skills and proven experience of a technical/administrative type. <u>The engagement will have as its subject the following activities:</u>

a) Technical support for the performance of the activities connected with the management of the municipal Technical Office.

1.2 The engagement referred to in point 1.1 above relates to a single profile, whose professional and experience requirement is indicated in the document "Description of the required professional profiles": <u>profile:</u> Expert in possession of a degree in engineering who has carried out activity in the field.

## Art. 2 – Participation requirements

2.1. To take part in the selection procedure, candidates must possess, for the chosen profile(s), on pain of exclusion, the educational qualification, the number of years of proven experience and the required skills referred to in the document "Description of the required professional profiles" (Annex 3), as well as the following requirements:

a. possession of Italian citizenship or that of one of the Member States of the European Union;
b. enjoyment of civil and political rights;
c. excellent knowledge of the Italian language;
d. no final criminal convictions and not being the recipient of measures concerning the application of prevention measures, civil decisions or administrative measures entered in the judicial record (even if amnesty, remission, pardon or judicial pardon has been granted);
e. not being subject to ongoing criminal proceedings for offences of particular gravity which, according to the rules in force, exclude the establishment of the employment relationship.

Possession of the required requirements and skills must be declared by the candidate in the "Application for participation" (Annex 2 to this Notice), with an indication of the number of years of experience gained with reference to the skills required for the professional profile applied for, as per the cited document "Description of the required professional profiles" (Annex 3).

2.2 Pursuant to art. 38 of d.lgs. n. 165/2001 as amended and supplemented, the selection procedure is also open to family members of citizens of the Member States of the European Union not having the citizenship of a Member State who hold the right of residence or the right of permanent residence, or citizens of Third Countries who hold the EC residence permit for long-term residents or who hold refugee status or subsidiary protection status, in accordance with the legislation in force.

2.3. All the prescribed requirements must be possessed at the expiry date of the deadline for the submission of the application for participation and must also persist at the moment of the possible start of the working relationship.

2.4. The submission of the application for participation amounts to full acceptance of the conditions set out in it, full awareness of the self-employed nature of the working relationship, as well as knowledge and acceptance of the rules, conditions and prescriptions laid down in this Notice.

2.5. This Notice and the subsequent selection do not in any way commit SO.SVI.MA. spa to conferring the professional engagements; the Company reserves the right, at its unquestionable judgement, to suspend or revoke the selection at any moment, giving notice on its website (www.sosvima.com), without the candidates being able to claim any right. A positive outcome of the selection and a useful placement in the ranking list do not in any way generate an obligation to confer the engagement on the part of the Company.

2.6. The procedure is given suitable publicity on the Company's website (www.sosvima.com).

## Art. 3 – Submission of the candidature, content of the application and submission deadlines

3.1. The application for participation in the selection, made pursuant to d.P.R. n. 445/2000, is drawn up using the template in the document "Application for participation" (Annex 2 to this Notice), leaving the contents unaltered even where not applicable to the candidate. In the application, drawn up in Italian, referring specifically to the procedure in which one intends to take part, the candidate must declare under his/her own responsibility, pursuant to and in the ways provided for by the cited d.P.R. n. 445/2000 as amended and supplemented:

a. first name and surname;
b. date and place of birth and codice fiscale [personal tax code];
c. citizenship;
d. municipality of residence and address;
e. the professional profile(s) in the document "Description of the professional profiles" for which he/she is competing (Annex 3);
f. possession of the educational qualification required for the professional profile(s);
g. knowledge of the main software packages, according to what/where indicated in the document "Description of the professional profiles" (Annex 3).

3.2. On pain of exclusion, the application for participation must:

a. be signed on the last page with a full and legible signature;
b. be scanned in pdf format in a single file;
c. be addressed to SO.SVI.MA. spa, at the following certified email address: <u>sosvima.agenzia@pec.it</u>, or at the ordinary email address <u>sosvima@gmail.com</u>. The subject of the PEC or email must state the surname and first name of the candidate as well as the professional profile(s) for which the application for participation is submitted; in the event of transmission of the application for participation to the ordinary email address, the Administration sends the candidate a communication of receipt. This acknowledgement does not take place in the event of transmission of the application to the certified email address, since the PEC as such fulfils the proof of delivery.
d. **arrive by 12,00 on 22 June 2026.** For the purposes of admissibility, the time of receipt of the application for participation at the aforesaid certified or ordinary email box of the Company shall be taken as proof;
e. be accompanied by the following attachments: 1) curriculum vitae drawn up according to the European format for the curriculum vitae, scanned in pdf format and in a single file, from which emerge the professional experiences and their duration, as well as all the elements useful for the evaluation of the skills required for each professional profile declared by the candidate in the "Application for participation" (Annex 2 to the Notice). The curriculum vitae must be drawn up, on pain of exclusion, in Italian, dated and signed on the last page with a full and legible signature. At the bottom of the curriculum, the following declaration must be included and signed: "The undersigned, aware that – pursuant to art. 76 of D.P.R. 445/2000 – untruthful declarations, falsity in acts and the use of false acts are punished under the criminal code and special laws, declares that the information corresponds to the truth. The undersigned, with regard to the processing of personal data, expresses his/her consent to their processing in compliance with the purposes and methods of D. Lgs. n. 196/2003"; 2) copy of a valid identity document of the signatory, scanned in pdf format.

3.3. For the purposes of the evaluation of qualifications, where the candidate possesses post-university qualifications (research doctorate, specialisation and advanced training courses of no less than two years, master's) for the profile(s) for which he/she takes part in the selection, he/she may attest possession of the qualifications with a substitute declaration of certification and state their details within the application.

3.4 The absence of an attachment, as well as the non-conformity of the application for participation or of an attachment with the required characteristics, entails the exclusion of the application.

3.5. If the candidate intends to submit an application for participation for several profiles, the application must be, on pain of exclusion, a single one and must be forwarded through a single dispatch.

3.6. Applications and their attachments arriving after the deadline set for their receipt will not be taken into consideration. The Company is not responsible for any delays and/or mishaps, whatever their cause or in any case attributable to the act of third parties, to fortuitous event or force majeure, in relation to applications and/or documents arriving after the aforesaid deadline.

3.7. The ordinary email or PEC address at which the candidate intends to receive communications for the purposes of the procedure is the one indicated in the application for participation. Any variation of it must be communicated promptly to the address: <u>sosvima.agenzia@pec.it</u> or <u>sosvima@gmail.com</u>.

3.8. The Procedure Manager verifies compliance with the methods of transmission of the applications.

## Art. 4 – Evaluation procedure

4.1. The verification of possession of the participation requirements and the evaluation of the candidatures is carried out by a specific Commission, composed of three members.

4.2. The Commission proceeds with the evaluation of the candidates through the following phases:

A. admission of the competitors and verification of the participation requirements. The Commission, having ascertained that the applications for participation have arrived within the peremptory deadlines provided for in this notice and in the prescribed ways, will proceed to: a) examine the content of the applications for participation; b) admit to the subsequent evaluation phase the candidates in possession of the participation requirements;
B. evaluation of the requirements valid for the purposes of assigning the score (qualifications, experience) on the basis of the criteria indicated in the document "Criteria for the evaluation of experts" (Annex 1 to this Notice). The assignment of a maximum score of 30 points is provided for;
C. a final individual acquaintance interview aimed at evaluating the skills required for each professional profile and the aptitude of the candidate in relation to the subject of the engagement. The assignment of a maximum score of 20 points is provided for. Consequently, the maximum total attributable score (phase B + C) is equal to 50 points.

4.3. With regard to phase B referred to in point 4.2 above, the Commission proceeds separately in the evaluation of the different profiles in the document "Description of the required professional profiles" (Annex 3) and draws up, for each professional profile, the merit ranking list which will be published on the Company's website with the value of notification for all legal purposes.

4.4. According to the order of the merit ranking list, a number of candidates equal to three times the positions required for each specific profile will be invited to the interview of phase C referred to in point 4.2 above; this limit may be exceeded in the case of candidates who have obtained equal scores. The interview will in any case take place even in the presence of a number of candidates lower than three times the positions required for each specific profile.

4.5. The candidates admitted to the interview will be summoned by communication to the ordinary email or PEC address indicated in the application for participation. The individual interviews will take place at the Company's offices, located in Viale Risorgimento 13/B. The list of candidates invited to the interview will be published on the Company's website <u>www.sosvima.com</u>.

4.6. At the end of phase C referred to in point 4.2 above, the Commission draws up for each professional profile the final ranking list, adding together the scores of phases B and C. The ranking lists will be published on the Company's website <u>www.sosvima.com</u>. This publication has the value of notification for all legal purposes. The ranking lists will have a duration of 24 months from the approval of the selection acts by the Administration.

4.7. A positive outcome of the selection and a useful placement in the ranking list do not in any way generate an obligation to confer the engagement on the part of the Company.

4.8. The Company also reserves the right, at its unquestionable judgement, to suspend or revoke the selection procedure at any moment, through a communication on its institutional website, without those selected being able to claim any right.

## Art. 5 – Evaluation criteria and assignment of scores

5.1. In the evaluation of the requirements valid for the purposes of assigning the score (phase B referred to in point 4.2 above), the Commission takes into account the criteria set out below and indicated for each profile in the document "Criteria for the evaluation of experts" (Annex 1 to the Notice):

a. Qualifications: educational qualifications and specialist training (university, post-university);
b. Skills and professional experience: proven experience in relation to the specific skills gained at public administrations.

5.2 The experience must be attested and will be evaluated according to its duration; professional experiences of less than 30 days are not taken into consideration.

## Art. 6 – Admissibility to evaluation and exclusions

6.1 The merit ranking list will be drawn up on the basis of the list of candidates who take part; it will have a validity of 24 months.

## Art. 7 – Conferral, duration and economic treatment of the engagement

7.1 The Administration carries out checks pursuant to and in the ways of art. 71 of d.P.R. n. 445/2000 as amended and supplemented concerning the truthfulness of the declarations made in the application, also for the purposes and effects of art. 76 of the same D.P.R. Untruthful declarations and the formation or use of false acts will be punished pursuant to art. 483 of the Criminal Code and will entail forfeiture of the engagement.

7.2 In the event of conferral of the engagement, the signing of the contract is subject to the submission by the selected candidate of a declaration made pursuant to and for the purposes of d.P.R. n. 445/2000 as amended and supplemented concerning, among other things, the absence of situations of conflict of interest and incompatibility. Furthermore, in the cases provided for by the legislation in force on public employment, where the winning candidate is an employee of the public administration, the engagement, pursuant to art. 53, paragraph 8, of d.lgs. n. 165/2001, may not be conferred without the prior authorisation of the administration of affiliation.

7.3 The engagement, to be carried out mainly at the Company's offices and at the offices of the Municipality of Collesano in relation to the needs of the Company itself, will be awarded for a total number of hours varying between approximately 800 and 900 hours, for a **taxable hourly cost (inclusive of the professional fund) per hour worked of € 20,00 (twenty/00), plus VAT if due.**

7.4 The signing of the contracts and their effectiveness are subject to the rules in force on the publicity of engagements.
