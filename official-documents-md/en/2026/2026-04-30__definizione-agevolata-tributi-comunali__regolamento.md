# COMUNE DI COLLESANO — Province of Palermo

## REGULATIONS FOR THE FACILITATED SETTLEMENT (DEFINIZIONE AGEVOLATA) OF THE REVENUES

## OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO PURSUANT TO PARAGRAPHS 102 TO 109 OF ART. 1

### Article 1 - Subject of the regulations

1. These regulations, adopted within the power provided for by art. 52 of legislative decree 15 November 1997, n. 446 and in implementation of art. 1, paragraphs 102 and 109, of law 30 December 2025, n. 199, govern the application of the facilitated settlement of the municipal tax and patrimonial revenues indicated in the following article 2, not collected following the notification of: -enforceable assessment notices, pursuant to art. 1, paragraph 792, of law 27 December 2019, n. 160, issued by the Municipality; -tax injunctions, under Royal Decree 14 April 1910, n. 639, issued by the Municipality. -charges entrusted to the Collection Agent (Agenzia delle Entrate-Riscossione) pursuant to D.P.R. 29 September 1973, n. 602.

### Article 2 - Subject of the facilitated settlement

1. The revenues that may be subject to the facilitated settlement according to the methods and deadlines governed by these regulations are the following:

- Municipal property tax (IMU) – (TASI)¹
- Waste tax (TARI)²

2. The revenues indicated in paragraph 1 above are settleable, also with reference to a single debt composed of principal, penalties and/or interest, where demanded by means of the following collection titles: -assessment notices under art. 1, paragraph 792, of law 27 December 2019, n. 160, issued by the Municipality and notified by 31/12/2025; -**charges entrusted by the Municipality of Collesano to the Collection Agent up to the date of 31 December 2023, in implementation of the provisions of art. 1, paragraphs 102 et seq., of Law 30 December 2025, n. 199.**

3. The debts referred to in the previous paragraph may be extinguished by payment, to be made in the manner and within the deadlines of the following article 5: -of the principal of the revenue subject to settlement; -of the notification expenses and those for the enforcement and precautionary procedures accrued up to the date of submission of the settlement declaration;

-**of the sums accrued by way of reimbursement of the expenses for the precautionary and enforcement procedures, as well as of the notification expenses of the payment notices notified by ADER;**

-of the collection charges accrued up to the date of submission of the settlement declaration; -of the further sums provided for in paragraph 4 of this article, where not entirely cancelled under these regulations.

4. Following the completion of the facilitated settlement, the following sums relating to the debts subject to settlement are not due: ☐ administrative tax penalties, to the extent of 100% ☐ interest assessed in the assessment phase, to the extent of 100%; ☐ default interest accrued after the expiry of the assessment notice **or of the payment notice**, to the extent of 100% ☐ sums due by way of six-monthly surcharge pursuant to art. 27 of law 24 November 1981, n. 689, to the extent of 100%;

5. Following the completion of the facilitated settlement referred to in the previous paragraph, the sums indicated in paragraph 4 above are extinguished.

6. Debts deriving from judgments of conviction of the Corte dei conti [Court of Auditors] are excluded from the facilitated settlement.

### Article 3 - Procedure for the facilitated settlement

**1.** The debtor manifests to the Municipality, by the deadline of 31/07/2026, the intention to make use of the facilitated settlement under these regulations by making a specific declaration, in the manner and in conformity with the forms that the Municipality itself makes available on its own *internet* site within 20 days of the approval of these regulations.

**2.** In the declaration referred to in the previous paragraph, the debtor indicates the number of instalments with which he intends to make the payment, based on the provisions of art. 5 of these regulations, as well as the pendency of any judicial proceedings concerning the debts to which the application refers, undertaking to renounce them, with set-off of the litigation costs.

**3.** Failure by the debtor to file the application renouncing the appeals entails the ineffectiveness of the facilitated settlement with reference to the debts subject to litigation, and the payments made are acquired as an advance on the total amount due.

**4.** The Municipality, by the deadline of 31/08/2026, communicates to the debtors who have submitted the declaration referred to in paragraph 1 their admission to the facilitated settlement, specifying the total amount of the sums due for the purposes of the settlement, as well as that of the individual instalments and the deadline of each of them, or the refusal of the facilitated settlement, in whole or in part.

**5.** For the purposes of determining the amount of the sums to be paid, account is taken exclusively of the amounts already paid by way of principal and by way of reimbursement of the expenses for the enforcement procedures and notification of the titles involved in the settlement. Any sums paid for the amounts subject to cancellation with the facilitated settlement are definitively acquired by the municipality and cannot be refunded.

### Article 4 - Effects of the declaration

**1.** Following the submission of the declaration referred to in art. 3:

**a.** the payment obligations deriving from previous deferrals existing at the date of submission of the declaration are suspended until the expiry of the first or single instalment of the sums due by way of settlement;
**b.** no new administrative vehicle stops (fermi amministrativi) or mortgages may be registered, without prejudice to those already registered at the date of submission of the declaration;
**c.** no new enforcement procedures may be started;
**d.** enforcement procedures previously started may not be continued, unless the first auction with a positive outcome has already been held.

### Article 5 - Payment of the amounts due

**1.** Payment of the sums referred to in article 2 above is made in a single instalment by the deadline of 31/10/2026, or in the number of instalments chosen on the basis of the options provided for in the following paragraph, the first by the aforesaid deadline and the subsequent ones at the frequency indicated for the respective amount bands.

**2.** The communication containing the instalment payment plan must be transmitted in the manner indicated in the declaration.

**3.** The debtor, in the case of payment by instalments, indicates in the facilitated-settlement application the number of instalments chosen from among the following options: ☐ for total amounts due up to 1.000 euros: up to 10 monthly instalments with a minimum instalment of € 100,00 ☐ for total amounts due over 1.000 up to 5.000 euros: up to 18 monthly instalments with a minimum instalment of € 100,00 ☐ **for total amounts due over 5.000 up to 15.000 euros: up to 10 two-monthly instalments** ☐ **for total amounts due over 15.000 up to 25.000 euros up to 10 two-monthly instalments** ☐ for amounts over 25.000 up to 50.000 euros: up to 16 two-monthly instalments ☐ for amounts over 50.000 euros: up to 20 two-monthly instalments.

**4.** In the case of payment by instalments, interest at the rate of 2% applies, starting from the deadline provided for the first instalment and taking into account the interest of the pre-deferral period.

**5.** Payment of the sums due for the settlement is made according to the specific methods provided for the relevant revenue.

### Article 6 - Completion of the facilitated settlement

1. In the event of non-payment, or insufficient or late payment, of the single instalment or of one of those into which the payment of the sums has been deferred, the settlement produces no effects and the compulsory collection activities for the recovery of the sums covered by the application resume. In this case, the payments made are acquired as an advance on the total amount due and do not determine the extinction of the residual debt.

2. If the debtor, as a result of previous partial payments, has already paid in full what is due, as determined under these regulations, in order to benefit from the effects of the facilitated settlement he must nevertheless manifest the intention to join it in the manner provided for by art. 3.

3. Sums relating to settleable debts, paid for any reason, even before the settlement, remain definitively acquired and are not refundable.

4. Payment of the first or single instalment of the sums due by way of facilitated settlement determines: -the extinction of the enforcement procedures previously started, unless the first auction with a positive outcome has already been held; -the suspension of the registered administrative vehicle stop;

### Article 7 - Facilitated settlement in the case of existing instalment plans or deferrals

**1.** The settlement option provided for by these regulations may also be exercised by debtors who have already partially paid, including following deferral measures issued by the Municipality, the sums due in relation to the enforceable assessments and/or the payment injunctions referred to in art. 1 of these regulations. In this case, to determine the amount of the sums due under art. 2 of these regulations, no account is taken of the amounts already paid by way of penalties and interest, including deferral interest, which remain definitively acquired and are not refundable. Sums relating to settleable debts, paid for any reason, even before the settlement, remain definitively acquired and are not refundable.

**2.** Payment of the first or single instalment of the sums due for the purposes of the facilitated settlement determines, limited to the debts subject to settlement, the automatic revocation of any previously granted deferral still in existence.

### Article 8 - Entry into force and effectiveness of the regulations

**1.** These regulations enter into force once the deadlines of art. 124 of legislative decree 19 August 2000, n. 267 have elapsed (unless immediate enforceability is declared pursuant to the same article);

**2.** These regulations acquire effectiveness with publication on the institutional website of the Municipality, pursuant to art. 1 paragraph 108 of law 30 December 2025, n. 199.
