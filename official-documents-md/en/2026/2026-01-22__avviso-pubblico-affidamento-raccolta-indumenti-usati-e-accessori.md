# MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO — Metropolitan City of Palermo

## ADMINISTRATIVE AND SOCIAL-ASSISTANCE AREA — ENVIRONMENT OFFICE

# PUBLIC NOTICE

OPEN TO ALL ECONOMIC OPERATORS SPECIALISED IN THE SECTOR, AIMED AT ACQUIRING EXPRESSIONS OF INTEREST FOR THE ASSIGNMENT OF THE SERVICE OF COLLECTION, TRANSPORT AND RECOVERY OF USED CLOTHING AND CLOTHING ACCESSORIES (CER Codes 20.01.10 — 20.01.11) FOR THE YEAR 2026.

## NOTICE IS GIVEN THAT

The Municipality of COLLESANO, in order to actively promote separate waste collection, in compliance with the provisions of D.Lgs. 3 April 2006, n. 152 as amended and L.R. 9/2010, intends to collect, respecting the principles of transparency and competition, expressions of interest from suitable Economic Operators (hereinafter EO) for the service of COLLECTION AND RECOVERY OF USED CLOTHES (CER CODE 20.01.10 - CLOTHING AND CER CODE 20.01.11 — TEXTILE PRODUCTS).

## 2) PURPOSE

This notice, to be understood as a preliminary exploration for a market survey, is aimed at receiving expressions of interest, not binding for the Entity, for the possible subsequent invitation to the procedure for the assignment of the services indicated above, favouring the maximum participation of qualified economic Operators, respecting the principles of non-discrimination, equal treatment and transparency.

## 3) OBLIGATIONS OF THE ASSIGNEE

The service covered by this notice will consist of the collection of used clothing, shoes and accessories, with the following methods and obligations:

- Coordination and supervision of the organisational aspects of the initiative;
- Positioning of mobile units (vans or lorries) duly authorised for the correct delivery/collection and transport of the waste covered by this notice, by specialised operators, and the routing of the same for recovery at plants authorised pursuant to art. 216 D. Lgs 152/06 as amended and D.M. n. 186/06 as amended;
- Monthly service frequency, agreed with the Entity's service managers;
- Realisation of an awareness campaign on the subject of the collection of textile and post-consumer clothing;
- Collection of the material covered by the expression of interest at any CCR [municipal collection centre] that may be built in the municipal territory, coming from the collection carried out by the company assigned the related hygiene service or from spontaneous delivery by users;

## 4) METHODS AND DEADLINES FOR SUBMITTING THE EXPRESSION OF INTEREST

Proposals must be received by no later than **12.00 on Monday 9 February** by means of a duly signed declaration transmitted to the PEC address: protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it, which must bear in the subject line the wording "Manifestazione di interesse l'affidamento del servizio di raccolta, trasporto e recupero di indumenti usati" *(wording as printed)*, with a simple photocopy of a valid identity document of the signatory attached.

Expressions of interest received after the peremptory deadline indicated will not be taken into consideration.

## 5) PLACE OF PERFORMANCE OF THE SERVICE:

The territory of the municipality of Collesano.
