## AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

## TENDER PROCEDURE FOR THE AWARD IN CONCESSION OF USE AND MANAGEMENT OF THE MUNICIPAL BUILDING FOR COMMERCIAL AND CATERING ACTIVITY

### Preamble

The Municipal Administration of Collesano, in execution of the resolution of the Giunta Comunale [town board] G.M. 34 of 09/03/2026 and of the managerial determination n. 725 of 11/06/2026, intends to award in concession of use and management the municipally-owned building located in c/da Drinzi and identified in the cadastre at map sheet 38, parcel 784, with the adjoining external area, in the ways indicated in this call and in the contractual clauses, by means of a public-evidence procedure. The principles concerning public-evidence procedures under RD 827/1924 apply to this concession award insofar as compatible. The provisions of D. Lgs. 36/2023 do not apply except for the legislative provisions expressly referred to in the acts of this procedure. The subject of the concession is described below with the characteristics of the building and its context, the composition of the offer, and the award criterion.

This is an open procedure to be awarded with the criterion of the most economically advantageous offer. Pursuant to art. 2 paragraph 2 of L. 241/1990, the deadline of the procedure is set at 90 natural and consecutive days from the expiry date of the deadline for the submission of offers.

### 2. SUBJECT AND PURPOSES OF THE NOTICE

The Municipality of Collesano is committed to enhancing the real-estate assets owned by the municipality in order to favour their use for purposes of public interest, as well as for the promotion of local development strategies based on the enhancement of endogenous resources, with particular reference to quality agri-food and artisanal products. The territory of the municipality of Collesano, in fact, boasts a heritage of biodiversity and production chains that need a permanent institutional and commercial showcase in order to be adequately enjoyed by residents and tourists. In this perspective lies the administration's interest in granting the building for a use of a commercial and catering nature aimed at:

**A. Enhancement of the agri-food Heritage and of Local Identity**
- Promotion of Authenticity: Creating a showcase of excellence that represents the agri-food and artisanal heritage of the territory, favouring the recognition of quality marks (DOP, IGP, BIO, PAT).
- Territorial Narration: Using the commercial space as a point of active narration (storytelling) of local production traditions, acting as a link between producers and final consumers.

**B. Economic Sustainability and Short Supply Chain**
- Support for the Short Supply Chain: Encouraging Km-0 circular-economy models, reducing the intermediation steps between production and sale in order to guarantee greater profitability for local businesses and fair prices for users.
- Showcase for the territory's Micro-Enterprises: Providing a structured commercial outlet to small production businesses that, individually, would not have the strength to establish a presence in strategic markets or prestigious areas.

**C. Urban Regeneration and Social Cohesion**
- Revitalisation of the Urban Fabric: Returning a public asset to the community through dynamic management, capable of generating tourist flows and interest even beyond the periods of high seasonality.
- Integration of the Tourist Offer: Integrating the sale of products with tourist information, creating an active presence that acts as a first point of welcome and orientation for visitors.

The building covered by this notice forms part of the **disposable assets of the municipality**. Therefore, its concession in use is governed by the provisions of the civil code and by the municipal regulations in force for disposable patrimonial assets.

### 3. DESCRIPTION OF THE BUILDING AND DURATION

The building is located in the territory of the municipality of Collesano, registered in the Building Cadastre at Sheet 38 Parcel 784; it includes the external appurtenant area consisting of a forecourt and is granted in the de-facto state in which the structure finds itself, of which the participant must take note by viewing it. The building extends over a surface of about 116 sqm and may host commercial and catering activities falling within the macro-categories of ATECO Codes **47** (retail trade) and **56** (catering). Inside the structure, subject to completion of the necessary legal formalities, the activities included in the above-specified macro-categories may therefore be carried out.

Uses other than those indicated must always be assessed in advance with the Municipal Administration and authorised by it. The floor plan and the graphic documents relating to the building and its appurtenant areas can be viewed at the municipal technical office. The building and its appurtenance, as stated, are handed over in the de-facto state in which they are found, following a handover report signed by the parties.

### 4. BASE RENT FOR THE TENDER AND UPDATING

The annual concession rent to be placed as the base of the selection procedure is set at **€ 5.673,60 [five thousand six hundred and seventy-three/60] per year**. This amount, calculated with reference to the real-estate quotation database of the Agenzia delle Entrate [revenue agency], constitutes the **auction base subject to upward bidding**.

### 5. REQUIREMENTS OF THE PROPOSING ENTITIES

The Public Notice is open to entities in possession of:

- **General requirements**: Absence of the grounds for exclusion provided for by the legislation in force on public contracts (e.g. DURC regularity [social-security compliance certificate], absence of criminal convictions).
- **Professional requirements**: Registration with the C.C.I.A.A. ### 6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONCESSIONAIRE AND MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR THE EXERCISE OF THE ACTIVITY

The building may be used exclusively in conformity with its intended use and must be returned to the Municipality of Collesano at the expiry of the concession in the state in which it was at the moment of handover, except for deterioration deriving from wear. For the catering and serving activity, the manager must obtain the necessary authorisations, observing all legal requirements, under his own responsibility and at his own entrepreneurial risk, holding the Municipal Administration harmless and indemnified from any claim by third parties. In particular, the concessionaire is responsible for:

- Carrying out, at his own care and expense, ordinary maintenance interventions including those relating to the installations and to every asset present, as well as all works that may become necessary for the issue of the prescribed administrative and health authorisations. Extraordinary maintenance interventions connected with the use of the building or that may become necessary due to the failure to carry out ordinary maintenance, or due to negligence or fault or responsibility or improper use or neglect of the structure or of the external appurtenant area.
- The right to carry out, at his own expense, works of transformation, modification and improvement of premises and installations, as well as of the external appurtenant area, following prior written authorisation from the Municipality, which reserves the right to accept their necessity and appropriateness, without prejudice to the acquisition of every legal authorisation. No asset present at the building on the date of handover may be sold or destroyed by the concessionaire without the prior authorisation of the municipality of Collesano.
- Keeping the structure and the assigned external appurtenant area in efficiency and in a state of decorum and cleanliness.
- Taking on the utility supply contracts, such as electricity, water, gas, heating, waste disposal and every other possible tariff, tax or fee provided for by law and attributable to the activity exercised.
- Guaranteeing easy and efficient availability, equipping himself, at his own expense, with adequate communication tools (telephony, email, Wi-fi) which must also serve for relations with the public.
- Returning the spaces free of things and persons at the expiry of the contract, as well as all the technical documentation relating to the building held by the concessionaire up to that moment.

### 7. DURATION AND COMMENCEMENT OF THE CONCESSION

The duration of the concession covered by this notice is set at **9 (nine) years** starting from the date of signing of the contract, without the possibility of tacit renewal and subject to verification of the persistence of the conditions necessary for renewal and, in particular, the fulfilment of the contractual obligations and the permanence of the requirements proven at the moment of signing the contract. The grantor reserves the right to hand over the asset in advance, pending the formal completion of the contract and subject to the signing of a report of taking delivery of the asset countersigned by both parties. At the expiry of the concession period, the municipal Administration will assess whether the conditions of public interest for a new award persist, renewing the contract or establishing the regulatory conditions for a possible new public-evidence procedure.

### 8. ECONOMIC CONDITIONS

The concessionaire shall pay to the granting municipality of Collesano a monthly rent in the amount resulting from the upward offer made during the tender on the minimum amount placed as the tender base, plus tax charges if due. It is specified that the offer base, as described above, will be subject to upward bidding during the tender and therefore the definitive rent will consist of the amount of the monthly rent provided for by the economic framework plus the upward percentage offered. Payment of the rent is to be made in six-monthly instalments in advance, respectively by 31 January and 31 July of each year. For the first year, the first instalment shall be paid in an amount proportionally referred to the months between the date of signing of the contract and the date of the first scheduled instalment. At the end of the concession there will be no economic recognition for the outgoing manager and the publicly-owned building must be returned to the municipality of Collesano in its full integrity, as it was handed over, and with any improvements made, which cannot be the subject of any economic recognition.

### 9. METHOD AND CRITERION OF AWARD AND SCORING GRID

The concession of the building will be carried out through the open procedure and with the criterion of the Most Economically Advantageous Offer, identified on the basis of the best quality/price ratio. The Commission, as a perfect collegial body, composed of three (3) members including the RUP [sole procedure manager], will have available a total score of 100. As regards the evaluation of the project proposals, each commissioner will assign a coefficient between 0 and 1 to each element of the project Proposal, according to the following scale of values:

| Judgement | Coefficient | Criterion for judging the proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 1,00 | The possibility of better solutions is reasonably excluded |
| Very good | 0,80 | High positive aspects and good correspondence to expectations |
| Good | 0,60 | Evident positive aspects, but inferior to optimal solutions |
| Fair | 0,40 | Some appreciable positive aspects |
| Sufficient | 0,20 | Barely perceptible |
| Absent or irrelevant | 0,00 | No relevant proposal |

The average of the coefficients assigned by the individual commissioners will then be calculated and multiplied by the maximum score obtainable for the specific element.

**b4) Fitting-out and decorum of the premises** — Max 20 points

Coherence of the interior and exterior furnishing solutions (e.g. dehors, outdoor tables) with the urban context and the prestige of the municipal building. Presentation of a specific programme for the cleaning and management of the appurtenant areas.

### B) ECONOMIC OFFER — UPWARD BID ON THE BASE RENT

The economic score will be assigned on the basis of the percentage increase proposed by the competitor compared with the annual rent placed as the auction base (€ 5.673,60 ). Downward offers or offers equal to the auction base are not admitted.

**a) Attribution of the economic score (Max 30 points**): The points will be calculated by means of the following linear proportional formula: Score of the offer under examination = 30 x ( Percentage increase of the offer under examination ) / Maximum percentage increase offered in the tender

At the moment of signing the concession contract, the awardee, as a guarantee of the complete and punctual fulfilment of the obligations assumed, must provide the municipality of Collesano with a definitive bank surety guarantee or insurance policy for an amount equal to 10% of the annual rent placed as the tender base, rounded up to the nearest thousand euros. This guarantee must continue uninterruptedly for the entire duration of the contract and must expressly provide, obligatorily on pain of exclusion:

- **Waiver of the benefit of prior enforcement** against the principal debtor referred to in art. 1944 of the civil code;
- **Waiver of the exception referred to in art. 1957, paragraph 2, of the civil code**: the validity of the surety remains effective for the entire duration of the contract and until the complete satisfaction of the obligations, independently of the expiry terms for the judicial action of the municipality.
- **Payment on simple written request**: The guarantor undertakes to pay the sums requested by the municipality within the maximum term of 15 (fifteen) days from receipt of a simple written request, with waiver of any exception or opposition ("first demand" clause).
- **Reinstatement of the surety**: Should the municipality collect, partially or totally, the guarantee due to breaches by the Concessionaire, the latter shall be obliged to reinstate the original amount of the surety within the peremptory term of 20 (twenty) days from the communication of the withdrawal, on pain of immediate termination of the contract for just cause.

The surety must in any case continue to have effect until a specific release communication is made by the beneficiary administration, with which the absence or the settlement of every possible exception and dispute arising in connection with the execution of the contract will also be attested. ### 14. INSURANCE OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONCESSIONAIRE

The concessionaire is liable for damage that may occur to users of the service, to third parties or to things in the course of the performance of the activities and attributable to the fault of his own operators or deriving from irregularities and/or deficiencies of the services. ### 15. BREACHES – REVOCATION OF THE CONCESSION

The concession in question may be revoked, for needs of public interest or in the presence of serious causes of force majeure, on the express and unquestionable request of the Municipality of Collesano with written notice to be sent to the concessionaire by PEC. In the event of revocation of the concession, the Municipality of Collesano may demand the immediate return of the asset in addition to compensation for any damage. The building must be returned free and clear of persons and/or things. If this is not done within the term set, once 30 days have passed from that term, the things present inside the premises will be taken to a landfill and the restoration costs will be charged to the holder of the concession. The concession is subject to a use that does not cause damage to the building and its appurtenances, that does not disturb public quiet and that does not conflict with the regulations in force on public security, hygiene and building matters. The concessionaire may withdraw from the contract unilaterally with at least 6 months' notice.

### 16. TERMINATION OF THE CONTRACT

The municipality reserves the right to terminate the contract pursuant to art. 1456 of the civil code in the event of:

1. Non-payment of the rent after the tolerance period of 30 days has elapsed;
2. Unauthorised change of the intended use;
3. Sub-concession or assignment of the contract to third parties;
4. Serious violations of hygiene-health or safety regulations in the exercise of the activity.
