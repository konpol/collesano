# PUBLIC NOTICE

## TRANSPORT SERVICE FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES TO REHABILITATION CENTRES

## Having recalled:

- Resolution of the Municipal Executive n. 163 of 30.12.2025 approving the implementation of a transport service to territorial services in favour of users with disabilities;
- Managerial Determination n. 2 of 14.01.2026 concerning the Remodulation of the ATS social infrastructure project;
- SO.SVI.MA Note - Remodulation of the ATS social infrastructure project ref. prot. n. 886 of 20.01.2026

**Notice is given of the start of the Transport Service for minors with disabilities who attend rehabilitation centres.**

The intervention is aimed at supporting attendance at the rehabilitation facility, fostering inclusion and participation in social life, in conformity with what is provided:

✓ by art. 13 of D.lgs. n. 267 of 18.08.2000
✓ by art. 26 of law 104 of 05.02.1992
✓ by art. 6 paragraph 2 lett. c of Sicilian Regional Law n. 68 of 18.08.1981

## Art. 1 Purpose of the service

The free municipal transport service to and from the Rehabilitation Centres provides support to families with minors with disabilities who must access the Rehabilitation Centres authorised by the ASP [provincial health authority].

## Art. 2 Recipients of the service

The transport service is addressed to minors with disabilities, recognised pursuant to Law n. 104/1992 art. 3 - paragraph 3, resident in the Municipality of Collesano, beneficiaries of multidisciplinary therapies provided by rehabilitation centres authorised by the ASP.

## Art. 3 Access requirements for the service

Beneficiaries may use the transport if in possession of the following access requirements:

- Assessment report pursuant to Law 104/92 attesting the type and degree of the handicap, issued by the competent health commission of the ASP, currently valid;
- Therapeutic plan containing the number of sessions and the relative type of treatment to be carried out, issued by the ASP, currently valid;

## Art. 4 Admission to the service

Access to the Service takes place following submission of a specific application by a parent exercising parental responsibility or by the legal representative. A form is attached to this notice. It must be accompanied by the following documentation produced in copy:

a) Assessment report under Law 104/92;
b) Therapeutic plan containing the number of sessions and the relative type of treatment to be carried out, issued by the ASP;
c) Photocopy of the identity document of the person submitting the application.

## Art. 5 Method of submission of applications

The application form attached to the Notice can be downloaded from the institutional website in the services section – browse by category – health, wellbeing and assistance, or can be requested at the Social Services Office. It must be duly completed in every part, accompanied by all the documentation indicated in art. 4 of this notice. The application may be submitted by the applicant in the following ways:

- Pec: protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it
- by hand to the Protocol Office of Collesano

## Art. 6 Timing and methods of identifying the beneficiaries of the service

Admission to the service takes place on the existence of the required requirements, subject to verification by the Professional Social Service. A waiting list may be provided. The validity of the request is annual. The application may be renewed annually if the access requirements persist.

## Art. 7 Withdrawal

The user has the right to withdraw from the service exclusively in written form.

## Art. 8 Rights and duties of users

In order to guarantee an effective and efficient service, the following rules to be observed are defined:

- accept the transport arrangements planned and agreed with the Rehabilitation Centre;
- do not ask the staff for services outside scheduled hours, nor for services not provided for by this notice;
- respect the scheduled transport times and promptly communicate any absence to the staff;
- report any service problems.

## Art. 9 Respect for privacy

The Municipality of Collesano, controller of the data provided, undertakes to process the personal and sensitive data communicated by applicants only for the purposes of this Notice and, in any case, in compliance with the legislation in force on privacy protection, with particular reference to what is provided by Law 675/1996 "Protection of persons and other subjects with regard to the processing of personal data", by D.Lgs. 196/2003 entitled "Code on the protection of personal data" and GDPR n. 679/2016 "European regulation on the protection of personal data".

It is specified that the notice is open without deadline for the submission of applications in the ways provided for by this notice.
