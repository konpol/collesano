## DECREES

### Article 1 — General provisions

By this call, and by the annexes which form an integral and substantial part of it, the selection is launched of 65.964 volunteer operators, of whom:

- 64.479 volunteer operators to be started in 2.635 projects, belonging to n. 548 Universal Civic Service intervention programmes to be carried out in Italy (Annex 1);
- 1.485 volunteer operators to be started in 204 projects, belonging to n. 54 Universal Civic Service intervention programmes to be carried out abroad (Annex 2).

The projects last between 8 and 12 months, with service hours of 25 hours per week or with an annual total of hours varying, proportionately, between 1.145 hours for 12-month projects, 1.049 hours for 11-month projects, 954 hours for 10-month projects, 859 hours for 9-month projects and 765 hours for 8-month projects, structured over five or six days a week.

Some projects provide a reservation of places dedicated to young people with fewer opportunities (in particular with disabilities, low schooling, economic difficulties, care leavers and young people in a temporary condition of personal and social fragility, as defined in the circular of 12 March 2025 indicated in the preamble); other projects, among those to be carried out in Italy, also provide a tutoring period or a period of one to three months in one of the countries of the European Union. Information on such projects is available both in the project summary sheets referred to in article 4 and on the DOL platform for the submission of applications referred to in article 5.

Young people possessing the requirements of article 2 of this call may take part in the selection, under the conditions specified therein. Each young person, on pain of exclusion from the procedure, may submit only one application for participation in the call and for only one project among those indicated in the two annexes.

The projects in Annex 1 of this call which, at the chosen service start date, can count on the contribution of only one selected volunteer operator, will not be activated. If, within three months of that date, following the selection procedures already carried out, the entity covers the initially funded positions with at least one further unit, the project may be activated in accordance with the provisions in force.

Only projects intended to be carried out abroad, in Annex 2, may be activated despite the contribution of a single selected volunteer operator, in consideration of their peculiar characteristics.

The service start date of the volunteer operators differs for the various programmes and is established, within the dates indicated in the following paragraph, taking account of the end of the selection procedures and the compilation of the ranking lists by each project-holding entity, the specific needs of the project, and the time needed for the Department's examination of the ranking lists sent by the entities and of the specific requests connected with the start of service.

The calendar of service starts for the volunteer operators selected following this Call is as follows:

- 18 September 2026;
- 9 October 2026;
- 30 October 2026.

The entities are in any case required to provide prompt and correct information to those concerned. The start of service of the volunteer operators employed in the projects takes place on the dates indicated above, without prejudice to the possibility for the entities to request subsequent replacements and/or postponed activation for initial failure to reach the minimum threshold of the number of volunteer operators, as provided for by the provisions in force.

The start of service by replacement of the young people found suitable but not selected at the outcome of the selection procedures, identified to cover vacant positions, will take place on the dates indicated below:

- 29 September 2026;
- 16 October 2026;
- 13 November 2026;
- 11 December 2026;
- 15 January 2027;
- 12 February 2027;
- 12 March 2027;
- 9 April 2027.

Each selected volunteer operator will be called upon to sign with the Department a contract fixing, among other things, the amount of the monthly allowance for the performance of the service at € 519,47, which may be increased on the basis of the variation, ascertained by ISTAT, of the consumer price index for the families of workers and employees.

The provisions of article 16, paragraph 3 of legislative decree n. 40/2017 apply to the allowances attributed to volunteer operators. The period served as a Civic Service volunteer operator is, at the request of the person concerned, recognised for pension purposes in the forms and ways provided for by article 4, paragraph 2, of decree-law 29 November 2008 n. 185, converted into law 28 January 2009, n. 2.

If the volunteer operator resides in a Municipality other than that in which the project is carried out, they are entitled to reimbursement, by the Department, of the expenses of the initial journey only to reach the project location (plane, train, coach, ferry, provided it is the cheapest means). They are likewise entitled to reimbursement of the expenses to reach their place of residence at the end of the service, which will take place in the same manner (plane, train, coach, ferry, provided it is the cheapest means).

At the start of service, an insurance contract is concluded for the risks connected with the performance of the service; in addition to this insurance cover, the holding entity may activate supplementary insurance to cover specific risks.

Once the project activities are finished, the volunteer operator will be issued by the Department with the certificate of completion of the Civic Service drawn up on the basis of the data provided by the entity. In addition, in the manner provided for by each project, the volunteer operator receives from the entity a certificate/certification of recognition and valorisation of the competences acquired on the basis of what is provided for by the selected project.

### Article 2 — Participation requirements

As provided for by article 14 of legislative decree 6 March 2017, n. 40, young people possessing the following requirements may submit an application to take part in the selection:

a) citizenship of Italy, or of one of the other Member States of the European Union, or of a non-EU country provided the candidate is legally residing in Italy;
b) having reached eighteen years of age and not having exceeded twenty-eight years of age (28 years and 364 days) at the date of submission of the application;
c) not having been sentenced, even non-finally, to imprisonment exceeding one year for a non-negligent offence, or to a penalty, even of a lesser amount, for an offence against the person or concerning the illicit possession, use, carrying, transport, import or export of weapons or explosive materials, or for offences concerning membership of or aiding subversive, terrorist or organised-crime groups.

Young people are asked to read carefully the projects and the summary sheets referred to in article 4. Attention is also recommended to projects with a reservation of places intended for young people with fewer opportunities (disability, low schooling, economic difficulties,¹ care leavers and young people with temporary personal and social fragility): to apply for the reserved places, the young person must necessarily possess the specific requirement demanded.

The participation requirements must be possessed at the date of submission of the application for participation and, with the exception of the age limit, maintained until the end of the service, on pain of exclusion from the procedure or from the continuation of the service.

### Article 3 — Further indications for participation

Applications may be submitted by young people who, without prejudice to possession of the requirements of article 2:

- because of the effects of the risk situations linked to the Covid-19 epidemiological emergency, interrupted their service either voluntarily or because the project in which they were engaged was definitively interrupted by the entity;
- interrupted their civic service at the conclusion of a sanctioning procedure against the entity which caused the revocation of the project, or because of the closure of the project or of the implementation location at the entity's reasoned request, provided that, in all cases, the period of service performed did not exceed six months;
- interrupted their service for health reasons such as not to allow its continuation, producing appropriate certification issued by a doctor belonging to the National Health Service, provided that the period of service performed did not exceed six months;
- were excluded from the Universal Civic Service because of exceeding the sick days provided for, provided that the period of service performed did not exceed six months;
- interrupted the Universal Civic Service for the accompaniment of the severely disabled and civilian blind referred to in art. 1 of law n. 288/2002 and art. 40 of law n. 289/2002, because of the death or renunciation of the assisted person, where it was not possible to arrange their replacement, provided that the period of service performed did not exceed six months;
- have already served in a project funded by the PON-IOG "Garanzia Giovani" [Youth Guarantee], provided they duly completed the service;
- in the course of 2021 were started in service for participation in a project funded by the PON-IOG "Garanzia Giovani" and subsequently, following checks carried out by the Department, excluded for lack of possession of the additional requirements;
- have already performed Civic Service within the European experimental project International Volunteering Opportunities for All or within the projects for the Civil Peace Corps;
- have already performed the "Regional civic service", that is, a service established by a law of a region or an autonomous province.

Applications for service may also be submitted by female volunteer operators admitted to the Civic Service in previous selections and subsequently placed on leave for pregnancy and maternity, who did not complete the six months of service, net of the leave period, provided they possess the requirements of the preceding articles.

Applications may not be submitted by young people who:

- belong to the military corps and the police forces;
- have, at the time of publication of this call, employment, collaboration or paid internship relationships of any kind with the entity holding the programme or the project or with the host entity;
- have had employment, collaboration or paid internship relationships of any kind lasting more than three months with the entity holding the programme or the project or with the host entity in the 12 months preceding the date of publication of the call;
- have already performed the National Civic Service, or have already performed or are performing the Universal Civic Service, or interrupted the service before the scheduled end, save as provided in the preceding paragraphs.

### Article 4 — Publication obligations of the entities

To enable young people to have all the elements to make the best choice of project for which to apply, the entities holding the projects publish on the home page of their websites, in addition to the list of their own projects, a summary sheet for each project reporting the essential information to guide the young person's choice, in the format set out in Annex A and Annex B depending on whether the project is Universal Civic Service in Italy (A) or Universal Civic Service abroad (B).

Each entity also enters into the Helios system the URLs of the publication pages. Civic Service projects are the tools through which the objectives identified in the intervention programme to which they belong are achieved. The sheet in the cited annexes therefore also identifies the main characteristics of the project's reference programmes.

The entity, in addition to the sheets, may also publish the entire project document, also bearing the burden of answering at its own expense any further requests for detail from young people.

Entities are obliged to publish on the home page of their websites the link to the On-line Application platform (hereinafter the DOL platform), the only tool through which the application referred to in article 5 must be completed and submitted. Entities are also required to indicate on their home page the opening days and hours of their offices, and a telephone number, to provide information or support to young people who need it.

### Article 5 — Methods of submitting applications

Aspiring volunteer operators (hereinafter "candidates") must submit the application for participation, addressed directly to the entity holding the chosen project, exclusively through the DOL platform reachable via PC, tablet and smartphone at https://domandaonline.serviziocivile.it.

Only one application for participation may be submitted, for a single project and a single location, to be chosen from among the projects listed in the annexes to this call and shown on the DOL platform.

To access the application completion and submission services on the DOL platform, the candidate must be recognised by the system. Italian citizens resident in Italy or abroad can access it via the Electronic Identity Card (CIE). The CIE is the identity document of Italian citizens which allows the ascertainment of the holder's identity and access to the online services of the Public Administrations both in Italy and in the countries of the European Union. Submitting the On-line Civic Service Application requires CIE credentials of security level 2.

Access is also permitted via the Public Digital Identity System (SPID). The Public Digital Identity System (SPID) is the simple, fast and secure access tool for the digital services of local and central administrations. Submitting the On-line Civic Service Application requires SPID credentials of security level 2.

Should citizens of European Union countries, or of non-EU countries legally residing in Italy, not have the possibility of acquiring SPID, they may request the Department to issue dedicated credentials to access the services of the DOL platform, following the procedure available on the platform's home page.

Non-EU citizens legally residing in Italy must, when requesting credentials for the submission of the online application, obligatorily attach, in addition to a valid identity document, their valid residence permit or the application for its issue/renewal.

**Applications for participation must be submitted exclusively in the online manner described above, by no later than 14:00 on 8 April 2026.**

Applications transmitted after that deadline or by other means will not be taken into consideration.

On the day following the submission of the application, the Department's protocol system sends the candidate, by email, the receipt attesting submission with the protocol number and the date and time of submission of the application. In the event of incorrect completion, it is permitted to cancel one's application until 14:00 of the day preceding the expiry date of this call.

Young candidates, to better orient themselves in choosing the project, in addition to using the search tools made available by the platform and on the Department's websites, may refer to the Civic Service entities in their territories.

When sending the application for participation, citizens resident in Italy may request the automatic and free activation of the National Youth Card. The National Youth Card is a free digital tool aimed at promoting access to goods and/or services for young people aged between 18 and 35 who are resident in Italy. Further information on the CGN and its associated benefits is available at: https://giovani2030.it/iniziativa/carta-giovani-nazionale/.

The Guide for completing and submitting the On-line Application with the DOL platform is available on the Department's websites www.politichegiovanili.gov.it and www.scelgoilserviziocivile.gov.it.

### Article 6 — Selection procedures

The selection of candidates is carried out, pursuant to article 15, paragraph 2, of legislative decree 6 March 2017, n. 40, by the entity holding the chosen project, through dedicated Commissions for the evaluation of any qualifications and curricular experience gained and declared by each candidate when submitting the application for participation. The Commissions also subject the candidates to an interview. The evaluation of qualifications and curricular experience precedes the interview.

The selection procedures must respect, among others, the principles of transparency and publicity, with the obligation, among other things, to minute the evaluation activities. The criteria for attributing scores to qualifications and curricular experience must refer to what is provided for by the selection system accredited by the entity. The methods of conducting the interview, if not already provided for by that system, must be predetermined by the Commissions.

Candidates comply with the instructions given by the entity regarding the times, places and methods of the selection procedures, which in any case are carried out in the Italian language.

In order to reduce selection times and allow a prompt start of service for the volunteer operators, in the face of a particularly high number of candidates, the entity may, exceptionally, carry out the evaluation of qualifications and curricular experience after the oral test, by analogy with and under the conditions indicated by the rules on access to public employment in art. 8 paragraph 1 of DPR 9 May 1994, n. 487.

Should the conditions for holding the tests in person not exist, and the accredited system does not require them to be held exclusively in that manner, the interviews may also be carried out by the Commissions online, respecting the indications of the accredited selection system. The entity may proceed by informing the candidates in advance of the manner in which the tests will be carried out.

It is the entity's responsibility to ensure, also in the case of online methods, respect for the principles of transparency and publicity, guaranteeing the possibility for anyone who wishes, and to an extent sustainable for the computer systems used, to attend the interviews. The candidate's presence at the online interview must be verified through the exhibition of a valid identity document and must be recorded in the system. If a candidate does not have the possibility of holding the interview online, the entity must adopt every possible alternative solution to allow the test to be held anyway.

The entity that submitted the project publishes on its website, in the section dedicated to the "Civic Service" on the home page, the calendar of convocation to the interviews at least 10 days before their start, on pain of annulment of the selections. It is recalled that any exclusion of a candidate from the interview must be promptly communicated to the person concerned, specifying the reason. Publication of the calendar has the value of notification of the convocation for all legal purposes, and a candidate who, despite having submitted the application, does not appear at the interview on the established days, without justified reason, is to be considered excluded from the selection for not having completed the procedure.

The entity evaluates, preliminarily, any qualifications and curricular experience of each candidate, attributes the related scores by transcribing them in a dedicated summary sheet and brings them to the candidates' knowledge, except in the exceptional cases recalled above, of which communication will in any case be given to them.

Educational qualifications obtained abroad may be evaluated only if the candidate has obtained, at the time of submitting the application, the recognition measure.

After the evaluation of qualifications, the entity subjects the candidates to an interview and compiles for each one a sheet accounting for the scores attributed. The entity must not indicate the qualification score for those absent from the interview. Candidates who obtained in the interview a score below the threshold possibly provided for by the selection system adopted are declared unsuitable to carry out the project for which they underwent selection.

The entity, once the selection procedures are finished, compiles the ranking lists for the individual project locations, in descending order of the score attributed to the candidates, highlighting those usefully selected with reference to the available places. The ranking lists must take account of the location indicated by the candidate in the application.

Considering that admission to perform the Universal Civic Service is subject to the age limit of twenty-eight, pursuant to article 14 of legislative decree 6 March 2017, n. 40, in order to allow maximum participation by those possessing the requirements, in the event of equal scores the older candidate is preferred.

The ranking list also includes candidates found suitable but not selected for lack of places, candidates unsuitable to carry out the project for which they underwent selection, and those who did not appear at the interview.

The entity that submitted the project must ensure adequate publicity for the ranking list on its website; in addition, the entity may use any other suitable means. Together with the ranking list, the entity publishes on its website the presumed service start date of the volunteer operators, which must be confirmed by the Department depending on the entity's correct execution of all the necessary operations.

### Article 8 — Obligations of the volunteer operator for the start of service

Each suitable selected candidate digitally signs the Universal Civic Service contract, signed by the Head of the Department, via PagoPA's App IO by 23:59 of the day preceding the start date. In the event of proven technical impossibility, the suitable selected candidate downloads, for signature, the Civic Service contract signed by the Head of the Department by accessing the reserved area of the Department's website. The contract indicates, among other things, the assigned location, the service start and end dates, the economic conditions and the service obligations referred to in article 10.

### Article 9 — Operational specifications for Civic Service projects abroad

For volunteer operators engaged in the realisation of Civic Service projects abroad and of projects providing the additional measure of a period of 1, 2 or 3 months in one of the EU countries, a daily allowance is provided, in addition to the monthly allowance of € 519,47, to be paid for the period of actual service abroad, differentiated on the basis of the "country cost" criterion of the country in which the volunteer operators are engaged. The aforementioned additional allowance is not paid during periods of service in which the volunteer operators are in the national territory (including those dedicated to training activities) and during leave periods, even if taken abroad. Likewise, that allowance is not paid to young people resident in the country where the project is carried out. In the event of illness abroad, the allowance is paid for the first 15 days for projects lasting 12 months; for projects of shorter duration, the days are calculated proportionally. The amount of the daily foreign allowance due to the volunteer operators is specified in the table in Annex D.

A daily contribution is also provided for the expenses of managing and implementing the projects, as well as for board and lodging, paid to the project-holding entity during the period of the volunteers' actual stay abroad, differentiated by geographical area of implementation of the project. The amount of the daily foreign contribution is also specified in the cited table in Annex D.

The transport expenses for the outward and return journey from Italy to the foreign country at the start and end of the project and, if provided for by the project, for one intermediate outward and return journey, are advanced by the entity carrying out the project and reimbursed by the Department within the limits of the indications provided by the Department itself.

### Article 10 — Service obligations of the volunteer operators

The Civic Service relationship is established with the signing, by the selected young person, of the contract referred to in article 8, which provides, among other things, the legal and economic treatment of the volunteer operator, as well as the rules of conduct with which they must comply and the related disciplinary sanctions should they fail to respect them.

In general terms, the volunteer operator is obliged to respect the service hours, to carry out the activities provided for by the project and to follow the prescriptions given by the employing entity regarding the organisation of the activities.

The volunteer operator may not carry out subordinate or self-employed work if incompatible with the correct performance of the Civic Service, pursuant to article 16, paragraph 5 of legislative decree 40/2017. The compatibility evaluation is the responsibility of the project-holding entity or the host entity.

The detail of the obligations, from the selected young person's reporting for service until the end of the service, is contained in the "Provisions concerning the regulation of relations between entities and volunteer operators of the Universal Civic Service" approved by departmental decree n. 1641/2024 of 12 December 2024 and subsequently amended by departmental decree n. 859/2025 of 1 August 2025.

For all operators started in the Civic Service, the voluntary interruption of the relationship before the scheduled end entails the impossibility of taking part in the future in the realisation of new Civic Service projects, as well as forfeiture of any benefits provided for by the specific project and the non-issue of the certificate of performance of the service.

As regards, in particular, the volunteer operators who will be engaged in projects abroad and in projects providing a period of stay in EU countries, in the 30 days preceding departure for the destination country, they are also obliged, for security reasons, to register on the website www.dovesiamonelmondo.it of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation. The project-holding entity must verify the actual registration of the volunteer operators on the aforementioned website, since failure to fulfil this obligation precludes the departure abroad of unregistered volunteer operators. Any erroneous departure abroad of unregistered volunteer operators entails their immediate return to Italy with the cost of the return journey charged to the entities.

Volunteer operators serving abroad and those performing a period of service in EU countries must also comply with the warnings provided by the competent diplomatic authorities, as well as with the rules of conduct and the provisions issued by the entities, which must ensure responsible management of the volunteer operators, inspired by the principle of risk minimisation and the strengthening of the culture of safety.

Also in the light of the changing international situation relating to the various ongoing emergencies, close to the departure of the volunteer operators for the various foreign destination countries, the MAECI [Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation] will provide the Department with indications and updates on any advisories against sending young people to specific territories with particular risk conditions.

In relation to the prevailing security conditions in the place or for serious reasons of expediency, the MAECI may order the temporary transfer to other localities or the repatriation of the young volunteers, promptly informing the Department and the entity or organisation at which the young volunteers serve. The MAECI identifies, where appropriate in agreement with the competent authorities of the foreign countries concerned, further measures to protect the volunteers' safety.

### Article 11 — Reservation of places in competitions for the recruitment of non-executive staff called by public administrations

Pursuant to article 18, paragraph 4, of legislative decree 40/2017, in favour of volunteer operators who have completed the Universal Civic Service or the National Civic Service without demerit, a quota equal to 15 per cent of the places is reserved in competitions for the recruitment of non-executive staff called by the public administrations referred to in article 1, paragraph 2, of legislative decree 30 March 2001, n. 165, by the special companies and by the entities referred to in the consolidated text of the laws on the organisation of local authorities, legislative decree 18 August 2000, n. 267, without prejudice to the rights of those entitled to recruitment pursuant to law 12 March 1999, n. 68, and taking account of the limits provided for by article 5, first paragraph, of the consolidated text of the provisions concerning the statute of civil servants of the State, decree of the President of the Republic 10 January 1957, n. 3, and by article 52, paragraph 1-bis, of the cited legislative decree n. 165 of 2001.

### Article 12 — Processing of personal data

The personal data of candidates and volunteer operators are processed in conformity with EU Regulation n. 2016/679 – hereinafter the Regulation, as well as in accordance with the Data Protection Rules within the Universal Civic Service, adopted by the Head of the Department for youth policies and the Universal Civic Service of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers by decree n. 705 of 29 July 2022.

The Controller of the processing of personal data is the Head of the Department pro-tempore, as identified by article 3 of the DPCM of 25 May 2018.

The personal data provided are used by the Department, as "Controller", and by the entity which, as "Processor" pursuant to and for the purposes of article 28 of the Regulation, handles the selection procedure and employs the volunteer operator in the realisation of the project, as well as by any host entity as "sub-processor".

The personal data provided are processed by PagoPA S.p.A., for the purposes connected with the possible signing of the Universal Civic Service contract in digital form via the App IO, as well as connected with the possible activation of the National Youth Card, via the same App IO, after the deadline set for the submission of applications, for those who expressed their consent when submitting the application for participation. PagoPA S.p.A. will operate as "Processor" pursuant to and for the purposes of article 28 of the Regulation.

The Processors undertake to guarantee organisational and technical measures suitable for the protection of the personal data processed. The personal data provided are necessary for the obligations provided for, by law and by this call, for the performance of the selection activities and for the possible establishment of the Civic Service relationship.

The personal data are processed for the purposes connected with the management of that relationship, the realisation of the Civic Service project, relations with the volunteer operators' Representation and the institutional activities of the Department and of the Regions and Autonomous Provinces. Data processing takes place, as a rule, with the aid of automated tools in the ways and within the limits necessary to pursue the purposes for which they were collected.

The communication of personal data and the authorisation for their processing are necessary for the evaluation of the participation requirements for the selection, on pain of exclusion from it. Where necessary for the pursuit of the purposes and the fulfilment of the specified obligations, the Department may collect - from other public administrations or public and private entities - other categories of personal data relating to candidates and volunteer operators.

The Controller may transfer the personal data collected to public or private parties that will provide specific services instrumental to the Department's institutional purposes. The Controller, in compliance with article 32 of GDPR 2016/679, puts in place measures suitable to guarantee that personal data are processed adequately and in conformity with the purposes for which they are managed, and employs security, organisational and technical measures suitable to protect personal data from alteration, destruction, loss, theft or improper or illegitimate use.

The communication of untruthful data results in the imposition of administrative sanctions, without prejudice to any criminal relevance of the conduct, pursuant to article 76 of D.P.R. 28 December 2000, n. 445.

At any time, data subjects may exercise the rights under the cited Regulation, including: the right to request the erasure of their personal data, their possible rectification, etc. These rights may be asserted by written request sent to the Presidency of the Council of Ministers - Department for youth policies and the Universal Civic Service at the email address: privacy@serviziocivile.it and to the Civic Service URP [public relations office] at the address: urp@serviziocivile.it.
