# PRESS RELEASE

## "ANCH'IO SONO LA PROTEZIONE CIVILE" [I TOO AM THE CIVIL PROTECTION]: THE COLLESANO SCHOOL CAMP GETS UNDER WAY

From 1 August 2026 to 5 August 2026 in Collesano

The municipal group of Civil Protection Volunteers and the Municipality of Collesano are taking part in the sixteenth edition of the national project «Anch'io sono la protezione civile», with a school camp reserved for young people from 10 to 16 years of age, scheduled from 1 August 2026 to 5 August 2026 in Collesano, at the Cloister of Piazza Santa Maria di Gesù.

In a programme structured into educational activities, practical drills and recreational moments, the participants will have the opportunity to get to know at first hand the civil protection system, fire risk, seismic risk; hydrogeological risk; team activities; practical drills and the useful behaviours to adopt in the event of an emergency to protect themselves, the environment, the territory and their own community.
