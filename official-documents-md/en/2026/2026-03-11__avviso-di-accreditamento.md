# UNION OF THE "MADONIE" MUNICIPALITIES

with offices in the Municipality of Petralia Soprana (PA)

## NATIONAL STRATEGY FOR INNER AREAS — Sheet AIMA 08 "Network of home social-health services for persons with disabilities" — CUP B91E20000050009

# ACCREDITATION NOTICE – UPDATE

## EXPRESSION OF INTEREST TO PROVIDE VOUCHERABLE SERVICES ATTRIBUTABLE TO PERSONS WITH HANDICAP

Whereas:

- the Union of the "Madonie" Municipalities, in its capacity as implementing entity of the National Strategy for Inner Areas (SNAI) of the Madonie prototype area, is also the beneficiary of the project intervention called "Network of home social-health services for persons with disabilities";
- Said intervention, with D.D.G. n. 2101 of 18/10/2021 of the regional Department for family and social policies, was admitted to financing under the PO FSE Sicilia 2014/2020 for the realisation of the interventions provided for by the Inner Areas Strategies (SNAI) in implementation of Specific Objective 9.3 – Action 9.3.6 "Implementation of service vouchers (to facilitate households' access to the network of home and day-cycle social-health services, and to foster an increase in the quality of services both in terms of services provided and of extension of time slots and integration of the chain), promotion of regular employment";
- The intervention will make it possible to implement the service-voucher system to facilitate the access of households with differently-abled members to the network of home and day-cycle social-health services, in order to foster an increase in the quality of services both in terms of services provided and of extension of time slots and integration of the chain, in close coordination with the three Social-Health Districts (DSS 33, DSS 35 and DSS 37) operating in the territory; it decided to adopt *(grammatical shift as printed in the original)*, in the perspective of subsidiarity, transparency, participation and support of private commitment in the social function, the accreditation system, through which the participation and active involvement of third-sector organisations has been promoted in the prevention, elimination or reduction of situations of need and difficulty of the person and in the achievement of the social objectives of improving the quality of life;
- The district accreditation system of the social organisations under art. 1, 4th paragraph, of law 328/2000 is aimed at defining the open services to be provided in favour of young persons with disabilities.

In this sense, and in order to orient the services to be provided to the actual needs of the target users, namely persons with disabilities aged 18 or under, the objective of this expression of interest is to provide a detailed list of the social-assistance services, analytically indicated below, attributable to the voucher form, which are as follows:

## PERSONS WITH "HANIDICAP" (Minors) *(so in the original, typo for "HANDICAP")*

- Attendance at day and meeting centres (excluding the canteen and laundry service)
- Attendance at integrated socio-rehabilitation and day centres both for self-sufficient persons and for those with physical, psychological and social problems, also as respite for families
- Transport service for attendance at schools of every order and grade, including preparatory school,
- Transport service to socio-re-educational and health-rehabilitation centres,
- Animal-assisted therapy
- Speech therapy and psychomotricity
- ABA therapy
- Communication and autonomy assistance service in favour of pupils with disabilities attending primary and lower-secondary school;
- Recreational, theatrical and sports activities, also for personal and social integration and in support of rehabilitation activities;
- Other \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

Participating entities must, among other things, demonstrate that, in the case of recourse to activities that will take place inside the day assistance and meeting Centre for the indicated target users, the centre possesses the organisational and structural standards expressly provided for by the PRESIDENTIAL DECREE OF 29 JUNE 1988 "Structural and organisational standards of the social-assistance services and interventions provided for by regional law 9 May n. 22".

The expression of interest is therefore aimed at the creation of an accreditation Register of the Union of the "Madonie" Municipalities, through analytical descriptions attributable to the above-mentioned services to be provided to users by voucher. Interested entities are invited to submit a specific application for participation accompanied by a detailed description of the type of service offered.

The application of expression of interest to participate must reach the Union of the "Madonie" Municipalities at the following pec address: <u>unionemadonie@pec.comeg.it</u> with the following subject:

**"AGGIORNAMENTO MANIFESTAZIONE DI INTERESSE AD EFFETTUARE SERVIZI VOUCHERIZZABILI RICONDUCIBILI AD INABILI PORTATORI DI HANDICAP" no later than 14,00 on 20 March 2026.**

The list of admissible expressions of interest possessing the required requirements will update the Register of entities entitled to provide the voucherable services activatable within the AIMA 08 intervention "Network of home social-health services for persons with disabilities".

**Entities that already took part in the First Accreditation Notice issued in November 2021 are asked — given the time elapsed — to confirm, again by the expiry date of this Notice, their wish to maintain their registration in the Register of entities accredited for the provision of the services in question and to resubmit the Service Pact signed at that time, signed with the current date.**
