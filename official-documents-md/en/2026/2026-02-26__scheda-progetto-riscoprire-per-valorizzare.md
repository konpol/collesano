# Annex A – Universal Civic Service Italy

**SHEET OF ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS OF THE PROJECT ASSOCIATED WITH THE UNIVERSAL CIVIC SERVICE INTERVENTION PROGRAMME – ITALY - year 2025**

## TITLE OF THE PROJECT:

## RISCOPRIRE PER VALORIZZARE [Rediscovering to Enhance] (PTXSU0018925013867NMTX)

## SECTOR AND AREA OF INTERVENTION:

**Sector D:** Historical, artistic and cultural heritage
**Area of Intervention:** 05. Protection and enhancement of historical, artistic and cultural assets

## DURATION OF THE PROJECT:

12 months

## OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT:

The project pursues the general objective of rediscovering and enhancing the historical, artistic and cultural heritage present in the municipal contexts involved, also through actions of active involvement of the resident population and young citizens.

## ROLE AND ACTIVITIES OF THE VOLUNTEER OPERATORS:

Participation in introductory meetings and constitution of the working group; Participation in general and specific training; Study, research of information and data, census and cataloguing of assets, mapping of the censused assets, archiving of information and data, updating of information and data; Preparation of informational material (information research, text drafting and content layout) and dissemination of the material through the web, social media, setting up of information and tourist points, libraries, schools, etc.; Use of cultural venues and guided tours (opening of cultural sites, welcoming visitors, urban guided tours, cultural visits between host locations); Conception and realisation of cultural cultural *(duplication as printed)* and tourist events to accompany the visitor in discovering local history, art, culture and tradition; front-office activities; Conception and realisation of guided tours within one's own Municipality or at specific local cultural assets/sites); Realisation of cultural activities, with schools, promotional campaigns, etc.); production of educational material or material preparatory to the realisation of workshops.

Participation in monitoring activities through the use of customer-satisfaction questionnaires and/or individual and/or group meetings.

## ANY PARTICULAR CONDITIONS AND SERVICE OBLIGATIONS AND ORGANISATIONAL ASPECTS:

The volunteer operators selected for the project will be required to perform their service with commitment, diligence and a collaborative spirit, respecting the values of the Universal Civic Service and the rules established by the lead entity and the host entities. They must observe respectful behaviour towards users, colleagues, the OLPs [local project operators] and all the entity's staff, maintaining confidentiality regarding sensitive information and data learned during project activities. Service hours will be distributed over 5 days per week for 25 hours a week. The specific hours of each location will be communicated at the start of the project and agreed between the volunteer and the host entity, respecting organisational needs and the indications of the Department for Youth Policies and the Universal Civic Service. In particular, volunteer operators must accept that the rest day may not necessarily coincide with Saturday and Sunday. Flexible hours are provided for on the occasion of extraordinary project events (e.g. exhibitions, public initiatives, workshops, information campaigns) that may also take place at weekends or at times other than ordinary ones. In such cases, the rest day will be recovered on another date in the same week. During the year of service, temporary transfers may be provided to other project locations or to places where training, updating or awareness activities take place.

## CHARACTERISTICS OF THE COMPETENCES OBTAINABLE:

At the end of the service, a Certification of Competences will be issued pursuant to D.Lgs. 13/2013.

## DESCRIPTION OF THE SELECTION CRITERIA:

**a) Methodology**: To foster the recruitment phase of aspiring volunteers to be started in service, the Entity plans a publicity phase for the projects to be started through:

1) Publication of the project sheets on the entity's website, on the page dedicated to projects within the section of the site dedicated to the Universal Civic Service.
2) Posting of posters and flyers at each project implementation location and in the places most frequented by young people in the municipalities concerned by each individual project.

A second phase will be the collection of applications for participation. On completion of this phase, a first documentary analysis will be carried out to verify the presence of the admission requirements of the Selection Call in which the candidates are taking part. Any exclusion of candidates from the selection process will be communicated to the aspiring volunteers in the ways provided for by the cited Call. Once this first procedure is complete, the evaluation of the qualifications and documents submitted by the candidates will follow, giving rise to the attribution of a first score as detailed below. The selection procedure will be completed with a selection interview through which the evaluation of the candidates will be deepened. The Entity will publish on its website the complete list of candidates admitted to the selection interview, indicating the time and place where it will be held, at least 15 days before the date of the interview, and of the candidates not admitted for one of the reasons provided for by the call. Publication of the interview calendar on the entity's website will have the value of notification. The selection of volunteers will be carried out directly by the entity's staff with human resources possessing specific experience in personnel selection. Candidates must comply with the instructions given regarding the times, places and methods of the selection procedures. The Entity's staff, once the selection procedures are finished, will draw up the ranking lists for the individual projects, or for the individual project locations, in descending order of score (or in the manner provided for by the aforementioned Selection Call). The names of candidates found suitable but not selected for lack of places will also be included in the same document. The document thus completed will be sent according to the computer procedure indicated by the Helios programme. The ranking lists will be published, subject to ratification by the Presidency of the Council of Ministers – Department for Youth Policies and the Universal Civic Service, on the Entity's website.

## b) Tools and techniques used:

The tools used for the recruitment phase consist of informational and publicity material such as flyers and posters, and radio and television advertising through local broadcasters operating in the project's reference territory. The tools used for carrying out the selection procedures are:

1. Documentary and qualifications evaluation
2. Personal interview

The documentary evaluation provides for the attribution of well-defined scores to a set of variables linked to qualifications and documents submitted by the candidates and/or self-certified. The check-list has been provided in computer format with the aid of the EXCEL application, which will allow easy and correct completion, calculating in a short time the scores to be attributed to each candidate. The personal interview will take place at the project implementation locations or in any case in the provincial or regional territory in which the locations are situated. A check-list is also provided for the evaluation interview to guide the selector through the topics of the interview. The check-list has been provided in computer format with the aid of the EXCEL application, which will allow easy and correct completion, calculating in a short time the scores to be attributed to each candidate.

## c) Variables to be measured and related indicators:

The variables to be measured, and the related indicators, are the following:

1) Knowledge: evaluated through the indirect indicator of educational qualifications and directly in the interview;
2) Background: evaluated through work and/or volunteering experience and in the interview;
3) Availability for the project: evaluated in the interview;
4) Ability: evaluated, during the interview, through the resolution of a practical problem related to the project.

## d) Selection criteria

After selection, candidates will be placed along an evaluation scale expressed in hundredths resulting from the sum of the maximum scores obtainable on the following partial scales:

1) Educational qualification: maximum score obtainable 12 points
2) Work and/or volunteering experience: maximum score obtainable 28 points
3) Interview: maximum score obtainable 60 points

## e) Indication of the minimum access thresholds provided for by the system:

The minimum access thresholds of the selection system are essentially linked to compliance with minimum behavioural rules that foster the civil, professional and personal growth of the young volunteers in civic service. In addition to the admissibility requirements provided for by the Selection Call, which provide for the exclusion of unsuitable candidates, candidates who obtain a score below 36/60 (THIRTY-SIX/SIXTIETHS) in the interview evaluation sheet will not be declared suitable to perform voluntary civic service in the chosen project for which they underwent selection.

## SPECIFIC TRAINING OF THE VOLUNTEER OPERATORS:

Specific training will take place at the premises made available by the co-designing entities, as specified below: *(same locations as the general training, listed above)*

Specific training will last a total of 72 hours and will be divided into two tranches (70%-30%) of 50 and 22 hours respectively.

Starting from the **Guidelines for general and specific training** of 31 January 2023, in order to make training more effective, the following methodological approaches will be used:

1. <u>formal</u>: the trainers present in the classroom will deal with the topics in question using interactive methods, moments of discussion and comparison, availing themselves of any experts in the subject matter. The latter, besides more in-depth knowledge, will be able to give the volunteer operators testimony of their daily experiences in the reference sector.
2. <u>non-formal</u>: through group games, brainstorming, simulations, the volunteer operators will be led to reflect, individually and in groups, on the key concepts that legitimise the experience they are about to live. Peer discussion and meetings with a more emotional and introspective value contribute to the creation of those group dynamics essential to the success of the project itself.

Specific Training may be delivered "in presence" or "at a distance" and will cover the following topics:

- **Module I**: Health and safety in the workplace: basic elements of D.Lgs. 81/2008 as amended;
- **Module II**: Planning of the intervention, research methods, context analysis
- **Module III**: Basic elements of art history
- **Module IV**: Urban, artistic, archival and archaeological heritage
- **Module V**: Definition of cultural asset and types of assets; Methods of computerised cataloguing/archiving of assets, creation of a database
- **Module VI**: Types of cultural venues (churches, museums, libraries, palaces, monuments, etc…), Management of a group of visitors;
- **Module VII**: Tools for the promotion of the territory, creation of networks between actors and interventions for local development
- **Module VIII**: Animation, promotion and awareness techniques
- **Module IX**: Territorial marketing and tourism marketing, design of an itinerary
- **Module X**: Social media marketing strategies and techniques
- **Module XI**: Creation of a promotional typographic product
- **Module XII**: How to make a video
- **Module XIII**: Contents and purposes of the internal regulations for the management of the Civic Service, management forms, in-depth study of the project monitoring plan.

## CONDUCT OF A TUTORING PERIOD

→ Duration of the tutoring period: 3 months
→ Hours dedicated: 25 hours (of which 20 collective hours and 5 individual hours)
→ Timing, methods and hourly structure: The tutoring pathway will develop in the second semester of the volunteers' service.

**Collective activities** will last 20 hours in total; they will be carried out as preparation for the whole pathway and comprise three modules:
- <u>Module 1 (10 hours)</u> Presentation module and creation of the working group.
- <u>Module 2 (5 hours)</u> Module on tools for presenting oneself to the world of work.
- <u>Module 3 (5 hours)</u> Active job searching.

**Individual activities** provide 5 hours for each volunteer and will be carried out during the three months of tutoring after the group modules.

→ Tutoring activities. The obligatory activities provided for during the tutoring sessions will be:

a. the organisation of moments of self-evaluation for each volunteer and of overall evaluation of the civic-service experience, of analysis of the competences learned and implemented during the civic service;
b. the realisation of orientation workshops on compiling the curriculum vitae, also through the Youthpass tool, or, in the case of citizens of third countries legally residing in Italy, the European Commission's Skills profile tool for Third Countries Nationals, as well as preparation for job interviews, use of the web and social networks for job searching and orientation towards starting a business;
c. activities aimed at fostering in the volunteer operator knowledge of and contact with the Employment Centre and Employment Services.

In order to foster greater knowledge of financing measures addressed to young people, during the three months of tutoring an information desk on public financing for business creation will be made available to the volunteers.
