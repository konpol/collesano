## Subject: Open call to hold events at the Madonie Living Lab — Communication.

Dear all, we hereby inform you that the Madonie Living Lab, a community innovation and experimentation infrastructure based in Petralia Sottana, has opened the first edition of the public call to select **five proposals to be realised in its spaces between June and September 2026.** The call is part of the territorial programming promoted by the Unione dei Comuni delle Madonie [Union of the Madonie Municipalities], together with us, the GAL ISC Madonie, the BCC Madonie and the ISIS "G. Salerno" of Gangi, and is curated by PUSH. Informal groups, collectives, associations, professionals, researchers, schools and bodies of the territory with a concrete idea to develop may apply: workshops, talks, courses, podcasts, performances, photographic or video productions, or any other format consistent with the laboratory's mission. The MaLL makes available equipped spaces — including a fab lab, a professional kitchen, a recording room and a virtual lab — together with technical, logistical and communication support.

This is the first of a series of periodic calls: in the coming months others will follow, to support the laboratory's programming also beyond September 2026.

## Applications are open until 31 May 2026.
