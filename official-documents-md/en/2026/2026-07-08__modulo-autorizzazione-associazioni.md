# CONSENT FORM FOR THE PROCESSING OF DATA (IMAGES AND AUDIO/VIDEO) OF THE MINOR CONNECTED WITH PARTICIPATION IN THE COLLESANO SCHOOL CAMP

By signing this form

and having received the information notice from the Autonomous Data Controllers, he/she/they authorise(s):

☐ the Civil Protection Department;
☐ The Municipality of Collesano and the municipal group of civil protection volunteers

to process personal data in the ways and for the purposes indicated therein.

Signature of the parent(s)/guardian(s) ____________________

The undersigned, as identified above, pursuant to arts. 10 and 320 of the Civil Code and arts. 96 and 97 of the Law of 22 April 1941, n. 633 (Copyright Law),

authorise(s)

the aforesaid Controllers as selected above, each within its competence, to hold the photographic and video materials portraying the minor collected during the event indicated above and to use such materials for their publication and dissemination on the Controllers' websites, on the platforms dedicated to the web-streaming of the event, in magazines or brochures, or leaflets and on any other medium whatsoever intended for dissemination, including social media, also for promotional purposes of the activity carried out by the Controllers, as well as for archiving/documentation purposes of the event, with nothing to claim or complain of.

This authorisation may be revoked at any moment, by sending the revocation in writing by registered letter with return receipt to the Controllers, at their respective registered offices as indicated in the information notice, without prejudice to the lawfulness of the use of the image up to the moment of revocation. Also in that case the revocation will have no effect where the deletion/removal of the image has become impossible or in any case difficult to achieve.

The Controllers are relieved of and held harmless from any direct or indirect liability concerning damage to image or to privacy that goes beyond the obligations to which they are bound by law, or for damage caused by the improper use of the photographic/video material by the users of the sites/social media or, in any case, by third parties.

With an express prohibition on the Controllers using the collected images in ways that prejudice the personal and professional dignity and the decorum of the minor.

The use of the images and of the other audiovisual materials is to be considered permitted entirely free of charge.

Signature of the parent(s)/guardian(s) ____________________

(NB: the signature, except in the case of guardianship or parental responsibility of a single parent, must be affixed by both parents).
