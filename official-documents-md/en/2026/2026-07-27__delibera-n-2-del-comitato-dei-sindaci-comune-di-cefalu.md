**-** Acknowledgement of the reworking of the Piano di Zona (P.D.Z.) [zone plan] 2013-2015. Approval of the technical sheets and financial plans of the following actions:

1. **Action 2:** "PUA Punto Unico di Accesso" [single access point].
2. **Action 1:** "Home Assistance for the Disabled" (3rd year).

**CONSIDERING** that:

1. In recent years the district care network has recorded a strong expansion of the services linked to the support of early childhood, of non-self-sufficient elderly people, of the very severely disabled and of personalised projects.
2. The increase in services and the need to centralise citizens' access in order to overcome institutional fragmentation have generated a considerable increase in the workload of the offices concerned.
3. There is therefore an urgent need for a system-wide action aimed at filling the shortage of human resources allocated to the social PUA of District n. 33, guaranteeing efficient integration with the services of the Azienda Sanitaria Provinciale (ASP) [provincial health authority].

**HAVING EXAMINED** the reworking proposal relating to **Action 2**, entitled **"PUA PUNTO UNICO DI ACCESSO" (Support to the District Planning Office)**, which provides for:

**Reference macro-level:** Services for access and taking into care by the care network.
**Type of intervention:** Taking into care.
**Service objective:** Support for the work of the Plan Group.
**Intervention areas covered:** Family responsibilities, Disability and non-self-sufficiency, Poverty and social exclusion.

**HAVING NOTED** that the aforesaid Action aims to guarantee the centrality of the person by reducing inequalities in access to social rights by means of:

The activation of a Single Desk (physical and digital).
The introduction of the "Triage of Need" to differentiate simple cases from complex ones.
The start of the Multidimensional Assessment (UVM) and the drawing up of the Individualised Assistance Projects (PAI).
Information integration and the sharing of databases between the municipalities of the District and the ASP.

**HAVING REGARD TO** the organisational and professional arrangement identified for carrying out the activities, with an operational base located at the Planning Office of the Distretto Socio-Sanitario n. 33 of Cefalù, which will rest on two specific figures under agreement with the ETS [third-sector entities] social cooperatives entrusted with the service:

1. **no. 1 Social Worker** (for front-office activities, reception, filtering, pre-assessment and taking into care in coordination with the specialist services and the ASP).
2. **no. 1 Administrative Operator expert in monitoring and evaluation** (CED administrative officer for the management of information systems, statistical processing, recording of the LEPS, customer satisfaction and institutional coordination).

**NOTING** that the project ensures an estimated total of **1.560 hours per year** for each professional figure (calculated on an average of 30 hours per week for 52 weeks) for a single planning year.

**HAVING REGARD TO** the reworked economic-financial framework of Action 2, included in the Financial Plan of the 2nd P.D.Z., which quantifies the overall cost of the intervention at [unreadable] (inclusive of management charges at 4% and VAT at 5%), divided as follows:

**HAVING NOTED** [unreadable] within the resources assigned to the District drawn from the Fondo Nazionale Politiche Sociali (FNPS) [national social policies fund] / per-inhabitant quota and co-financing, as per the allocation plan by funding source attached;

**CONSIDERING** the further need to intervene in favour of the disabled population of the territory in the light of the updated needs analysis, which records a drastic increase in district applications for home assistance and home educational services, which rose from about 60 in 2021 to about 180 in 2024;

**HAVING NOTED** that the ordinary funds previously made available (e.g. FNA) have so far allowed partial, limited responses confined to very short periods of time;

**HAVING THEREFORE EXAMINED** the reworking proposal relating to the second line of intervention, called **Action 1**, entitled **"HOME ASSISTANCE FOR THE DISABLED (Services and measures to encourage staying at home)"**, referring to the 3rd year of the P.D.Z. 2013-2015, which provides for:

**Reference macro-level:** Services and measures to encourage staying at home.
**Type of intervention and objective:** Home assistance.
**Intervention areas covered:** Disability and non-self-sufficiency, Poverty and social exclusion.

**HAVING ESTABLISHED** that Action 1 is addressed to disabled persons within the meaning of L. 104/92 over 18 years of age resident in the 9 municipalities of District D.33, for whom the Planning Office will prepare a specific public notice and the Multidimensional Assessment Unit (UVM) will define the relevant Individualised Care Plans (PAI);

**HAVING NOTED** the types of services and integrated interventions defined by the technical sheet of Action 1:

1. **Home Assistance (SAD):** Help interventions for personal/housing hygiene and care, meal preparation, handling of paperwork and secretarial support, moral and social support.
2. **Home or group educational service (SED):** Activities at gathering centres aimed at self-care, domestic autonomy, strengthening cognitive/motor skills, money management, socialisation and expressive workshops.

**HAVING REGARD TO** the arrangement of resources and professional figures identified for the delivery of the SAD and SED services by means of social vouchers and an accreditation system (lasting one year):

**no. 1 OSA home-care assistant** (under agreement with the entrusted ETS social cooperatives) for a total of **2.400 hours**.
**no. 1 Professional educator** (under agreement with the entrusted ETS social cooperatives) for a total of **4.290 hours**.
*Integrated support* from the social workers of the 9 municipalities and the specialist staff of the ASP (child neuropsychiatrist/psychiatrist).

# RESOLVES

**to take note of and approve** the reworking of the following two lines of intervention included in the programming of the Piano di Zona of the Distretto Socio-Sanitario n. 33:

**Action 2:** *"PUA Punto Unico di Accesso"* for the total amount of [unreadable].
**Action 1:** *"Home Assistance for the Disabled (SAD and SED)"* (3rd year) for the total amount of **156.626,40**.

**to note** that the related forms, service sheets and financial plans are attached to this act to form an integral and substantial part of it.

**to note** that the coverage of the overall expenditure of the two actions is found within the resources of the Fondo Nazionale Politiche Sociali (FNPS).

**to establish** that the management of the approved services will take place in compliance with the D.Lgs. 36/2023 and the D.Lgs. 117/2017 according to the following operating rules:

**For Action 2:** Public procedure pursuant to the D.lgs. 36/2023 or D.lgs. 117/2017 (Third Sector Code) for the engagement of the professional figures supporting the Planning Office of Cefalù.
**For Action 1:** Activation of the accreditation system and the issue of social vouchers, guaranteeing the beneficiaries free choice of the managing body.

**To entrust** to the Planning Office and to the lead municipality of Cefalù the adoption of all subsequent management acts, including the preparation of the public notices for the beneficiaries of home assistance and the activation of the quarterly/ongoing monitoring provided for by the projects.

**To transmit** a copy of this resolution to the competent regional department and to the municipalities of the District.
