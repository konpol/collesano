# MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

Metropolitan City of Palermo

## ELECTORAL OFFICE

## MUNICIPAL ELECTORAL COMMISSION

## MINUTES n° 2

## Subject: APPOINTMENT OF POLL CLERKS (SCRUTATORI) FOR THE REFERENDUM CONSULTATIONS OF 22 AND 23 MARCH 2026.

On the TWENTY-SEVENTH day of the month of FEBRUARY of the year 2026, at 11:00, in the Municipal Palace, duly convened by note prot. 3007/2026, the Municipal Electoral Commission met in the persons of:

| | Office | Role |
|---|---|---|
| CASCIO Tiziana | Mayor | President |
| CASCIO Maria Candida | Municipal Councillor | Full member |
| MACALUSO Mario | Municipal Councillor | Full member |

Municipal Councillor BARTOLONE Maurizio, full member, is absent for work reasons.

The Head of the Municipal Electoral Office, Rinella Filippo, delegated for the purpose by the Municipal Secretary, D.ssa Patrizia C. Sferruzza, attends as recording secretary.

The meeting having been recognised as lawful, pursuant to art. 14 of Consolidated Law 20/03/1967, n. 223, as amended by art. 26, paragraph 5, of Law 24 November 2000, n. 340, the President invites those present to proceed with the appointment of the poll clerks needed for the referendum consultations indicated in the subject.

## THE MUNICIPAL ELECTORAL COMMISSION

in public meeting

**Whereas** the electoral assemblies have been convened for the conduct of the referendum consultations indicated in the subject. **Having regard to** art. 6 of law 8 March 1989, n. 95, as replaced by art. 9, paragraph 4, of law 21 December 2005, n. 270 and by law 22/2006, which provides that between the twenty-fifth and the twentieth day before the date set for voting, the Municipal Electoral Commission, in public meeting, announced two days beforehand by a publicly displayed poster, in the presence of the list representatives, if designated, proceeds:

a) with the appointment of the poll clerks, for each polling section of the Municipality, choosing them from among the names in the Register of poll clerks in a number equal to that needed (for the Municipality of Collesano n. 3 for each normal polling section and n. 0 for the special section);
b) with the formation of a ranking list of further names from the aforesaid Register, to replace the poll clerks appointed under letter a), in the case of withdrawal or impediment.

**Having regard to** the fact that the electorate of this Municipality is divided into FOUR polling sections. **Having noted** that as of today the Register of poll clerks comprises n. 421 electors suitable to perform the functions of polling-section clerk and that its size allows all the needed poll clerks to be appointed. **Having noted** that it is necessary to proceed:

a) with the appointment of n. 12 titular poll clerks to be assigned to the n. 4 polling sections, of which n. 0 to the special section;
b) with the formation of a ranking list of a further n. 12 names, from the aforesaid Register, to replace the titular poll clerks, in the case of possible withdrawal or impediment.

**Having taken note** that on 25/02/2026 the poster announcing this public meeting was made public. **Having regard to** the prefectural provisions issued on the matter.

## All this premised

# ORDERS

**To proceed**, with a computer program of random draw:
- with the appointment of n. 12 titular poll clerks, to be assigned to the 4 normal sections and n. 0 clerks to be assigned to the special polling station, choosing them from among the names in the specific municipal Register;
- with the formation of a ranking list of a further n. 12 names, in order of appointment, from the aforesaid Register, to replace the appointed titular clerks, in the case of possible withdrawal or impediment.

**To note** that:
- the electors appointed **titular poll clerks**, assigned to the individual sections, are listed **in annex 'A'**, which constitutes an integral and substantial part of these minutes;
- the electors appointed **substitute poll clerks** are listed **in annex 'B'**, which constitutes an integral and substantial part of these minutes.

**To notify,** within the terms established by law, the appointment of the chosen poll clerks, as established by art. 6 of law 8 March 1989, n. 95 as amended by art. 9, paragraph 4, of law 21 December 2005, n. 270.

A copy of these minutes is transmitted to the Prefect of Palermo, to the Public Prosecutor at the Court of Termini Imerese and to the President of the District Electoral Commission of Termini Imerese.
