To the Mayors of the Municipalities of the GAL ISC MADONIE

## To the members of the Board of Directors

## To the private-sector members

## AT THEIR OFFICES

Castellana Sicula, 07/04/2026 – Prot. n. 154/26

SUBJECT: **Promotion and sales initiatives at Mercato San Lorenzo-Palermo**

With great pleasure, we inform you that the GAL ISC Madonie [local action group] has recently signed a three-year memorandum of understanding with **Sanlorenzo Mercato S.r.l.** of Palermo, establishing collaboration in the participatory planning and organisation of events and activities in the <u>historical-cultural, environmental and tourism</u> fields, as well as in the <u>promotion, protection and conservation of the food and wine traditions of the Madonie territory</u>.

The GAL and Sanlorenzo Mercato, within their respective institutional purposes, by signing the protocol undertake to foster the development of the territory and the enhancement of its peculiarities, proposing among other things:

a) the promotion and realisation of initiatives or activities intended to attract visitors to the territory and to foster the growth of the community;
b) the promotion and realisation of important recreational events, tourist itineraries, food and wine tours, musical and artistic activities, etc;
c) the promotion and enhancement of typical local traditions;
d) the promotion of local craftsmanship, commerce and all services in general;

Those interested in taking part in the initiatives to be co-planned are invited to complete the attached form and send it by 20 April 2026 to the following address: <u>galiscmadonie@gmail.com</u>.
