## DRAFT CONTRACT FOR THE CONCESSION OF USE

## OF A BUILDING BELONGING TO THE DISPOSABLE ASSETS OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO LOCATED IN C/DA DRINZI AND IDENTIFIED IN THE CADASTRE AT SHEET 38 PARCEL 784 WITH THE ADJOINING EXTERNAL AREA

By this private deed, valid for all legal purposes, between the parties:

1. **The Municipality of [Municipality Name]**, (hereinafter referred to as the "Ente" or "Grantor"), with its seat in [Address], C.F. [tax code], represented by the Manager/Head of the Heritage Sector, [First name and Surname], authorised to sign this act in execution of Managerial Determination n. [N.] of [Date];
2. **The Company / Sole Proprietorship [Firm/Company Name]**, (hereinafter referred to as the "Concessionaire"), with registered office in [Address], C.F./P.IVA [Number], represented by the Legal Representative pro tempore [First name and Surname], born in [Place] on [Date];

## WHEREAS:

- The building located in Collesano in c/da Drinzi, identified in the Building Cadastre at Sheet 38, Parcel 784, belongs to the **disposable assets** of the municipality;
- The municipality has carried out a public-evidence procedure started with the public notice of a tender procedure for the award in concession of use and management of the municipal building for commercial and catering activity on _______ [Date] and concluded with the award in favour of the Concessionaire by Determination n. [N.] of [Date];

## IT IS AGREED AND STIPULATED AS FOLLOWS:

## Art. 1 - Subject of the contract

The municipality grants in use to the Concessionaire, who accepts, the building described in the preamble. The Concessionaire declares that he has taken detailed note of the state of the premises through a specific site visit and finds it fully suitable for the agreed use.

## Art. 2 - Intended use

The building must be used **exclusively for the performance of commercial and catering activity**. It is absolutely forbidden to change the agreed intended use or to use the premises for other purposes, on pain of termination by law of this contract pursuant to art. 1456 of the civil code.

## Art. 3 - Duration of the concession

The concession has a mandatory duration of **9 (nine) years**, starting from [Start Date] and with a non-extendable expiry on [End Date]. As this is an asset of the disposable patrimony granted for specific purposes, the contract will cease at expiry without the need for notice of termination. Any automatic or tacit renewal is excluded.

## Art. 4 - Concession rent and payment methods

The annual concession rent, determined following the tender procedure, is equal to **€ [Amount] ([Amount in words]/00 euros)**, plus VAT if due.

- Payment must be made in two six-monthly instalments in advance, the first by 31 January and the second by 31 August, through the municipality's payment systems. *(so in the text; the notice indicates 31 July)*
- The rent will be updated annually, starting from the second year, to the extent of 100% of the variation in the ISTAT index.

## Art. 5 - Expenses, utilities and taxes

All management expenses, utility consumption (electricity, gas, water) and local taxes linked to the use of the building (e.g. TARI) are entirely at the expense of the Concessionaire. The Concessionaire shall arrange the transfer or new activation of the utilities in his own name within 15 days of signing.

## Art. 6 - Maintenance and modifications to the premises

- **Ordinary maintenance**: Falls entirely to the Concessionaire, who is required to keep the premises in a perfect state of conservation and cleanliness. In addition, all expenses that may become necessary for the issue of the prescribed administrative and health authorisations fall to the concessionaire.
- **Extraordinary maintenance**: Remains at the expense of the municipality, except in the case of expenses and/or damage connected with the use of the building or that may become necessary due to the failure to carry out ordinary maintenance, or due to negligence or fault or responsibility or improper use or neglect of the structure or of the external appurtenant area.
- **Modifications and improvements**: The Concessionaire may not carry out any work of modification, addition or renovation without the prior written authorisation of the municipality. Authorised improvements will remain acquired to the property of the municipality at the end of the concession, without any right to indemnities.

## Art. 7 - Prohibition of sub-concession and assignment

The Concessionaire is absolutely forbidden to assign this contract or to sub-grant the building, even partially or in the form of free loan for use, to third parties, on pain of immediate termination of the relationship.

## Art. 8 Surety Or Insurance Guarantee

As a guarantee of the exact fulfilment of the contractual obligations and of the payment of the rent, the Concessionaire delivers to the municipality, at the moment of signing, a **surety policy [bank / insurance]** equal to **€ _______________** (for an amount equal to the annual rent offered, rounded up to the nearest thousand euros). This guarantee must continue uninterruptedly for the entire duration of the contract and must expressly provide, obligatorily on pain of exclusion:

- **Waiver of the benefit of prior enforcement** against the principal debtor referred to in art. 1944 of the civil code;
- **Waiver of the exception referred to in art. 1957, paragraph 2, of the civil code**: The validity of the surety remains effective for the entire duration of the contract and until the complete satisfaction of the obligations, independently of the expiry terms for the judicial action of the municipality.
- **Payment on simple written request**: The guarantor undertakes to pay the sums requested by the municipality within the maximum term of 15 (fifteen) days from receipt of a simple written request, with waiver of any exception or opposition ("first demand" clause).
- **Reinstatement of the surety**: Should the municipality collect, partially or totally, the guarantee due to breaches by the Concessionaire, the latter shall be obliged to reinstate the original amount of the surety within the peremptory term of 20 (twenty) days from the communication of the withdrawal, on pain of immediate termination of the contract for just cause.

The surety must in any case continue to have effect until a specific release communication is made by the beneficiary administration, with which the absence or the settlement of every possible exception and dispute arising in connection with the execution of the contract will also be attested. Failure to establish the aforesaid guarantee results in the revocation of the concession.

## Art. 9 Insurance Obligations Of The Concessionaire

The concessionaire is liable for damage that may occur to users of the service, to third parties or to things in the course of the performance of the activities and attributable to the fault of his own operators or deriving from irregularities and/or deficiencies of the services.

The concessionaire relieves the municipality of Collesano from any action that may be brought against it by third parties for the non-fulfilment of the obligations provided for by the contract or for carelessness or fault in their non-fulfilment. The concessionaire delivers to the municipality an insurance policy, with a leading company, against the following damage:

- Fire: tenancy risk and every other risk of total or partial loss of the building portion;
- Third-party liability (RCT) for civil liability relating to the exercise of his activity, for any possible damage to third parties;

The insurance policy covering the aforesaid risks must carry adequate maximum amounts according to the prudent assessment of the Concessionaire himself, who has the obligation to guarantee its validity and effectiveness for the entire duration of the concession contract. The liability of the Concessionaire for damage exceeding the insured maximum amounts remains unaffected. In the event of cancellations and annulments of the aforesaid policies, the concessionaire must produce new insurance policies with at least the same maximum amounts and conditions. The policy/policies must contain an express waiver of the right of subrogation by the concessionaire's insurer against the Municipality of Collesano, as well as their insurance companies. The concessionaire must deliver in advance to the municipality a copy of the policy/policies and of the relevant conditions, which must be to the latter's satisfaction and, where it raises exceptions or advances requests for specific covers, these must be immediately insured, and in any case the concessionaire must deliver annually to the municipality a copy of the receipt of payment of the insurance premium.

## Art. 10 Breaches – Revocation Of The Concession

The concession in question may be revoked, for needs of public interest or in the presence of serious causes of force majeure, on the express and unquestionable request of the Municipality of Collesano with written notice to be sent to the concessionaire by PEC. In the event of revocation of the concession, the Municipality of Collesano may demand the immediate return of the asset in addition to compensation for any damage. The building must be returned free and clear of persons and/or things. If this is not done within the term set, once 30 days have passed from that term, the things present inside the premises will be taken to a landfill and the restoration costs will be charged to the holder of the concession.

The concession is subject to a use that does not cause damage to the building and its appurtenances, that does not disturb public quiet, and that does not conflict with the regulations in force on public security, hygiene and building matters. The concessionaire may withdraw from the contract unilaterally with at least 6 months' notice.

## Art. 11 - Termination of the Contract

The municipality reserves the right to terminate the contract pursuant to art. 1456 of the civil code in the event of:

1. Non-payment of the rent after the tolerance period of 30 days has elapsed;
2. Unauthorised change of the intended use;
3. Sub-concession or assignment of the contract to third parties;
4. Serious violations of hygiene-health or safety regulations in the exercise of the activity.

## Art. 12 - Competent court

For any dispute deriving from the application, interpretation and execution of this contract, the parties indicate as the exclusive competent court the Court of Termini Imerese. Read, approved and signed by the parties. Collesano, _________________
