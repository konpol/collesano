### OF SELECTION FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF THE EVALUATION UNIT (NUCLEO DI VALUTAZIONE) IN SINGLE-MEMBER COMPOSITION FOR THE MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO FOR THE TWO-YEAR PERIOD 2026-2028.

CONSIDERING that the Evaluation Unit: -performs, within each administration, a fundamental role in the process of measurement and evaluation of structures, organisational positions and managers, and also in the fulfilment of the integrity and transparency obligations placed on administrations: -contributes to adopting the measurement and evaluation system, guaranteeing the correctness of the annual performance measurement and evaluation process as a whole in relation to its processes and, in turn, to presenting an evaluation for management; -supports the political-administrative body in the definition of strategic objectives, fostering their coordination, and in the identification of responsibilities for those objectives; -contributes to aligning staff behaviour with the organisation's mission. To this end, the Evaluation Unit helps make the performance objectives of the various structures of the Authority evident and facilitates communication on the state of their achievement; -validates the planning, programming and control system, preparatory to the payment of merit-related supplementary economic treatments, guaranteeing the correctness of the measurement, evaluation and reward processes; All this premised and considered, it

## GIVES NOTICE

That a public notice is called for the appointment of the Evaluation Unit in single-member composition with an external member for the performance of the tasks and functions provided for by D. Lgs. n. 150/2009, chosen in the manner specified in this notice

#### ART. 1 COMPOSITION AND APPOINTMENT

- The Evaluation Unit is a single-member body composed of a single external member;
- It is appointed by the Mayor by his/her own measure from among the persons who have submitted their candidature
- The choice is made by evaluation of the curricula submitted.
- The provisions of art. 7, paragraph 6, of D.lgs. n. 165/2001 do not apply;

#### ART. 2 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS – GROUNDS OF INCOMPATIBILITY

- Being Italian citizens or citizens of one of the Member States of the European Union;
- Enjoying civil and political rights;
- Age not over 65 years;
- Holding a degree (old system) or specialist degree or master's degree in Economics, Law, Economic and Statistical Sciences, Political Science. Possession of recognised equivalent qualifications issued in other European Union countries may be considered;
- Having a good knowledge of software and computer programs;
- Possessing intellectual, managerial and relational abilities, given the role of promoter of continuous improvement in the Authority as well as of transparency and integrity.

The following **may not be appointed** members of the Evaluation Unit:

- Those who hold elective public offices or positions in political parties or trade-union organisations, or who have continuous collaboration or consultancy relationships with the aforesaid organisations, or who have held them in the three years preceding the designation;
- Those who have performed political-direction assignments or held elective public offices at the Municipality of Collesano in the three years preceding the appointment;
- Those who have been convicted, even by a judgment not yet final, of the offences provided for by Chapter I of Title II of Book II of the Criminal Code;
- Those who are in a situation of conflict, even potential, of their own interests, of the spouse, of cohabitants, of relatives by marriage up to the second degree;
- Those who have a relationship of marriage, cohabitation, kinship or affinity up to the second degree with first-tier managers in service in the administration, or with the political or administrative leadership, or in any case with the political-administrative direction body;
- Those who are responsible for corruption prevention at the Municipality;
- Those who are members of the boards of directors of companies in which the authority holds shares, or who have held such a position in the three years preceding the appointment;
- Those who fall within the cases of non-conferability and/or incompatibility under D. Lgs. 39/2013;
- Judges or State attorneys who perform their functions in the same regional or district territorial area in which the Municipality operates;
- Those who have carried out, not episodically, professional activity in favour of or against the Municipality;
- Those who are Auditor of Accounts at the Municipality;
- If public-administration employees, those who have received a disciplinary sanction more serious than a written reprimand/censure;
- Not having been removed, for cause, from the position of member of the evaluation unit before the end of the mandate; *(so in the original text)*
- Those who incur the cases of incompatibility and ineligibility provided for Auditors of Accounts under article 236 of D. Lgs. 267/2000;
- Those who are retired

#### ART. 3 – FUNCTIONS

1. The Evaluation Unit performs its activity in single-member form and answers directly to the mayor.
2. In the exercise of its functions, it may request information and/or documents from the offices and may carry out checks. It reports to the mayor, indicating, by area, service or office and taking account of the progress of administrative activity with reference to the assigned objectives, any delays and/or deviations. It formulates proposals to, where appropriate, bring administrative activity into line with the programmatic objectives.
3. For the performance of its functions, the unit may avail itself of the support of the municipal offices.
4. The Evaluation Unit performs the following activities:

a) It carries out, after hearing the Mayor, the annual evaluation of the Elevate Qualificazioni [senior qualification positions] (formerly Heads of Organisational Position), ascertaining the actual achievement of the planned objectives, for the purposes of paying the results allowance, as established by the current evaluation and incentive system;
b) It carries out the evaluation of the organisational performance of the Municipality and of the organisational units into which the administrative structure of the Authority is divided;
c) It proposes to the Executive the definition and updating of the methodology for grading organisational positions;
d) It submits for the Executive's approval the permanent methodologies for evaluating organisational positions and the remaining employed staff;
e) It verifies the adoption and implementation of the permanent evaluation system for Sector Heads and employed staff and of the internal control systems under art. 147 et seq. of D. Lgs. 267/2000;
f) It collaborates with the Area Heads in the evaluation of the municipal employees assigned to them, guaranteeing the correctness of the processes of measurement, evaluation and award of staff bonuses, in compliance with the principle of valuing merit and professionalism as well as rewards;
g) It collaborates with the Administration and with the Heads for the organisational and managerial improvement of the Authority;
h) It performs any other function provided for by legislative or regulatory provisions;

#### ART. 4 APPOINTMENT - DURATION OF THE ASSIGNMENT - ECONOMIC TREATMENT

The Evaluation Unit is appointed by the Mayor following public selection by evaluation of curricula. The Evaluation Unit remains in office for a period of **TWO** years, running from the date of the mayoral decree of appointment, and in any case until the completion of the evaluation of the last reference year. The Mayor may revoke the assignment before its expiry only for adequately documented failures. The Evaluation Unit ceases from office upon expiry of the mandate, voluntary resignation and upon the occurrence of the incompatibility grounds provided for. The single member of the Evaluation Unit is granted an all-inclusive annual remuneration of € 1.500,00 gross of tax withholdings, VAT and social-security charges.
