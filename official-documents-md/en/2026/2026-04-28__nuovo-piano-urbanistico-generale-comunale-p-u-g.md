# AVVISO PUBBLICO [public notice]

## NEW GENERAL MUNICIPAL URBAN PLAN (P.U.G.)

The Municipal Administration, having started the procedure for the formation of the New General Municipal Urban Plan (P.U.G.), in observance of art.6 of L.R. 19/2020 [regional law], according to which, within the formation of plans, there must be guaranteed "the participation of all public and private entities as well as associations and organisations, whether natural or legal persons, through the active listening to needs, public debate on the general objectives, the widest publicity of the planning acts and documents, the possibility of submitting observations and proposals for modification, ensuring the timely and adequate examination of the related submissions through their acceptance or their reasoned non-acceptance", and in compliance with the procedure for the formation and approval of the PUG pursuant to art.26 paragraph 3 of L.R. 19/2020,

## INVITES

all public and private entities and the representatives of the professional orders and colleges who, by their specific competences, are interested in the plan, to take part in the public debate which will be held at the Council Chamber on

## 6 May at 15.30.
