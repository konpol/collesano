# Subject: Public Notice «Ripresa Sicilia Plus» - Promotion of collaborative research and technology transfer - Communication.

The Notice is intended to facilitate the implementation of investment projects within the framework of Priority 1 "A more competitive and smarter Sicily" of the ERDF Sicilia 2021-2027 Programme.

The objectives are: developing new knowledge and technologies (and exploiting existing ones) to support technology transfer and the advancement of experimentation processes (relating to new or improved products or services); strengthening the innovative capacity of enterprises (also using scientific knowledge to translate it into innovative products or processes); improving the competitiveness of the regional production system; fostering the implementation of the regional Smart Specialisation Strategy (S3) 2021-2027.

The projects must guarantee effective collaboration between enterprises and research organisations, provide for the industrial exploitation of the results and pay particular attention to the principles of sustainable development, the green economy, the blue economy and processes centred on the circular economy.

All financed interventions must be implemented in coherence with the smart specialisation areas provided for by the S3 Sicilia 2021-2027.

The financial endowment available for the operations selected under this notice amounts to **126.141.452,00 euros.**

## Recipients / Beneficiaries

Enterprises of any size and their aggregations, already established or yet to be established:
- Large Enterprises (LEs) (only if in collaboration with MSMEs);
- Micro, Small and Medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs);
- Mid-Caps (Enterprises other than SMEs referred to in art. 5 of EU Reg. 2021/1058).

Research organisations, public or private (according to the definition of art. 2 of EU Reg. 651/2014).

Research infrastructures, Innovation Poles and Testing and experimentation infrastructures (according to the definitions of art. 2 of EU Reg. 651/2014).

### Methods of participation

Applications must be completed and sent via the dedicated **IT platform**, from 29 September until 29 October [of this year].

Proposing parties must have a certified e-mail address (**PEC**), a **digital signature** kit compliant with the requirements of the national Body for the Digitalisation of the Public Administration in the public list of certifiers (www.digitpa.gov.it/firme-elettroniche), and a **SPID** digital identity (level 2) or **CNS/CIE**.
