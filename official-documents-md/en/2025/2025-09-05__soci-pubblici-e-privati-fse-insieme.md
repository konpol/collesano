# Subject: Notice n. 27/2025 PR ESF+ Sicilia 2021/2027 INSIEME - Social INvestments for the Integration through Education, sport and culture of Minors at risk of Exclusion – Communication.

Dear Sirs, we inform you that the Notice n. 27/2025 in question has been published on the website of the Department of Family and Social Policies of the Sicilian Region.

With this notice, the Sicilian Region intends to promote social investments to support minors between 6 and 16 years of age belonging to family units at risk of social and labour exclusion, in order to increase the opportunities for participation in initiatives of cultural and social growth with the objective of:

- promoting the empowerment, sociality and, more generally, the physical, psychological, cultural and social well-being of minors;
- increasing the opportunities of access to family-work reconciliation services for family units, in particular those most at risk of exclusion.

The notice finances projects aimed at:

- increasing opportunities for minors at risk of exclusion;
- accessing educational, social, sporting and cultural initiatives;
- preventing social and psychophysical hardship, through moments of aggregation, development and social inclusion;
- organising activities involving the Third Sector entities operating in the territory, as well as the other relevant actors of the educating community (through the possible establishment of a territorial support network).

The following may participate: Third Sector Entities enrolled in the National Single Register (RUNTS) which:

- have their registered and/or operational office in Sicily;
- have at least 2 years of experience in similar projects, financed by a public administration.

**The project must involve a primary or secondary school (lower or upper) as a non-onerous external partner.**

The Notice makes available a total of € 31.924.210,00.

Each project may receive a maximum contribution of:

- € 250.000,00 if it involves at least 50 minors.
- € 125.000,00 if it involves at least 25 minors.

and must include at least two of the following activities, one of which must compulsorily be the sporting activity:

1) Activities for individual development and the fight against educational poverty.
2) Artistic and creative activities.
3) Sporting activities.
4) Territory discovery pathways.
5) Pathways for the development of legality and active citizenship.

**The deadline for the submission of applications is the 60th day following the opening of the information system. The opening date will be communicated later on the website of the Sicilian Region and on www.sicilia-fse.it.**

To consult the publication of the aforementioned Notice, click on the following link: https://www.sicilia-fse.it/avvisi-e-bandi/pr-fse-2021-2027/avviso-27-2025#testo-integrale-dell-avviso
