# Subject: Call "SRD03 – Investments in agricultural holdings for diversification into non-agricultural activities – exemption regime" - Communication.

Dear Sirs,

this is to inform you that the 40-million call to incentivise the diversification of the activities of agricultural holdings and to foster economic growth and sustainable development in rural areas, countering the depopulation of these areas, has been published on the website of the Regional Ministry of Agriculture.

These are interventions aimed at the creation and enhancement of agritourisms, social farming, and educational and didactic activities.

## Specifically:

*[summary sheet embedded as an image — text recovered with OCR:]*

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | The intervention is aimed at incentivising investments for business diversification activities that foster economic growth and sustainable development in rural areas, also contributing to improving the territorial balance, both in economic and social terms |
| **Types of eligible investments** | Investments for the creation, enhancement and development of the following type of connected agricultural activity pursuant to article 2135 of the Civil Code: a) agritourism; b) social farming; c) educational/didactic activities; d) processing of agricultural products into products not included in Annex I of the TFEU (and possibly of a minority share of products included in Annex I) and their working and marketing in farm sales outlets. |
| **Beneficiaries** | Agricultural entrepreneurs, individual or associated, with the exclusion of entrepreneurs engaged exclusively in forestry and aquaculture activities. |
| **Type of payment** | Reimbursement of eligible costs actually incurred by the beneficiary |
| **Support rate** | Rates: 60%: small enterprises · 50%: medium-sized enterprises · 40%: large enterprises |
| **Form of support** | Capital grant |
| **Financial endowment** | For all types of investment Euro 40.000.000,00. The endowment may be increased with additional resources |
| **Person responsible for the procedure** | Director of Service 3 – Multifunctionality and diversification in agriculture |
| **Contacts** | PEC: dipartimento.agricoltura@certmail.regione.sicilia.it |
| **Application submission period** | 05 November 2025 – 05 February 2026 |
