# Subject: Public notice "Open Innovation Sicilia" – Communication.

By DDG n. 1843 of 08/07/2025, the Department of Productive Activities approved the public **Notice** "**Open Innovation SICILIA**", and the related forms, concerning the methods and procedures for the granting and disbursement of the benefits provided for by **Action 1.1.3 "Creation and strengthening of dedicated spaces for the promotion of innovation"** in implementation of the PR ERDF Sicilia 2021/2027.

Parties configurable as **Innovation Poles** pursuant to article 2, point 92 of Regulation (EU) n. 651/2014 as amended, in possession of the requirements provided for by the Notice, may submit an application for access to the benefits as Proposing Parties.

**The acceleration and/or incubation programmes proposed by the Proposing Parties (Innovation Poles), aimed at supporting the birth, development and growth of new innovative entrepreneurial initiatives**, consistent with the priority areas of the Regional Strategy for Smart Specialisation (S3) Sicilia 2021–2027, are eligible for financing.

The following may participate as Adhering Parties:

- MSMEs established no more than 60 months ago (for acceleration programmes);
- Teams of natural persons or aspiring entrepreneurs not yet constituted as an enterprise (for incubation programmes).

The Notice and the related documentation attached to it can be viewed:

- on the institutional website of the Department of Productive Activities at: https://www.regione.sicilia.it/istituzioni/regione/strutture-regionali/assessorato-attivita-produttive/dipartimento-attivita-produttive
- on the institutional website of the ERDF Sicilia 2021-2027 Programme (www.euroinfosicilia.it)
- on the IT platform for completing the application for benefits, whose address will be communicated by the regional Administration.

The financial endowment available for the operations selected under this notice amounts to **9.548.472,00 euros.**

Applications for access to the benefits must be sent starting from 12:00 noon on 02/09/2025 until 12:00 noon on 31/10/2025 in the manner provided for by the Notice and exclusively through the dedicated IT platform.
