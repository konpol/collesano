## OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

# NOTICE TO THE CITIZENS — RODENT CONTROL IN MUNICIPAL PREMISES

The citizens are informed that, following Mayoral Ordinance n. 156 of 31/12/2025, on **5 January 2026** the premises of the municipal offices will be closed in order to carry out a **rodent-control intervention and consequent cleaning of the premises.**

Non-deferrable services will continue to be guaranteed to the citizens through the on-call system. The following essential services for the community will continue to be ensured, in the manner indicated:

- Administrative Area-Service 3: limited to birth declarations and death declarations, and the issue of urgent and non-deferrable identity documents, through the on-call system of the assigned staff: Rinella Filippo – tel. 3498550437;
- Technical-Maintenance Area: the Maintenance Service will ensure its activity, for urgent cases, through the on-call system of the assigned staff;
- Administrative Area-Service 8: the Surveillance and Municipal Police Service will continue to carry out its activity;
- Administrative Area – Environment Office: the Waste Collection Service will be provided regularly;

Activities will resume regularly on **7 January 2026**.

The operation will be carried out by a specialised company, in compliance with the regulations in force on safety and the protection of public health.

THE MAYOR — *Dott.ssa Tiziana Cascio*
