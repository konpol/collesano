**INFORMATION NOTICE ON DATA PROCESSING**
*(arts. 13 and 14, Regulation 2016/679/EU – General Data Protection Regulation – GDPR and art. 13 of Legislative Decree n. 196 of 30/06/2003 as amended)*

In relation to the personal data processed by the Administrative Area of the Municipality of Collesano, for the management of this procedure, pursuant to Regulation 2016/679/EU (*General Data Protection Regulation* – GDPR) and of art. 13 ["del dell'art. 13", text as printed] of Legislative Decree 30.6.2003 n. 196 (hereinafter, the "*Privacy Code*"), you are informed that:

## 2. PURPOSE AND LEGAL BASIS OF THE PROCESSING

The purpose of the processing for which the personal data are intended is the execution of the services specified in this contract. The data collected may also be processed for archiving purposes (protocol and document storage) as well as, in aggregate form, for statistical purposes.

## 3. METHODS OF PROCESSING

The processing of personal data is based on the principles of correctness, lawfulness and transparency, protecting the confidentiality of the data subject and his/her rights.

The data will be processed with paper and/or electronic/computer tools, in compliance with the principles of Legislative Decree 196/2003 and EU Regulation 2016/679 and in observance of the provisions of law or regulation to fulfil the obligations and powers of local authorities. The processing of data takes place with the adoption of security measures suitable to minimise the risks of destruction, loss, unauthorised access or processing not permitted or not conforming to the purposes of the processing itself, on servers located within the European Union belonging to the Controller and/or to third-party companies engaged and duly appointed as Data Processors on the basis of a contract or other legal act. The data will not be transferred outside the European Union.

## 4. SCOPE OF COMMUNICATION

The data provided to the Municipality of Collesano are processed by authorised internal staff and may be communicated to public bodies by virtue of regulatory obligations and to the authorities for the performance of the purposes inherent in and consequent to the procedure to which they refer. The data will not be disseminated. Any publication takes place in the cases and within the limits provided for by the legislation in force.

## 5. DATA RETENTION

Pursuant to article 5, para. 1, lett. e) of Regulation 2016/679/EU, the data will be kept in conformity with the rules on the retention of paper/digital data/documents of the public administration.

## 6. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

The data subject (that is, the natural person to whom the personal data refer) is entitled to the rights provided for by Regulation 2016/679/EU and, in particular, may request access to the personal data concerning him/her, their rectification, supplementation or, where the conditions are met, the limitation of the processing, or object to their processing. He/she has the right to lodge a complaint, pursuant to article 77 of Regulation 2016/679/EU, with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Italian Data Protection Authority) with its seat in Piazza di Monte Citorio n. 121, 00186 – ROME, E-mail ["E-mal", text as printed]: garante@gpdp.it, PEC: protocollo@pec.gpdp.it, or with another competent European supervisory Authority. The provision of the data is necessary for the correct conduct of the examination. The data subject is obliged to provide the personal data.
