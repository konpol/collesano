# APPLICATION FOR ENROLMENT IN THE SINGLE REGISTER OF PERSONS SUITABLE FOR THE OFFICE OF POLLING-STATION TELLER

**(Art. 9, law 30 April 1999, n. 120)**

To the MAYOR of the Municipality of Collesano

**SUBJECT**: Request for enrolment in the single Register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station teller.

The undersigned ………………………………………………………………………………
Born in ................................................................................ on ........................................ resident in ..................................................... in Via/Corso …............................................................. n. ........

considering that, by public notice posted in November of this year, the voters of this Municipality were invited, if interested, to submit a special application to be enrolled in the Register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station teller referred to in art. I of law 8 March 1989, n. 95 [text as printed; art. 1], as amended by art. 9 of law 30 April 1999, n. 120,

## REQUESTS

to be included in the aforesaid Register, being available to perform the functions of teller.

To this end, he/she declares:

a) to be a voter of the Municipality;
b) to have fulfilled compulsory schooling;
c) not to be in the conditions referred to in art. 23 of the consolidated law on the composition and election of the bodies of municipal administrations, approved by D.P.R. 16 May 1960, n. 570, and in art. 38 of the consolidated law containing rules for the election of the Chamber of Deputies, approved by D.P.R. 30 March 1957, n. 361 *.

Collesano, _____________________

> _________________________________
>
> (The applicant)

A photocopy of an identity document is attached (Only if not submitted in person at the desk).

___________________

\* Pursuant to art. 23 of the consolidated law on the composition and election of the bodies of municipal administrations and art. 38 of the consolidated law ["dei testo unico", text as printed] containing rules for the election of the Chamber of Deputies, the following categories of persons may not exercise the functions of President of a sectional electoral office, teller or secretary:

a) those who, at the date of the elections, have passed their seventieth year of age;
b) employees of the Ministries of the Interior, of posts and telecommunications and of transport;
c) members of the Armed Forces on duty;
d) provincial doctors, health officers and district doctors;
e) municipal secretaries and employees of the Municipalities assigned or seconded to serve at the municipal electoral Offices;
f) candidates in the elections for which the voting is held
