# Document 1 — Notice (Avviso.pdf)

# MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

# NOTICE

## SINGLE REGISTER OF PERSONS SUITABLE FOR THE OFFICE OF POLLING-STATION TELLER

### THE MAYOR

- Having regard to art. 3 of law 8 March 1989 n. 95, most recently replaced by art. 9, paragraph 3, of law 30 April 1999, n. 120, which provides for the posting of a special notice to invite anyone who wishes to apply for inclusion in the single municipal register of polling-station tellers;
- Having regard to Circular of the Ministry of the Interior n. 166/99 of 13 September 1999, on: "Register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station teller, art. 9 of Law 30 April 1999, n. 120";
- Having regard to the circular of the Ministry of the Interior — Directorate-General of Civil Administration — Central Directorate for electoral services — 3 February 2000, n. 9/2000;
- Considering that all voters in possession of the requirements provided for by the aforesaid law n. 95/1989 may be included in the said register, upon application;

### MAKES KNOWN THAT

All voters of the Municipality who wish to be included in the single municipal register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station teller must submit a special application to the Mayor **BY THE END OF NOVEMBER 2025**.

Enrolment in the aforesaid register is subject to possession of at least the compulsory-education qualification.

Pursuant to the provisions of articles 38 of D.P.R. 30 March 1957, n. 361, and 23 of D.P.R. 16 May 1960, n. 570 and subsequent amendments, the following are excluded from the functions of teller of a sectional electoral office:

1) employees of the Ministries of the Interior, of posts and telecommunications and of transport;
2) members of the Armed Forces on duty;
3) provincial doctors, health officers and district doctors;
4) municipal secretaries and municipal employees assigned or seconded to serve at the municipal electoral offices;
5) candidates in the elections for which the voting is held.

The application must be completed on the special forms available at the Electoral Office and obtainable on the website http://www.comune.collesano.pa.it/ and delivered to the Authority's Protocol Office or sent to the e-mail address: servizidemografici@comune.collesano.pa.it

It is further specified that voters already included in the single register of persons suitable for the office of polling-station teller need not submit a new application.

Voters already enrolled in the register may request, by next December, to be removed for serious, justified and proven reasons.

Electoral Office contacts:
Email: servizidemografici@comune.collesano.pa.it
PEC: anagrafe.comunecollesano@sicurezzapostale.it
Tel: 0921 664749

Collesano, 10/11/2025

THE MAYOR — Tiziana Cascio
