# Subject: New separate waste collection service - Delivery of equipment for the users served by Door-to-Door Collection

Dear Fellow Citizen,

we have a great objective before us: reaching 80% Separate Waste Collection.

It is not just a percentage: it means concrete savings for everyone and, above all, a cleaner, safer and healthier environment for our community.

After the distribution of the information brochures, the delivery phase of the new equipment begins.

30-litre bins (mastelli) are available for the collection of "plastic" and for the collection of "residual dry waste". Each bin will be assigned exclusively to users registered with the Municipality, belonging to the urban centre and using the door-to-door collection service.

All bins are equipped with RFID tags, which make it possible to verify the correct disposal of waste. The distribution of the bins will take place at the municipal warehouse located in via G. Meli, from 09:00 to 13:00 and from 14:00 to 17:00, from 3 to 8 November 2025.

To collect the bins it is necessary to bring the latest TARI (waste tax) bill and a valid identity document of the bill holder. In the case of a proxy, it is necessary to present the proxy signed by the holder of the TARI position, a copy of the document of the holder of the TARI position, and an identity document of the delegate.

We also inform you that the K-Tarip app is active and can be downloaded free of charge on smartphones: inside you will find the collection calendars, the instructions for separating waste correctly and all the useful information to stay up to date.

Also available are the Facebook page: Caruter.Collesano, the e-mail: segnalazioni@caruter.it, and the freephone number: 800-956686.

We count on everyone's collaboration: separating waste well means paying less and living better.

The Mayor
Dott.ssa Tiziana Cascio
