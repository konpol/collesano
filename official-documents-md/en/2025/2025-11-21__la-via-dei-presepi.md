**Diocese of Cefalù**

Prot. 12/2025 — Cefalù, 25 October 2025

To the Mayors of the Municipalities of the Diocese of Cefalù

Dear Mayors,

the Diocese of Cefalù and the Pro Loco Cefalù APS invite you to take part in the "Diocesan Nativity Scene Exhibition" which, as usual, will be the cornerstone of the Christmas event "La Via dei Presepi" [The Way of the Nativity Scenes]. A happy occasion to strengthen the synergic network between the Municipalities of the Diocese and an opportunity to express one's cultural identity through the nativity-scene tradition.

Trusting in your collaboration, we ask you to make available a Nativity Scene representing your Municipality, taking into account the following indications:

- the work must be original (with respect to those presented in previous events);
- provide the work ["elabotato", text as printed] with a support and, to cover the latter, apply red satin along the perimeter of the base of the Nativity Scene, including the back, reaching down to the floor;
- use natural-light cables to illuminate the Nativity Scene;
- supply the necessary electrical material (power strip, triple plugs, extension cords and anything else);
- ensure that the Nativity Scene stands without the aid of walls and that it is tidy also at the back, so that it can be fully visible.

Please communicate your adhesion to the event by no later than Tuesday 18 November of this year, writing to the email address prolococefalu@gmail.com or contacting us at n. 3271965559. It is important that you also indicate the name of the nativity-scene maker, his/her telephone number and the dimensions of the Nativity Scene.

It is specified that participation presupposes the exhibition of the work for the entire duration of the initiative; therefore it will not be possible, for any reason, to withdraw the Nativity Scene before the end of the exhibition.

The delivery of the Nativity Scenes must be made at the Church of the SS.ma Trinità, Monumental Complex of San Domenico, located in Via Costa n° 63 (place of the exhibition) and may take place from 28 November to 30 November 2025 at the following times: 09:00 — 12:00 / 15:00 — 17:00. On Saturday 10 January and Sunday 11 January 2026, observing the same times, collection will be possible. Access to the church is via a wide staircase, reachable with small vehicles (car, "Porter").

Awaiting your kind reply, we extend our most cordial greetings.

The Vicar General — [signature, unreadable]

The President — [signature, unreadable]
