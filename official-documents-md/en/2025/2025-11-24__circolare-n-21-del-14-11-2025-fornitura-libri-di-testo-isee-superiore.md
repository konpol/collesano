# Document 1 — Circular n. 21 of 14/11/2025

*Working copy with protocol number*

## Regional Ministry (Assessorato) of Education and Vocational Training
## Department of Education, Universities and the Right to Education

Service 5 Right to Education Tel 091 7074575 – 7074582 - 7074584 dipartimento.istruzione@certmail.regione.sicilia.it

## Annexes: 2

Subject: Regional law 22/10/2025 n.31 - Art. 31 - paragraph 1 - Contribution for the free and semi-free supply of textbooks (Law 448/98 – art. 27) – Procedures for the submission of applications school year 2025/2026 ISEE from € 10,632.95 to € 13,000.00

To the Mayors of the Municipalities of Sicily through the Metropolitan Cities and the Free Municipal Consortia — THEIR OFFICES

With Circular n. 15 of 29/08/2025, this Department has already outlined the procedures for the disbursement of the state allocations intended for the free and semi-free supply of textbooks to students of lower and upper secondary schools, state and state-recognised, whose family unit has an equivalent economic situation indicator (ISEE), currently valid, equal to or lower than € 10,632.94.

*[protocol stamp repeated on the pages: "Arrival: AOO CMPA, N. Prot. 00081913 of 14/11/2025 — 7.v, 3.v, 1.v, 7.v, 1/2025A/CMPA.fra, CMPA.arm, 1.nd, 4.a"]*

Regional law 22/10/2025 n.31 "Budget variations of the Region for the financial year 2025 and for the three-year period 2025-2027", in particular art. 31, paragraph 1, has allocated, for the year 2025, the sum of € 4,000 thousand euros to supplement the sums already assigned by the State to the Region, for the purposes referred to in art. 27 of law 23/12/1998, n. 448 for the free and semi-free supply of textbooks to pupils of lower and upper secondary schools (Mission 4, Programme 2).

In view of the above, the beneficiaries of this intervention are, exclusively, students of lower and upper secondary schools, state and state-recognised, whose family unit has an equivalent economic situation indicator (ISEE), currently valid, from € 10,632.95 up to a maximum of € 13,000.00.

**Valid ISEE certificates must be taken into consideration, that is, all those that do not bear any "annotation" (omission/discrepancy), under penalty of exclusion.**

If the declarant is not in possession of the relevant I.S.E.E. certificate, he/she must enter, in the application for participation, the protocol number and the date of submission of the DSU (Single Substitutive Declaration); it will be the responsibility of the local authorities to subsequently acquire the certificate through the INPS. The Municipalities, upon receipt of the Circular, shall take care to post a copy of it on their own notice board (Albo), as well as to transmit a copy of it, with the application for participation attached (annex 2), **to all the school institutions (lower and upper secondary schools) located in their territory.**

The application for the contribution, formulated using the attached application template, must be accompanied by the following documents:

**1) Photocopy of the identity document of the person applying for the benefit (father, mother or guardian), currently valid.**
**2) Tax code of the applicant.**
**3) Photocopy of the certificate of the Equivalent Economic Situation Indicator (I.S.E.E.), currently valid.**

Those applying for the benefit shall not attach any expense documentation relating to the purchase of the textbooks, but must keep a copy of it for at least 5 years and exhibit it upon request of the Administration, aware of the forfeiture of the benefits obtained following untruthful declarations and that false acts are punished pursuant to the Criminal Code and the special laws on the matter (*46 and 47 of D.P.R. 28 December 2000, n° 445 and* art. 71 – 445/00).

The amount of the contribution will be determined by the writing Department and differentiated by class attended; therefore, the Municipalities must communicate the number of beneficiary students, using exclusively the template attached hereto, as follows:

**Lower secondary school:**
- number of beneficiary students attending the first class
- number of beneficiary students attending the second class
- number of beneficiary students attending the third class

**Upper secondary school:**
- number of beneficiary students attending the first class
- number of beneficiary students attending the second class
- number of beneficiary students attending the third class
- number of beneficiary students attending the fourth class
- number of beneficiary students attending the fifth class

The application for participation must be submitted, exclusively, to the school institution attended, **by no later than 15 December 2025 (The date of 17 October 2025 indicated in Circular n. 15/2025 is replaced by the date of 15 December 2025)**

The school institutions will handle the receipt of the applications for participation and will transmit them to the Municipalities of residence, **by no later than 15 January 2026 (The date of 19 November 2025 indicated in Circular n. 15/2025 is replaced by the date of 15 January 2026)**

The Municipalities, after appropriate checking of the documentation received, must transmit the number of beneficiary students, using exclusively the template attached hereto (Annex 1), to the Regional Department of Education, Universities and the Right to Education – Service 5 Right to Education **EXCLUSIVELY by PEC and in PDF format**, to the following address: dipartimento.istruzione@certmail.regione.sicilia.it

**In order to allow the department to proceed with a single distribution of the sums (state and regional), the transmission of the data by the municipalities must take place by no later than 31 March 2026 and must contain the total number of beneficiary students relating both to this Circular and to Circular n. 15 of 29/08/2025. (The date of 13 March 2026 indicated in Circular n. 15/2025 is replaced by the date of 31 March 2026)**

## The following communications from the Municipalities (annex 1) will not be examined:

- those received without regular protocol and signature;
- those not in PDF format;
- those received after the **peremptory deadline of 31 March 2026**.

**If there are no beneficiaries, the Municipality must nevertheless communicate this, again by PEC (using annex 1), by 31 March 2026**

In view of the above, it is communicated that no notice will be given of the erroneous or missed transmission of the requested data. It is also clarified that, having received the requested data by **31 March 2026**, the Department will proceed to draw up the distribution plan of the total sum, excluding the non-compliant Municipalities, since the missed or "dardiva" ["dardiva", text as printed; "tardiva" = late] transmission of the necessary data would inevitably ["inevitalbilmente", text as printed] cause the delay of the payments also in favour of those Municipalities that have complied with the deadlines indicated in this Circular. The Department of Education, Universities and the Right to Education, on the basis of the number of pupils, will determine the amount of the contribution, differentiated by class attended, and will credit the sums in favour of the Municipalities.

## The Municipalities, when disbursing the contribution to the beneficiaries, must comply with the following:

- the amount of the contribution must not exceed the figure indicated in the application by the person applying for the benefit and, in any case, must not exceed the cost of the textbooks adopted for the class attended;
- in the event that the student is repeating a year and enrols at the same school institution and in the same course of study, he/she may request the contribution only for textbooks different from those of the previous year.

**The method of payment of the contribution remains entrusted to the Municipalities, either by issuing a book voucher to be spent exclusively at bookshops and stationery-bookshops with ATECO CODE 4761 and 4762, or by direct disbursement in favour of the beneficiaries of the contribution.**

Considering that, with the new ISEE level, the Department cannot estimate in advance the number of additional applications that will be received, a distribution on account will be made with the resources of the financial year 2025 and a final distribution in the 2026 financial year, until the funds are exhausted. Should a financial insufficiency arise, the Department will proceed with the distribution, recalculating the indices for each class, until the resources are exhausted.

The Administrations receiving the sums must:

- report on the disbursement made within and no later than 180 days from the date of crediting of the contribution;
- return the sums not used, as not due to the beneficiaries, pursuant to the provisions laid down by the writing Department with **CIRCULAR N° 7 of 08/05/2025.** Of the payment made, the Municipality must give immediate communication to this Department – Service 5 Right to Education - specifying the law, the amount and the school year to which the return refers, by PEC to the following address: dipartimento.istruzione@certmail.regione.sicilia.it

The Circular and the related annexes will be published in the NEWS of the Regional Department of Education, Universities and the Right to Education on the website www.regione.sicilia.it

Interested parties are asked to comply as strictly as possible with the deadlines indicated in this Circular for the submission of the requested documentation, in order to allow the writing Department to proceed with the preparation of the distribution plan and the consequent payment of the sums assigned to each Municipality within the first half of 2026.

Pursuant to art. 71 et seq. of D.P.R. 28 December 2000, n° 445, the Bodies responsible for the implementation of the intervention are required to "*carry out suitable checks, including random checks, and in all cases in which well-founded doubts arise, on the truthfulness of the substitutive declarations referred to in arts. 46 and 47*".

Annexes:

1. Data communication template for the Municipalities s.y. 2025/2026
2. Application template for those applying for the benefit s.y. 2025/2026
