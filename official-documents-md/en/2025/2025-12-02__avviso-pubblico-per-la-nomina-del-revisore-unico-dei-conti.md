## PUBLIC NOTICE FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF THE SOLE AUDITOR OF ACCOUNTS

- Having regard to the Statute and the regulations of the Gal Isc Madonie.
- Having regard to the resolution of the Members' Assembly of 26/09/25, on the basis of which only certain specific administrative aspects, principles and operating methods of the legislation in force for local authorities are borrowed [art. 6 letter paragraph 1 and 2 of regional law n. 17 of 11/08/16, art. 39 of regional law n. 16 of 11/08/17 amending some parts of art. 10 of regional law 17 March 2016 n. 3 as replaced by article 6 of regional law 11 August 2016 n. 17], combining them with the statutory provisions in force.
- Having regard to art. 10 of regional law 17 March 2016, n. 3, which, among other things, provides: "…….In municipalities with a population of up to 5,000 inhabitants and in unions of municipalities, the economic-financial audit is carried out by a single auditor of accounts"
- Having regard to the provisions of paragraph 1 of art. 6, Regional Law 11/08/2016, n. 17.
- Having regard to the provisions of paragraph 2 of art. 6, Regional Law 11/08/2016, n. 17, which establishes: that the auditors of accounts of local authorities are chosen by drawing lots from among those enrolled in the register of statutory auditors referred to in legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39, as well as among those enrolled in the order of chartered accountants and accounting experts; the requirements that professionals must possess (divided into bands); the methods of choosing professionals (drawing of lots)

**GIVEN** the need to proceed with the renewal of the audit body by entrusting the assignment to a suitable person

**CONSIDERING** that the appointment of the Board of auditors of accounts is the exclusive competence of the Members' Assembly

**DEEMING IT NECESSARY** to proceed with the publication of a special public notice, on the website of the Gal Isc Madonie and on the websites of the member Municipalities, for the submission of applications for participation by professionals resident or having their tax domicile in the Municipalities belonging to the Gal Isc Madonie (In detail: See annex 1) and enrolled in the register of statutory auditors referred to in legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39, as well as in the order of chartered accountants and accounting experts

## Considering that

- in the light of the legislative amendment:
  - In municipalities with a population of up to 5,000 inhabitants and in unions of municipalities, the economic-financial audit is carried out by a single auditor of accounts;
  - The public component of the membership of the Gal Isc Madonie consists of 34 Municipalities and can therefore be configured as a Union of Municipalities;
  - each auditor of accounts may not hold more than four assignments at the same time, and any such incompatibility must be declared at the time of submitting the application for participation in the procedure for choosing the audit body of the local authority;
  - the appointment of the economic-financial audit body must be made by drawing lots;
  - the economic-financial audit body will be chosen from among those who have submitted an application and demonstrate possession of the requirements referred to in point 4) below.
- the auditor to be appointed must not be in the conditions of incapacity referred to in art. 2382 of the civil code, nor in the following situations of incompatibility or ineligibility: grounds of ineligibility and forfeiture referred to in art. 2399 of the civil code, "administrators" meaning the members of the Board of Directors of the Gal Isc Madonie; being a member of the Board of Directors of the Gal Isc Madonie; holding assignments or consultancies at the Gal Isc Madonie or at bodies or institutions dependent on or in any case subject to its control or supervision; having held the office of Auditor in the two-year period preceding the appointment;
- the functions and responsibilities of the audit Body itself are not compatible with the performance of the function of tax consultant held within the Gal Isc Madonie.
- The Members' Assembly, at its meeting of 26/09/2025, approved the draft Public Notice and mandated the legal representative to carry out the necessary and consequent acts.

## Art. 2 — Functions and responsibilities

The audit body performs the following functions:

a) collaboration with the Board of Directors in accordance with the provisions of the statute and the regulations;
b) opinions on:
1) any economic-financial programming instruments;
2) any proposed budget;
3) proposals for the establishment of and/or participation in external bodies;
4) proposals for recourse to borrowing;
5) proposals for the use of innovative finance instruments, in compliance with the rules in force on the matter;
6) proposals for the recognition of settlements;
7) proposals for accounting and procurement regulations;
c) supervision of the accounting, financial and economic regularity of the management with regard to the acquisition of revenues, the making of expenditures, the administration of assets, the completeness of documentation, tax fulfilments and the keeping of accounts; the audit body performs these functions also with reasoned sampling techniques;
d) report on the final statement of the annual management. The report contains the attestation of the correspondence of the statement to the results of the management, as well as observations, considerations and proposals aimed at achieving efficiency, productivity and economy of management;
e) report to the council body on serious management irregularities, with simultaneous denunciation to the competent judicial bodies where hypotheses of liability arise;
f) cash checks (on a quarterly basis).

It is specified that the opinions referred to in letter b) of paragraph 1 express a reasoned judgement of congruity, coherence and accounting reliability of the budget forecasts and of the programmes and projects. The opinions suggest to the Board of Directors the measures suitable to ensure the reliability of the approaches. The opinions are compulsory.

The Board of Directors is required to adopt the consequent measures or to adequately justify the failure to adopt the measures proposed by the audit body.

In order to guarantee the fulfilment of the functions described above, the audit body has the right of access to the acts and documents of the Gal Isc Madonie and may participate in the Members' Assembly for the approval of any budget and of the management statement. It may also participate in the other assemblies of the council body and in the meetings of the Board of Directors.

The audit body is provided, by the Gal Isc Madonie, with the means necessary for the performance of its tasks, as established by the statute and the regulations and by the relevant legislation in force.

The audit body may entrust collaboration in its function, under its own responsibility, to one or more persons having the requirements referred to in article 234, paragraph 2. The related fees remain borne by the audit body.

The audit body is answerable for the truthfulness of its attestations and fulfils its duties with the diligence of an agent. It must also maintain confidentiality about the facts and documents of which it has knowledge by reason of its office.

The following is specified:
- Activity carried out by the Gal Isc Madonie: The Gal Isc Madonie (a LEGAL ENTITY OF PRIVATE LAW, MIXED PUBLIC/PRIVATE ["DIRITTTO", text as printed], enrolled in the register of private legal persons) is the delegated body for the implementation, at territorial level, of the Actions of the territorial rural development instruments (PSP 2023-2027) with consequent: Administrative activity (Board and related minutes); Examination of applications for contributions under calls with subsequent funding decrees, control and verification activity; Procedures for the award of supplies/services/assignments with related controls and verifications; Accounting and administrative activity relating to supplies of goods/provision of services/professional assignments/subordinate and para-subordinate work; Accounting and administrative activity concerning relations with the membership and the partnership.

## DECLARES

pursuant to arts. 46 and 47 of DPR n. 445/2000:

- to have been enrolled for at least 10 years in the register of statutory auditors referred to in legislative decree 27 January 2010, n. 39, as well as among those enrolled in the order of chartered accountants and accounting experts;
- to have held at least two assignments as auditor of accounts, each lasting three years;
- to have obtained, in the year preceding that of the publication of the notice, at least 10 training credits for having participated in training courses and/or seminars on public accounting and the economic and financial management of territorial authorities;
- to have residence or tax domicile in the Municipalities belonging to the Gal Isc Madonie (In detail: See annex 1);
- not to be in the conditions/situations/grounds of ineligibility and forfeiture established by art. 2382 of the civil code;
- not to be in the conditions of ineligibility referred to in art. 2399 of the civil code;
- not to be a member of the Board of the Gal Isc Madonie;
- not to have taken on assignments ["assunto possono incarichi", text as printed] or consultancies at the Gal Isc Madonie or at bodies or institutions dependent on or in any case subject to its control or supervision;
- not to have held the office of Auditor in the two-year period preceding the appointment;
- not to hold more than four assignments at the same time;
- not to have criminal convictions by final judgment nor pending criminal proceedings;
- not to have been excluded from the active electorate in Italy and/or in the states of belonging or origin;
- to be in full enjoyment of civil and political rights in Italy and/or in the states of belonging or origin;
- Not to have been:
  a) removed, forfeited or dispensed from employment at a public administration for persistent insufficient performance, or dismissed following disciplinary proceedings;
  b) declared forfeited from public employment pursuant to art. 127, letter d) of D.P.R. 10 January 1957, n. 3 and subsequent amendments and additions;
- not to have committed offences from which derives, as an accessory penalty, the incapacity to contract with the public administration;
- that there are no grounds of forfeiture, suspension or prohibition in relation to him/her as provided for by article 67 of the code of anti-mafia laws and prevention measures, referred to in legislative decree September 2011, n. 159 [text as printed], or an attempted mafia infiltration referred to in article 84, paragraph 4, of the same code;
- not to have received the disqualification sanction referred to in article 9, paragraph 2, letter c), of legislative decree 8 June 2001, n. 231, or another sanction entailing the prohibition to contract with the public administration, including the disqualification measures referred to in article 14 of legislative decree 9 April 2008, n. 81;
- not to be affected by judicial liquidation or by states of compulsory liquidation or arrangement with creditors;
- not to have committed serious violations, definitively ascertained, of the obligations relating to the payment of taxes and duties or social security contributions;
- not to have ongoing disputes with the Gal Isc Madonie;
- his/her willingness to participate in the selection in question, undertaking to comply with all the legislative and regulatory provisions, including of a technical nature, in force at the time of the appointment and those that may enter into force during the duration of the assignment;
- to accept the automatic condition of forfeiture of the appointment should one or more declarations made in the application for participation prove untruthful upon verification;
- to accept, without reservation or condition, having taken full knowledge of them, all the conditions and provisions indicated in the selection Notice;
- to undertake, without any reservation, in the event of conferral of the assignment, to carry it out in accordance with all the conditions, methods, prescriptions, clauses and anything else provided for by the relevant legislation in force;
- to undertake, in the event of award of the assignment, to eliminate any ground(s)/condition(s)/situation(s) of incompatibility within the times and in the manner provided for by the legislation in force. Under penalty - in the event of failure to eliminate it - of the failure to definitively award the assignment.
