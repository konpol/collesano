## Subject: PR ERDF Sicilia - Action 2.1.2 "Energy requalification in enterprises" – Notice published.

Dear Sirs, we hereby inform you that yesterday the notice in question was published by the regional Department of Productive Activities, aimed at facilitating the implementation of investment projects aimed at reducing energy consumption and climate-altering gas emissions of enterprises active in the regional territory of Sicily.

Micro, small and medium-sized enterprises of the private sector (MSMEs), individually or in association, that have a production unit located and operational in the territory of the Sicilian Region and that are validly constituted and registered as active for at least three years in the Register of Companies and have approved and filed at least three financial statements or equivalent documents, according to their own legal system and relevant tax regimes, may submit an application for access to the benefits of this Notice.

For the purposes of this Notice, investment programmes aimed at the energy efficiency of already existing local/production units are eligible, through the implementation of interventions on the buildings, plants, machinery and equipment used in the production processes and/or in the provision of services, as well as the construction of plants for the production of energy from renewable sources for mere self-consumption, aimed as a whole at the reduction of at least 30% of energy consumption and of direct and indirect greenhouse gas emissions with respect to the ex-ante values recorded in the relevant local/production units.

In detail, the eligible types of intervention are listed below:

## A. Energy efficiency interventions on the production processes carried out within the local unit subject to investment:

Power factor correction; Introduction/replacement of motors, pumps, inverters, compressors or refrigeration units according to the best technologies available on the market; Insulation compatible with the production processes; Recovery of process heat from furnaces, transformers, motors or other equipment; Steam cycle optimisation systems; Replacement of heating/cooling systems with systems powered by condensing gas boilers or with systems powered by high-efficiency heat pumps; Replacement of traditional water heaters with heat-pump or solar-collector water heaters for the production of domestic hot water, whether or not integrated in the building's heating system; Building automation systems and control systems for lighting and internal air conditioning by means of timers, sensors of brightness, presence, movement, concentration of humidity, CO2 or pollutants; Systems for the management and monitoring of energy consumption; intelligent and integrated systems of remote control, regulation, management and optimisation of energy consumption and polluting emissions.

**B. Energy efficiency intervention on buildings**: Insulation of the opaque envelope of the building: external/internal side walls (e.g. thermal cladding) or those in contact with unheated areas, including roofs, that is, all dispersing opaque surfaces; Replacement of windows and frames and/or glazed surfaces; Lighting efficiency systems; Passive air-conditioning systems (shading systems, solar irradiation filtering, storage systems, solar greenhouses, natural light transport systems, etc.);

## C. Replacement of plants and machinery with new and more efficient ones located inside the company buildings referred to in letter A.

## D. Plants for the production for self-consumption of energy from RES used in the production processes and/or the provision of services:

installation of high-efficiency cogeneration/trigeneration plants; photovoltaic plants with or without storage; mini-wind plants located inside the production unit; solar thermal or thermodynamic plants; hydroelectric plants; geothermal plants; biomass plants; other plants for the production of energy from RES (e.g. tidal, wave, marine currents).

The total eligible cost for each investment programme applying for the benefits must not be less than 50,000.00 euros and not more than 500,000.00 euros.

The aid is granted to enterprises in the form of plant grants: up to a maximum of 60% of the eligible expenses and up to a maximum of € 300.000,00 per single enterprise; up to a maximum of 60% for Micro and Small Enterprises, 50% for Medium-sized Enterprises.

The Notice and the related documentation attached to it can be viewed on the institutional website of the Department of Productive Activities at: https://www.regione.sicilia.it/istituzioni/regione/strutture-regionali/assessoratoattivitaproduttive/dipartimento-atti [link as printed, truncated in the original]

**Applications for access to the benefits must be sent starting from 12:00 noon on 16/12/2025 and until 12:00 noon on 21/01/2026.**
