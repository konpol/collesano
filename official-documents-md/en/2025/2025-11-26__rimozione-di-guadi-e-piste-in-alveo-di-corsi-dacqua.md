CONSIDERING that on 28/10/2025 and 05/11/2025 specific meetings were held with the Civil Engineering Services of Trapani and Palermo, respectively, from which it emerged that the hydraulic authorisations pursuant to art. 93 et seq. of Royal Decree n. 523/1904 and the river state-property concessions issued by those same Services did not concern permanent ford crossings of watercourses;

CONSIDERING that the presence, maintenance and/or use of any tracks and fords in riverbeds induce the use of such improper and unsafe routes, with consequent exposure of the population to serious dangers and risks for public and private safety, constitute an obstacle to the regular flow of river waters and an increase in hydraulic danger conditions, and constitute unlawful occupation of areas belonging to the river water state property;

CONSIDERING that the presence of tracks and fords in riverbeds may be a contributing cause of the accumulation of foreign bodies at nodes and intersections with hydraulic works of the river network, entailing pressure on embankments up to their consequent rupture, as well as actual obstructions and reductions of the hydraulic section causing floods;

CONSIDERING that the presence of tracks and fords in riverbeds may cause hydraulic anomalies due to the accumulation of debris upstream of the crossing and erosion of the bed downstream of it;

CONSIDERING that works for crossing the beds of public watercourses may be authorised by this Authority and must be built in compliance with the specific sector legislation (chap. 5.1.2.3 of the Technical Standards for Construction approved by Ministerial Decree of 17/01/2018, chap. C5.1.2.3 of Circular 21/01/2019, n. 7 of the Superior Council of Public Works, "Technical directives for the verification of hydraulic compatibility of bridges and crossings" and "Technical directives for the verification of hydraulic compatibility of culverting and covering of watercourses" approved on 29/03/2022 by Decrees of the Secretary General of the Basin Authority n. 71 and n. 72, respectively);

CONSIDERING that all the tracks and fords existing within riverbeds and floodplain areas, falling within the territories of the addressee Municipalities, which do not concern temporary internal site roads of works duly authorised by this Authority, are irregular, highly dangerous and entail a high risk for public safety;

to the Mayors of the addressee Municipalities

# O R D E R S

- All the tracks and fords existing within riverbeds and floodplain areas, falling within their respective territories, which do not concern temporary internal site roads of works duly authorised by this Authority, must be promptly removed, at the care and expense of the public and private parties identified as responsible for their construction, maintenance and improper use.

- All the actions and interventions necessary to identify the offenders, and those aimed at prohibiting the transit of persons and vehicles to protect the safety of citizens, must be prepared and implemented.

- The absolute prohibition of transit of persons and vehicles, pending the above removal, to protect the safety of citizens, in the event of weather events and in the event of the issue of criticality/alert notices linked to meteo-hydrogeological and hydraulic phenomena of any danger level, and the adoption of the interventions to prevent the use of tracks and fords existing within riverbeds and floodplain areas, placing non-removable obstacles to the related transit of persons and vehicles.

The Mayors, in their capacity as Government Officials pursuant to Legislative Decree n. 267/2000 and as territorial civil protection Authorities limited to the structures belonging to or dependent on their respective administrations pursuant to Legislative Decree n. 224/2018, in the meantime, pursuant to art. 12 of Legislative Decree n. 1/2018, are responsible for the adoption of the necessary contingent and urgent measures and for the activation of the related procedures for the protection of private and public safety, especially on the occasion of alert notices for meteo-hydrogeological and hydraulic risk of any level issued by the Civil Protection.

It is requested that this ordinance be published on the Albo Pretorio [official notice board].
