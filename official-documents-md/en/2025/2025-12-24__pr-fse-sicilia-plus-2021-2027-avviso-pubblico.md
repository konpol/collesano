# UNION OF MADONIE MUNICIPALITIES

## PUBLIC NOTICE

## NON-COMPETITIVE PUBLIC PROCEDURE FOR THE IDENTIFICATION OF THIRD SECTOR ENTITIES AVAILABLE FOR THE CO-DESIGN AND PARTNERSHIP MANAGEMENT OF THE WELCOMING INSERTION PLAN OF THE MADONIE INNER AREA.

#### Definitions

The following definitions are provided for a better understanding of the content of this act.
- **CTS**: Third Sector Code, approved by Legislative Decree n. 117/2017;

- **Proceeding Administration (AP)**: Union of Madonie Municipalities, as identified by virtue of Priority 5 of the PR FSE+ Sicilia 2021/2027, the body holding the public procedure of co-design and partnership management (co-management), in compliance with the principles of law n. 241/1990 and subsequent amendments on administrative procedure;
- **Third Sector Entities (ETS)**: the entities indicated in art. 4 of Legislative Decree n. 117/2017, containing the Third Sector Code;
- **Application for participation and declarations of possession of requirements**: the application submitted by the ETS to participate in the co-design and partnership management procedure;
- **Project proposal**: the project document submitted by the ETS, in the manner provided for by the Notice, subject to evaluation by a special Commission appointed by the proceeding Administration;
- **Welcoming Insertion Plan (PIA)**: the project document aimed at facilitating and supporting the transfer and inclusion of family units;
- **Co-design Table**: the forum designated for carrying out the co-design activity for the implementation of the project activities, aimed at the – shared – development of the definitive project of the Welcoming Insertion Plan between the EAP and the Union of Madonie Municipalities;
- **Definitive project**: the definitive project document proposed by the ETS after the award phase, which must be approved by the proceeding Administration;
- **Implementing partner entities (EAP)**: one or more Third Sector entities (ETS), individual or associated, whose project proposal… *[text truncated in the PDF text layer]* procedure for the implementation of the activities and interventions subject to co-design;
- **Agreement (Convenzione)**: the act to be signed between the person responsible for the AP and the implementing partner entities (EAP), which is the entity responsible for the co-design and co-management resulting from this procedure (Annex 2);
- **Person responsible for the procedure**: the person indicated by the proceeding Administration as the Person responsible for the procedure pursuant to law n. 241/1990.

### ART. 1 — Subject of the co-design and co-management and purpose of the procedure

This Notice is addressed to the ETS interested in taking part in the co-design Table of the interventions provided for the shared design and subsequent management of the PIA, as described in Priority 5 of the attached PR FSE+ Sicilia 2021/2027.

### ART. 2 — Duration

The collaboration Agreement, resulting from the co-design, will run from the signing of the special Agreement between the AP, as beneficiary, and the EAP, and will be effective for the entire duration of the PIA, including the economic reporting activities.

### ART. 4 — Participation requirements

The Notice is addressed to entities which, at the date of submission of the application, possess, under penalty of exclusion, the following requirements, self-certified by means of the application (Annex 1):

a) Third Sector entities as defined by art. 4 of the Third Sector Code (Legislative Decree 117/17), enrolled in the RUNTS at the date of publication of this notice;
b) having their registered and operational office on Italian territory;
c) being fiscally resident in Italy.

In the case of groupings, it is compulsory to indicate specifically the member(s) and the related services provided within the co-design and co-management. Participating ETS must also guarantee that the persons employed in any capacity in the performance of the activities possess professional requirements adequate to their performance.

That being said, participants must possess, at the date of publication of this Notice, the following requirements:

**4.1 - General requirements**:
- qualification as Third Sector Entities as identified by art. 4 of the Third Sector Code (Legislative Decree 117/2017) and regularly enrolled in the RUNTS at the date of publication of this notice;
- absence of grounds preventing contracting with the Public Administration referred to in arts. 94 and 95 of Legislative Decree 36/2023, applied by analogy to this procedure where compatible, and of any other preclusive grounds under the provisions in force;
- not being in a condition of incapacity to contract with the public administration or in any other situation considered by law prejudicial to or limiting contractual capacity;
- not being subject to insolvency/bankruptcy proceedings/compulsory administrative liquidation;
- not being in a condition of inactivity at the Register of Companies (if an enterprise);
- being in compliance with the obligations imposed by Law n. 68 of 1999;
- being in compliance with the payment of social security contributions (DURC) and VAT declarations;
- having carried out in the last three years, under direct management or in partnership, at least one initiative pertinent to the activities attributable to the PIA;
- Third Sector Entities submitting an expression of interest must also be in compliance with the insurance position of volunteers and the contribution and welfare position of employees and collaborators.

**4.2 – Special requirements**

**4.2.1 - Professional suitability**: ETS established for at least 3 years by public deed or authenticated or registered private deed, regularly enrolled in the RUNTS, or social enterprises/social cooperatives enrolled in the dedicated section of the Register of Companies.

**4.2.2 - Economic-financial suitability**:
A) Economic-financial solidity proven through suitable declarations of at least one Bank or an Intermediary authorised pursuant to Legislative Decree n. 385/1993, which may not be self-certified;
B) for entities that cannot fulfil the requirement referred to in point A) above, a copy of the last three financial statements proving the solidity of the Entity and, in any case, any further act and/or document deemed useful to prove possession of the indicated requirement.

**4.2.3 - Technical-professional capacity**:
A) Having carried out in the last 2 years (preceding the publication of this Notice) activities and interventions analogous to those covered by this procedure; having carried out the activities in question in collaboration with public Bodies and/or public administrations will be a priority qualification;
B) guaranteeing the employment of staff with the professional qualifications and experience for the management of the required activities (the CVs of each operator must be attached). Possession of the requirements referred to in this point, with the related activities entrusted to each operator, must be self-declared by the *pro tempore* legal representative of the applicant pursuant to D.P.R. n. 445/2000 and subsequent amendments.

**4.3 Grounds for exclusion of participants in groupings**: Without prejudice to the possession by all members of the aggregation of the general requirements (art. 4.1), the special requirements must be possessed in majority measure by the ETS designated as Group Leader/Agent.

### ART. 5 — Methods of participation

**5.1 – Documentation attached to the Notice**

The following documentation is an integral and substantial part of this Notice:
- Annex 1: Template of the application for participation and declaration of possession of requirements;
- Annex 2: Expression of interest for the selection of the Inner Areas;
- Annex 3: Draft Agreement.

The ETS intending to participate in the procedure in question undertakes to carefully examine each of these documents. The ETS taking part in the procedure accepts the provisions contained in this notice and the related attached documentation.

**5.2 – Methods and deadlines for participating**

Interested parties must submit an application for participation by PEC to the address unionemadonie@pec.comeg.it, under penalty of forfeiture, **by no later than 30/01/2026** (a deadline appropriate in relation to the activities required of participants), producing the following documentation (in Italian):
- Application for participation and declaration of possession of requirements according to Template 1, digitally signed by the legal representative of the interested Entity.
- Project proposal drawn up starting from the Expression of interest for the selection of the Inner Areas, which:
  a) must follow the order of the elements and evaluation criteria referred to in art. 6.2 below;
  b) must indicate the resources, in various capacities, made available to the partnership, which will be subject to evaluation pursuant to the aforementioned art. 6.2.

Incomplete or conditional documentation, as well as documentation produced after the above deadline, will not be taken into consideration. The following wording must be indicated in the subject of the PEC: PIA CO-DESIGN PROCEDURE APPLICATION FOR PARTICIPATION.

**Participation in multi-entity composition**

In the case of participation in multi-entity composition, the application for participation and declaration of possession of requirements (as per the attached Template) must be digitally signed by all the Legal Representatives of the members of the established or to-be-established aggregation, without prejudice to the possibility of expressly delegating this task to the Legal Representative of the Entity designated as Group Leader/Agent of the aggregation, as resulting from the irrevocable mandate conferred by the mandating Entities. The project proposal must be digitally signed by all the Legal Representatives of the Entities forming the aggregation, as proof of the seriousness and awareness of the commitments undertaken.

### ART. 6 — Phases of the procedure

Following the submission of the applications for participation in the manner and within the deadlines described in art. 5 above, the procedure will take place in the following phases:

a) selection of the Third Sector Entities that have submitted the expression of interest and that possess the required requirements, for the purpose of identifying the detailed definitive project and the related timetable and financial plan;
b) formation and publication of the merit ranking list on the institutional website of the proceeding Administration;
c) invitation to the ETS usefully placed in the final merit ranking list to take part in the co-design Table for the development of a definitive project;
d) co-design, with the possibility of analysing the project proposals, selecting the components deemed consistent with the general purpose, supplementing and making changes to the project ideas in order to identify the operational directions actually practicable during the implementation of the project, and defining jointly and in a shared manner with the proceeding Body and the other partners, transparently and in adversarial proceedings, the definitive project of the interventions and activities, thus drawing up the guidelines on which the PIA must be drafted;
e) signing of the Agreement with the specific purpose of implementing the co-designed interventions, regulating the tasks, establishing the shared resources and governing the reciprocal obligations.

A technical commission, appointed for the purpose, will have the task of evaluating the formal regularity of the applications for participation transmitted to the proceeding Administration and received by the deadline. The evaluation of the initial project proposals will be carried out on the basis of the criteria and parameters referred to in art. 6.2 below. The technical commission may request any additions or clarifications regarding the documentation relating to the expressions of interest submitted.

The following applications will not be admitted:
- Lacking the necessary requirements required by this notice;
- Lacking the documentation required for the submission of the application;
- Received after the deadline.

Passing the selection phase entails a commitment by the selected entity to participate in all the meetings of the aforesaid co-design Table.

**6.1 – Examination of the formal regularity of the applications for participation**

After the closure of the deadline for the receipt of applications, the Person responsible for the procedure, assisted by the technical commission, will verify in a special public session the formal regularity of the applications submitted and the related self-declarations made by the interested parties. After completing the examination of the applications submitted, the Person responsible for the procedure will proceed as follows:
1) he will communicate to the interested parties the grounds preventing the acceptance of the applications, pursuant to art. 10 bis of law n. 241/1990 and subsequent amendments;
2) at the outcome of the sub-procedure referred to in the preceding letter a), he will confirm or not the rejection of the application.

**6.2 – Evaluation of the project proposals**

The Commission, as a perfect collegial body, composed of three (3) members including the RUP, will have at its disposal a total score of 100. The project proposal must reach a minimum score of 70/100, as a threshold, to protect the public interests connected with this procedure; failing this, the proposal will not be examined.

As regards the evaluation of the project proposals, each commissioner will assign a coefficient between 0 and 1 to each element of the project Proposal, according to the following scale of values:
