# Document 1 — Public notice

# MUNICIPALITY OF COLLESANO

*Metropolitan City of Palermo*

# PUBLIC NOTICE (BANDO PUBBLICO)

**For the assignment of the residual resources of the "Support Fund for marginal municipalities" (Fondo di sostegno ai comuni marginali) for the years 2021 – 2022 and 2023**

**HAVING REGARD TO**:

- art. 1, paragraph 196, of law 30 December 2020, n. 178, according to which «*In order to foster social cohesion and economic development in the municipalities particularly affected by the phenomenon of depopulation and for which significant shortcomings in attractiveness are found due to the reduced offer of material and immaterial services to persons and economic activities, in compliance with complementarity with the national strategy for inner areas, the Fund referred to in art. 1, paragraph 65-ter, of law 27 December 2017, n. 205, is named "Support Fund for marginal municipalities"*»;

- art. 1, paragraph 198, of law 30 December 2020, n. 178, according to which «The Fund referred to in paragraph 196 is increased by 30 million euros for each of the years 2021, 2022 and 2023. These resources and those referred to in art. 1, paragraph 65-sexies, of law 27 December 2017, n. 205, are distributed by decree of the President of the Council of Ministers, on the proposal of the Minister for the South and territorial cohesion, which establishes the terms and methods of access and reporting.»;

## HAVING REGARD TO:

- art. 1, paragraph 65-ter, of law 27 December 2017, n. 205, according to which «Within the framework of the national strategy *for the development of inner areas, a fund to support economic, artisan and commercial activities is established at the Department for cohesion policies of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers with an endowment of 30 million euros for each of the years 2020, 2021 and 2022. The fund is distributed among the municipalities present in the inner areas by decree of the President of the Council of Ministers, on the proposal of the Minister for the South and territorial cohesion, which establishes its terms and methods of access and reporting*»;

- art. 1, paragraph 65-sexies, of law 27 December 2017, n. 205, according to which «*The Fund referred to in paragraph 65-ter is increased by 30 million euros for each of the years 2021, 2022 and 2023, in order to implement support interventions for the populations residing in disadvantaged municipalities. The charges deriving from this paragraph are covered by a corresponding reduction of the resources of the Development and Cohesion Fund - 2014-2020 programming referred to in art. 1, paragraph 6, of law 27 December 2013, n. 147. By a special decree of the President of the Council of Ministers, on the proposal of the Minister for the South and territorial cohesion, the beneficiary bodies are identified, on the basis of the following criteria: depopulation, social deprivation, indicators of the income of natural persons lower than the reference averages*;

**CONSIDERING** that "The Support Fund for marginal municipalities" is aimed at fostering social cohesion and economic development in the municipalities particularly affected by the phenomenon of depopulation and for which significant shortcomings in attractiveness are found due to the reduced offer of material and immaterial services to persons and economic activities, in compliance with complementarity with the national strategy for inner areas. The fund mobilises a total of 180 million euros assigned to 1,187 municipalities, selected for their particularly disadvantaged conditions, being at high risk of depopulation, with a particularly high index of social and material vulnerability (IVSM) and with a low level of income of the resident population.;

**RECALLING** the Decree of the President of the Council of Ministers of 30 September 2021, published in the Official Gazette on 14 December 2021 - General series n. 296, and in particular art. 2, paragraph 2, letter c), which distributes the Support Fund for marginal Municipalities for the years 2021-2023, in order to foster social cohesion and economic development in the Municipalities particularly affected by the phenomenon of depopulation and for which significant shortcomings in attractiveness are found due to the reduced offer of material and immaterial services to persons and economic activities;

**TAKING NOTE** that the Municipality of Collesano, by virtue of the aforementioned DPCM, is among the marginal municipalities and has received a financial allocation for the three-year period 2021-2023 amounting to a total of € 165,045.35;

**HAVING REGARD TO** Decree-Law 60/2024 (the so-called Cohesion decree) which, in art. 15 paragraph 2, introduced a new deadline for the use of the contributions assigned to the Marginal Municipalities, postponing it to 31/12/2025 and in fact allowing the possibility of also reusing the residual sums arising from the resources not assigned with the notices relating to the years 2021 and 2022.

**CONSIDERING**, in particular, what is established by the aforementioned article, which provides "*In order to foster social cohesion and economic development in the disadvantaged municipalities referred to in article 1, paragraph 65-sexies, of law 27 December 2017, n. 205, **the resources assigned** to the Municipalities pursuant to the same paragraph 65-sexies and article 1, paragraph 198, of law 30 December 2020, n. 178, **shall not be revoked if used by them by 31 December 2025**. For the purposes of this paragraph, the resources are deemed to be used with the adoption by the Municipality, as resulting from the monitoring system referred to in article 1, paragraph 245, of law 27 December 2013, n. 147, of the measure identifying the beneficiaries of the initiatives eligible for financing in accordance with the provisions of the decree of the President of the Council of Ministers provided for by article 1, paragraph 65-sexies, of law n. 205 of 2017 and by article 1, paragraph 198, of law n. 178 of 2020".*

**WHEREAS**, following the publication of the "Cohesion" decree, a survey was carried out on the use of the resources assigned in the two previous years and, by determination n. 1169 of 09/12/2024, the draft Public Notice addressed to the citizens was approved for the assignment of the resources provided for by the "Support Fund for marginal municipalities" for the year 2023, and for the savings of the years 2021 and 2022, for the total sum of € 115,818.19 (of which € 55,015.11 savings relating to the first year, € 5,787.97 savings relating to the second year and € 55,015.11 sums assigned for the third year);

**CONSIDERING** that, following the above procedure, by determination n. 768 of 23/07/2025 a contribution of € 5,000.00 was assigned in favour of a citizen who transferred his residence to the municipality of Collesano and a contribution of € 60,796.21 in favour of a user who started a new commercial and artisan activity ["attività commerciale e artigianali", text as printed] in the territory of the municipality of Collesano;

**TAKING INTO ACCOUNT**, therefore, that with respect to the planned sum of € 115,818.19, contributions have been assigned for a total amount of € 65,796.21 and that residual funds remain for a sum equal to **€ 50,021.98;**

**CONSIDERING** that it is the intention of the municipal administration, as per Municipal Executive resolution n. 146 of 10/11/2025, to identify, as categories of interventions in which to proceed with the assignment of the above sums, those belonging [text as printed]:

1) to cat. b) granting of contributions for the start-up of commercial, artisan and agricultural activities, to which the sum of € 40,021.98 is to be allocated

2) to cat. c) "granting of contributions in favour of those who transfer their residence and habitual abode to the Municipality", to which the sum of € 10,000.00 is to be allocated

Taking into account all of the above, the Municipality of Collesano has therefore decided to proceed with the issue of this specific Public Notice for the assignment of the residual resources pertaining to the years 2021 - 2022 and 2023, amounting to a total of euro **50,021.98.**

## ART. 1 - BENEFICIARIES

The populations residing in the disadvantaged municipalities may benefit from the contribution in question, as expressly provided for by the DPCM of 30 September 2021 and specifically:

a) **Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs),** as defined by Annex I of Regulation (EU) n. 651/2014 (as amended), by Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC of 6 May 2003, published in the Official Journal of the European Union L 124 of 20 May 2003, transposed by Decree of the Minister of Productive Activities of 18 April 2005 (Official Gazette n. 238 of 12 October 2005), which:

1. have an operational office in the municipality or decide to open one;
2. undertake a new economic activity after the publication of this notice. "New economic activity" also means the activation of new and additional ATECO Codes. Already existing activities that start a new economic activity in the municipal territory through a special production unit may also benefit from the resources. The financing cannot be disbursed in favour of economic activities already established in the territory of the municipality that simply transfer their office within the same municipal territory;
3. are not in difficulty, according to the definition of art. 2 para. 18 of Regulation (EU) n. 651/2014 (as amended);
4. are regularly constituted and enrolled in the register of companies;
5. are not in a state of liquidation or bankruptcy and are not subject to bankruptcy or composition with creditors proceedings.

## ART. 2 - ELIGIBLE INITIATIVES AND IMPLEMENTATION DEADLINES

The contributions are granted for the purpose of implementing interventions for the start-up of commercial, artisan and agricultural activities through an operational unit located in the municipal territory, or which undertake new economic activities in the aforesaid territory are regularly constituted and enrolled in the register of companies [text as printed]. Each participant may submit only one application for aid. In the event of submission of several applications, the last application submitted is taken into consideration. The resources must be used by no later than 31 December 2025.

## ART. 3 – RESOURCES MOBILISED FOR EACH CATEGORY OF INTERVENTION.

The following table shows the residual resources made available and assigned to each category of intervention:

| Category of intervention | Total available resources |
|---|---|
| a) adaptation of properties belonging to the available assets of the municipality | **€ 0.00** |
| b) granting of contributions for the start-up of commercial, artisan and agricultural activities | **€ 40,021.98** |
| c) granting of contributions in favour of those who transfer their residence and habitual abode to the Municipality | **€ 10,000.00** |

It is specified that:
- in the event that, in one or more of the two categories of intervention, requests lower than the available resources are recorded, the unused resources will supplement the available resources assigned to the other type of intervention shown in the preceding table;
- in the event that the total sum of the benefits requested by enterprises, professionals or natural persons exceeds the ceiling of mobilised financial resources, in compliance with the principle of subsidiarity and social cohesion, they will be spread over all the applications that are declared eligible and, consequently, the requested contribution **will be reduced proportionally to the amount of the sum requested by the participant.**
- This procedure does not constitute a preliminary selection of the applications, and the contribution is subject to the actual disbursement of the financing by the Territorial Cohesion Agency;

## ART. 4 - CONTRIBUTION PAYABLE

Expenses directly attributable to the intervention, incurred and paid by the beneficiary enterprise after the date of submission of the application and in any case after the date of publication of this Public Notice, are eligible.

The benefits are granted pursuant to and within the limits of:
- Commission Regulation (EU) n. 1407/2013 of 18 December 2013 on the application of Articles 107 and 108 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union to «***de minimis***» aid;
- Commission Regulation (EU) n. 1408/2013 of 18 December 2013 on the application of Articles 107 and 108 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union to «*de minimis*» aid in the agricultural sector;
- Commission Regulation (EU) n. 717/2014 of 27 June 2014 on the application of Articles 107 and 108 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union to «*de minimis*» aid in the fishery and aquaculture sector.

Specifically, the support, in the form of a capital grant, is granted up to a maximum of **75% of the eligible expenditure net of VAT** and always in the presence of adequate financial capacity.

It is specified that, from the point of view of State aid rules, there is no explicit prohibition on the possibility of supplementing/cumulating the types of contributions provided for in art. 2, paragraph 2, of the DPCM of 30 September 2021 and reported in the preceding Art. 3, without prejudice to compliance with the general rules applicable to the cumulation of State aid and in particular those provided for by the aforementioned "*de minimis*" aid rules.

## ART. 5 - METHODS AND DEADLINES FOR SUBMITTING THE APPLICATION FOR ADMISSION TO FINANCING AND RECEIVABILITY

Applications for the benefits must be received, under penalty of inadmissibility, exclusively by PEC: protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it by no later than **2:00 p.m. on 27 November 2025**. The application for admission to the benefits - under penalty of exclusion – consists of the following documents:
- Application for Admission to financing (Annex "A") to this Notice, complete with all the required attachments;
- Substitutive declaration on *De Minimis* (Annex "B").

## ART. 6 – ELIGIBILITY AND ASSIGNMENT OF RESOURCES

The Municipality first verifies the receivability and eligibility of the applications received on the basis of what is reported in the preceding Arts. 2 and 3 and then proceeds, in accordance with the resources distributed by category and with what is expressly provided for in the preceding art. 3, with the assignment of the related resources. The proponents of the admitted and financeable applications are given written communication, by PEC or to the e-mail address indicated in the application, of the amount of the resources assigned, after consulting the National Register of State Aid (RNA), for the verification of compliance with the ceiling of Euro 200,000.00, referred to in Regulation (EU) n. 1407/2013, and the consequent registration of the individual "de *minimis*" aid. The maximum contribution that may be granted to the same beneficiary, understood in the sense of a single enterprise, amounts to Euro 200,000.00 over 3 financial years on a rolling basis. In the calculation of this ceiling, account must also be taken of all other *de minimis* aid which the Enterprise has already received/is receiving in relation to the same financial years. The Municipality will verify this maximum ceiling through the functionalities of the "*National Register of State Aid*" ("*RNA*") and in particular through the so-called "*de minimis survey*": at the registration stage of each individual aid, "*RNA*" shows the *plafond* still available for the granting of new *de minimis* aid. Therefore, the grant may be set within the permitted limit.

## ART. 7 - METHODS OF DISBURSEMENT OF THE FINANCING

The aid granted is credited to the specific current account indicated by each beneficiary. Requests for disbursement of the contribution must be prepared using exclusively the forms made available.

**- FOR CATEGORY B) ["PER L CATEGORIA B)", text as printed], UP TO A MAXIMUM OF TWO INSTALMENTS:**

a) the first instalment as an advance payment or as a progress payment in the form of a down payment;
b) the second instalment as a balance.

The first instalment of the contribution may be disbursed:
- as an advance payment upon presentation of a surety policy, equal to 40% of the amount of the contribution assigned. The request form, attached to this notice, must be accompanied by the bank guarantee or insurance policy in favour of the municipality, irrevocable, unconditional and payable on first demand, for an amount equal to the contribution advanced, according to the facsimile template attached to the notice. The policy may be extinguished, upon request, only after the payment of the balance;
- as a progress payment, against an amount of expenditure incurred of not less than 40% of the total eligible expenses. The request form must be accompanied by the following documentation:

a) Summary list of the expenses incurred, signed by the legal representative of the beneficiary (Annex "E").

The balance of the contribution is disbursed upon presentation of a specific request - drawn up in conformity with the prepared forms - and of the final expenditure report according to the methods listed below:
- Balance request (Annex "D");
- Summary list of the expenses incurred, signed by the legal representative of the beneficiary (Annex "E").
- authentic copy (pursuant to arts. 18, 19 and 47 of D.P.R. 445/2000) of the invoices issued by the suppliers and of the other equivalent supporting documents;
- copy of the supporting documents relating to the payments made and of the bank statements with the related movements highlighted.

**FOR CATEGORY C), IN A SINGLE PAYMENT UPON THE CHANGE OF RESIDENCE.**

## ART. 8 - REVOCATIONS

The following constitute grounds for total revocation of the benefits:

a) the definitive cessation, within five years of the disbursement of the balance of the contribution, of the activity of the beneficiary for which the benefits were granted, or the placing in liquidation, the loss of the eligibility requirements or the admission to insolvency proceedings of the beneficiary;
b) the transfer of the productive activity benefiting from the contribution outside the municipal territory, before five years have elapsed since the conclusion of the intervention;
c) the refusal of the beneficiary to allow checks on the implementation of the intervention;
d) the failure to comply, with regard to employed workers, with collective labour agreements and labour protection regulations, contested and ascertained by the competent Offices against the beneficiary;
e) in the event of untruthful declarations or use of false acts by the beneficiary;
f) the failure to transmit the final documentation to the municipality;
g) any other ground for revocation provided for by law or by the Notice.
