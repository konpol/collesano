## INTERNAL NOTICE

#### OF SELECTION FOR THE ATTRIBUTION OF SALARY DIFFERENTIALS WITHIN THE PROFESSIONAL AREAS PURSUANT TO ART. 14 OF THE CCNL (national collective labour agreement) OF 16/11/2022

THE HEAD OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE AND SOCIAL-WELFARE AREA — HUMAN RESOURCES SERVICE

#### Recalling:

- Art. 23 of Legislative Decree n. 150/2009, which establishes that "*Public administrations selectively recognise the economic progressions referred to in article 52, paragraph 1-bis, of legislative decree 30 March 2001, n.165, as introduced by article 62 of this decree, on the basis of what is provided for by the national and supplementary collective labour agreements and within the limits of the available resources.*";
- Art. 52 of Legislative Decree 30 March 2001 n. 165, as amended by art. 62 of Legislative Decree n. 150/2009, which establishes in paragraph 1 bis… "*Progressions within the same area take place according to principles of selectivity, on the basis of cultural and professional qualities, the activity carried out and the results achieved…*";
- Legislative Decree 18 August 2000 n. 267, containing the "Consolidated Text of the Laws on the organisation of Local Authorities";
- Legislative Decree 11 April 2006 n. 198, containing the "Code of equal opportunities between man and woman;
- the National Collective Labour Agreement of the Local Functions staff sector for the three-year period 2019-2022, signed on 16/11/2022;

**Whereas** art. 12 of the National Collective Labour Agreement of 16/11/2022 revised the classification system of Local Authority staff, replacing the four professional categories A, B, C and D introduced by art. 3 of the CCNL of 31/3/1999, within which various economic positions had been created, with the new 4 professional areas (area of Operators, Area of Expert Operators, Area of Instructors (Istruttori), Area of Officials and of High Qualification).

**Taking into account**, in this regard, Table A of the C.C.N.L. of 16/11/2022 relating to the gross annual amount and maximum number of salary differentials, and Table B of the CCNL of 16/11/2022 on automatic transposition into the new classification system;

**Having regard**, in particular, to:

a) arts. 79 paragraphs 1 and 2 and art. 80 paragraph 2 letter j) of the CCNL of 16.11.2022 which, in particular, confirms that the charges for the payment of the salary differentials are borne by the decentralised resources;

b) art. 14 paragraph 2, letter a) of the C.C.N.L. of 16/11/2022 "*Economic progression within the areas*", which provides, for the purposes of participation in the selective procedure for the attribution of the salary differentials:

- that the workers have not benefited from any horizontal economic progression in the last three years, and that for the purposes of verifying this requirement, account is taken of the starting dates of the progressions carried out;
- the absence, in the last 2 years, of disciplinary measures exceeding a fine; where, at the deadline for the submission of applications, disciplinary proceedings are in progress, the employee is admitted to the procedure with reservation and, if he/she is placed in a useful position in the ranking list, the payment of the differential is suspended until the conclusion of the disciplinary proceedings. If, as a result of the proceedings, the employee is given a sanction exceeding a fine, the employee is definitively excluded from the procedure;

**Having regard to**:

- The Supplementary Decentralised Collective Agreement (CCDI), normative part, for the three-year period 2023/2025, signed on 18/12/2023, in particular art. 4 "Economic Progressions within the Areas", which entrusted to the approval of a specific regulation the determination of the Criteria for the definition of the procedures for economic progressions within the areas;
- The Supplementary Decentralised Collective Agreement, normative part, for the three-year period 2023/2025, as amended on 16/07/2025, which approved the Regulation for the "Procedures and Criteria for Economic Progressions within the Areas";

**Considering** art. 3 of the Regulation for the "Procedures and Criteria for Economic Progressions within the Areas", which establishes "*The selections are carried out annually by means of an area selective procedure, subject to negotiation of the resources, and will take effect from 1 January of the year in which the institution of economic progressions within the areas is financed. The requirements must be possessed as at 31/12 of the year preceding the selection. In the annual distribution of the Decentralised Resources Fund, the number of salary differentials attributable in the year for each area is identified*";

**Having regard to** the constitution of the Fund of decentralised resources, Economic part, for the year 2024, constituted by determination n. 487 of 21/06/2024;

**Recalling** managerial determination n. 871 of 20/08/2025 approving this notice;

### MAKES KNOWN

That in order to carry out the procedures for the attribution of the salary differentials of the employees belonging to the areas of the, Area of Expert Operators, Area of Instructors ["alle aree degli, Area degli Operatori Esperti, Area degli Istruttori", text as printed], according to the agreements signed with the CCDI 2023/2025, **THE SELECTION IS ANNOUNCED** for an internal selective procedure for economic progressions within the areas (salary differentials), taking effect **from 01.01.2024**, and reserved for the permanent staff of the Municipality of Collesano in service as at 31/12/2023 belonging to the aforesaid Areas, in relation to the available resources.

#### Art. 1 - Positions announced

The positions announced are equal to 50% of the permanent employees of the Municipality of Collesano in service as at 31/12/2023 who are entitled, as agreed in the CCDI 2023/2025, and as indicated below:

#### Area of Instructors
Total units estimated as at 01/01/2024 – 5;

**Area of Expert Operators**
Total units estimated as at 01/01/2024 – 4.

#### Art. 2 - General requirements for Competing in the Selection

For participation in the selective procedure, the following minimum requirements are required:

1. the worker must not have benefited from any economic progression in the last 3 years. For the calculation of the requirement, account is taken of the starting dates of the economic progressions carried out (1 January of the year of definitive signing of the supplementary agreement).
2. he/she must be an employee with a permanent employment contract at the Municipality of Collesano as at 31/12/2023;
3. absence, in the last 2 years, of disciplinary measures exceeding a fine; where, at the deadline for the submission of applications, disciplinary proceedings are in progress, the employee is admitted to the procedure with reservation and, if he/she is placed in a useful position in the ranking list, the payment of the differential is suspended until the conclusion of the disciplinary proceedings; if, as a result of the proceedings, the employee is given a sanction exceeding a fine, the employee is definitively excluded from the procedure;
4. he/she must not already have benefited from the maximum number of possible differentials provided for per area as per Table A of the CCNL of 16/11/2022.

For lack of the admission requirements, the Personnel office may at any time order exclusion from the selection.

#### Art. 3 – Methods and deadlines of the procedure

The internal selection procedure begins with this notice published on the online Albo Pretorio of the Municipality and on the institutional website of the Body in the section "Amministrazione Trasparente – Bandi di concorso" **for at least 30 days**. Employees in possession of the requirements must submit an application for participation drawn up on plain paper using the attached form, which forms an integral part of this notice. The application must reach the Protocol Office of the Municipality of Collesano by and not the ["entro e non il", text as printed; "by no later than the"]

### peremptory deadline of 12:00 noon on 20 September 2025.

The application may also be sent by e-mail to the following institutional address: protocollo.comunecollesano@pec.it with the following subject "***procedura per l'attribuzione dei differenziali stipendiali – anno 2024***" (procedure for the attribution of salary differentials – year 2024). The declarations made and signed in the application for participation have the value of self-certification pursuant to D.P.R. 445/2000; in the case of falsity in acts and untruthful declarations, the criminal sanctions pursuant to art. 76 of D.P.R. 445/2000 apply.

#### AREA OF EXPERT OPERATORS:

a) **65 points** – evaluation of performance in the three-year period preceding the year in which the procedures are started (2021-2022-2023). In the event of a missing evaluation due to the employee's absence in one or more years of the preceding three-year period, the last useful year preceding that of the absence will be taken into account. The evaluations of individual performance for the three-year period obtained at the Municipality of Collesano will be acquired ex officio during the examination of the applications for participation in this notice. As regards evaluations obtained at other Bodies/Administrations and not present in the personnel file, the staff concerned must supplement or amend the data in the Body's possession by attaching a copy of the relevant missing evaluation.

b) **30 points**: professional experience gained in the field of reference, that is, the skills acquired and the professional growth achieved in the same profile, with or without interruption, including fixed-term or part-time, in the same or other administrations of the sector, as well as in the same or corresponding profile at other administrations of different sectors, as at 31/12 of the year preceding the effective date of the institution, excluding the seniority necessary to access the selection established in paragraph 2, point B), letter a). In the case of employees hired through mobility, the calculation of seniority also takes into account the years accrued at the other administration. Any periods of leave of absence are not computed ["non vengono computato", text as printed] in the calculation of seniority. For the purposes of accruing seniority of service, a part-time employment relationship is considered a full-time employment relationship. The score is determined by attributing 2.4 points for each year of service since the last salary differential or economic progression acquired (today salary differential) and 0.2 for each month of service. For periods of less than a calendar month, a fraction exceeding 15 days is considered a full month.

c) **5 points:** Professional skills acquired through training courses. The professional skills acquired are:
a) those certified following training courses at schools, bodies and training institutes recognised in the Italian legal system;
b) the professional titles and qualifications obtained up to the effective date of the economic and legal attribution of the economic progression, provided they are pertinent to the activities and functions of the Municipality;
c) attendance, certified by the training provider, at training or professional refresher courses.

The scores are attributed to professional titles and qualifications as explained in art. 5 of the regulation.

#### AREA OF INSTRUCTORS:

a) **70 points** – evaluation of performance in the three-year period preceding the year in which the procedures are started (2021-2022-2023). In the event of a missing evaluation due to the employee's absence in one or more years of the preceding three-year period, the last useful year preceding that of the absence will be taken into account. The evaluations of individual performance for the three-year period obtained at the Municipality of Collesano will be acquired ex officio during the examination of the applications for participation in this notice. As regards evaluations obtained at other Bodies/Administrations and not present in the personnel file, the staff concerned must supplement or amend the data in the Body's possession by attaching a copy of the relevant missing evaluation.

b) **20 points**: professional experience gained in the field of reference, that is, the skills acquired and the professional growth achieved in the same profile, with or without interruption, including fixed-term or part-time, in the same or other administrations of the sector, as well as in the same or corresponding profile at other administrations of different sectors, as at 31/12 of the year preceding the effective date of the institution, excluding the seniority necessary to access the selection established in paragraph 2, point B), letter a). In the case of employees hired through mobility, the calculation of seniority also takes into account the years accrued at the other administration. Any periods of leave of absence are not computed ["non vengono computato", text as printed] in the calculation of seniority. For the purposes of accruing seniority of service, a part-time employment relationship is considered a full-time employment relationship. The score is determined by attributing 2.4 points for each year of service since the last salary differential or economic progression acquired (today salary differential) and 0.2 for each month of service. For periods of less than a calendar month, a fraction exceeding 15 days is considered a full month.

c) **10 points:** Professional skills acquired through training courses. The professional skills acquired are:
d) those certified following training courses at schools, bodies and training institutes recognised in the Italian legal system;
e) the professional titles and qualifications obtained up to the effective date of the economic and legal attribution of the economic progression, provided they are pertinent to the activities and functions of the Municipality;
f) attendance, certified by the training provider, at training or professional refresher courses.

The scores are attributed to professional titles and qualifications as explained in art. 5 of the regulation.

The economic progression is attributed to the employees who have obtained the highest score within their own area, in descending order, with effect from 1 January of the year of definitive signing of the reference supplementary agreement. Once the ranking lists per area have been determined ["Una volta determinata le graduatorie", text as printed], the salary differentials will be attributed in sequence, on the basis of the available resources, up to the number set for each area.

Within each ranking list, in the event of an equal score between two or more employees, the following precedence criteria will be applied, in order:

1. higher average score in the performance evaluation of the last three years;
2. greater number of years since the last salary differential/economic classification category
3. greater seniority of service in the Body
4. greater age

Should any of the employees entitled to the economic progression be admitted with reservation, the position held is frozen and the related sums set aside until the outcome of the disciplinary proceedings. Employees who have not obtained economic progressions for more than six years are attributed an additional score to that obtained (maximum 3%) as follows:

| number of years without progression | % |
|---|---|
| From more than 6 years to 7 | 1% |
| From more than 7 years to 8 | 2% |
| Over 8 years | 3% |

The provisional ranking lists, separate per area, will be approved by determination of the Director of the Administrative Area and will be published on the Albo Pretorio and in "Amministrazione Trasparente – Bandi di concorso – Bandi e avvisi di selezione". Interested parties may submit a written complaint to the Human Resources Service within **7 days** of the publication of the ranking list. After this period, the Human Resources Service will examine any applications received (within the following 10 days) and, having verified the related scores, will confirm or amend the provisional ranking list, making it definitive by determination of the Director of the Administrative Area. The definitive ranking list will be published on the Albo Pretorio and in "Amministrazione Trasparente – Bandi di concorso – Bandi e avvisi di selezione". Once the ranking list has become definitive, the head of the Human Resources Service will adopt the acts necessary for the legal and economic recognition of the differentials on the basis of the ranking order and within the limit of the established number of differentials.

The ranking lists are effective from 01/01/2024, the year for which the attribution of the economic differential was provided.
