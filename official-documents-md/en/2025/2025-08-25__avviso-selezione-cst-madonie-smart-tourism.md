# SELECTION NOTICE

**FOR EXPRESSIONS OF INTEREST IN THE PROFESSIONAL COLLABORATION OF N.6 EXPERTS OF THE MULTIDISCPLINARE [text as printed; "multidisciplinary"] TEAM FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PROJECT**

## MADONIE SMART TOURISM

**Creation of a Territorial Service Centre and intelligent marketing for personalised tourism**

### THE RUP (Person responsible for the procedure)

**Having regard to** decree-law 30 April 2019, n. 34, containing "Urgent measures for economic growth and for the resolution of specific crisis situations", converted with amendments by law 28 June 2019, n. 58, published in the Official Gazette of the Italian Republic n. 100 of 30 April 2019 and, in particular, article 28 containing "Simplifications for the definition of Territorial Pacts and area contracts";

**Having regard to** the Decree of 30 November 2020, published in the Official Gazette of 25 January 2021, by which the Ministry of Economic Development approved the "Criteria for the distribution and transfer of the residual resources of the territorial pacts to be used for the financing of projects aimed at the development of the territorial entrepreneurial fabric, also through the experimentation of innovative services in support of enterprises";

**Noting** that:
- SO.SVI.MA. S.p.A. is identified as the Responsible Party of the Madonie Territorial Pact (hereinafter "Pact") pursuant to the provisions of point 2.5 of CIPE resolution n. 29 of 21 March 1997;
- the Municipality of Collesano was identified by the General Assembly of the partnership of the Madonie Territorial Pact as the beneficiary of the intervention named "MADONIE SMART TOURISM - Creation of a Territorial Service Centre and intelligent marketing for personalised tourism", within which the professional figures to be selected with this public notice of expression of interest are provided for;

**Recalling** determination n. 870 of 20/08/2025 by which the Draft of the Selection notice for Expressions of interest in the professional collaboration of n.6 experts of the multidisciplinary Team for the implementation of the project "MADONIE SMART TOURISM - Creation of a Territorial Service Centre and intelligent marketing for personalised tourism", within the Madonie Territorial Pact, was approved, together with the annex "Template 1 - Application for participation";

# MAKES KNOWN

that this Selection Notice for the Acquisition of Expressions of Interest is announced, addressed to professionals and experts in various professional fields, for professional collaboration in the creation of a multidisciplinary Team of n. 6 experts for the implementation of the project "*MADONIE SMART TOURISM - Creation of a Territorial Service Centre and intelligent marketing for personalised tourism*". In relation to the figures to be sought, the following is specified:

### ART. 1 - SUBJECT OF THE NOTICE

The Municipality of Collesano, with its seat in Corso Vittorio Emanuele, 2 – 90016 Collesano (PA), in its capacity as beneficiary of the Madonie Territorial Pact, launches a *call* for expressions of interest in professional collaboration in the multidisciplinary Team for the implementation of the project "MADONIE SMART TOURISM - Creation of a Territorial Service Centre and intelligent marketing for personalised tourism".

The Madonie Territorial Pact refers to the following 21 Municipalities, mostly located in the Province of Palermo: Alimena, Blufi, Bompietro, Caltavuturo, Campofelice di Roccella, Castelbuono, Castellana Sicula, Collesano, Gangi, Geraci Siculo, Gratteri, Isnello, Lascari, Petralia Soprana, Petralia Sottana, Polizzi Generosa, Pollina, Resuttano, San Mauro Castelverde, Scillato, Sclafani Bagni.

The Territorial Service Centre (CST) shall collaborate in the conception, implementation and promotion of initiatives and projects for the development of tourism within the Madonie Territorial Pact, developing the promotion activities and projects concerning:

- Knowledge of the system
- Territorial brand identity
- Direction, development and coordination of product and strategic alliances
- Digital Marketing
- Communication
- Sponsorships and local events
- Analysis and monitoring of markets
- Sustainability and territorial development
- Enhancement of Madonie products
- Promotion of the cultural system
- Development of interregional projects
- Interventions in favour of specific territorial areas
- Accessible tourism.

### ART. 2 - ACTIVITIES OF THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE TEAM

The multidisciplinary team is founded on a methodological approach based mainly:

- on the development of the integrated tourism promotion of the Madonie Territorial Pact, coordinated by the Head of the Tourism Service Centre (CST) with project management functions, for the management, monitoring and control of the activities relating to the management of the CST and the implementation of the territorial strategy provided for by the Pact;
- on the implementation of a multidisciplinary Team that can count on certain specialist professional skills for the implementation of the CST and of the tourism promotion project "MADONIE SMART TOURISM";
- on the transfer of knowledge, tools and methodologies between experts, beneficiaries, and staff of the adhering local authorities, so as to guarantee the maximum achievement of the objectives of experimentation of the Pact's strategy;
- on interaction, in compliance with the principles of loyal collaboration, with the various actors and partners of the Pact;
- on the concrete implementation of all the instruments of territorial promotion and enhancement in the field of local tourism already realised with previous financed interventions and with the actions provided for by the project of the Madonie Territorial Pact.

The Team shall handle digital communication on all social media channels, in a manner consistent with the territorial brand identity, adapting each channel to appropriate languages, images and contents (*tone of voice*). It shall also build, consolidate and nurture a large network of international relations with digital communication professionals, involving the main and most important influencers of the international scene. It shall oversee *digital* communication on the occasion of territorial events also through the event's social media channels, always in close collaboration with the editorial and system partners. It shall develop the increase of the *brand awareness* and the *web reputation* of the territory in which the MADONIE SMART TOURISM project will be implemented.

Transversal characteristics of this approach are represented by:
✓ continuous interaction between the skills of the various professional figures involved;
✓ flexibility, that is, the possibility of continuous adaptation, both with respect to the technical, organisational and temporal needs arising in the course of the work, and with respect to any project variants;
✓ continuity in terms of service provision.

For admission to the selection, possession of the following requirements is required:

- not having been removed, dispensed or declared forfeited from employment at a public administration, or barred from public office on the basis of a final judgment;

### PROFILE 1: Destination manager - Head of the Tourism Service Centre (CST)

The **Destination Manager** is a professional who designs and proposes Tourism Development Plans for the promotion and relaunch of a tourist destination. He/she evaluates the competitiveness both of tourist destinations and of individual territorial tourism offers. Endowed with a deep knowledge of the tourism heritage of the reference territory, the Destination Manager is able to recognise and promote the specific potential of a territory in terms of tourism development.

Technical consulting to be provided in support of the Territorial Pact and the CST regarding:

a. the ability to read local scenarios and the dynamics of national and extra-national tourism;
b. the promotion of a network culture among local actors and the beneficiaries of the Pact, with skills in territorial animation and participatory processes;
c. the effective integration of various disciplinary sectors: management of local tourism quality, development of tourism products and services, management of the value of relationships, communication, promotion, marketing, case studies and surveys;
d. the integration of development policies in which the community is the bearer of identity and values;
e. the integration of experiences through the heritage of material and immaterial natural and cultural resources, and of the other attractions of the Madonie;
f. the integration of communication strategies for the definition and construction of the Madonie tourism ecosystem of excellence, capable of increasing the reputation of the enterprises and operators already active and contributing to a better positioning of the others;
g. the integration of a new tourism product generating advantages for users/visitors in order to increase the profitability of operators and the competitiveness of the destination.

The figure sought shall also hold the role of head of the CST's activities; in this sense he/she shall:
- understand and analyse everything that constitutes a tourist attraction, evaluate its state and understand how to enhance the unique features of the Madonie territory;
- define a promotion strategy based on the strengths of the territory and an analysis of the needs and requirements of the visitor;
- push the tourism offer with new attractive proposals;
- involve local actors by taking on a role of mediator/facilitator of participatory processes;
- coordinate the work of the team constituting the CST, of the tourism service providers and of all the people involved in the process of promotion and communication of the tourist destination, including the tourist offices of the municipalities adhering to the project;
- define the communicative format of the marketing campaigns.

For the purposes of participation for the profile of **Destination Manager**, candidates must possess the following requirements:

- **Educational qualifications**

1) University degree (DL) of the old system obtained under the system pre-existing D.M. 509/1999, or a specialist or master's degree (LS/LM) of the new university system in subjects pertinent to the assignment of "*destination manager*", specifically: Tourism sciences – L-15; Economics and management of tourism services – L-18; Business economics – L-18; Territorial, urban, landscape and environmental planning – L-21; Cultural heritage – L-1; Political science and international relations – L-36; Administration and organisation sciences – L-16; Business economic sciences – LM 77; Economic sciences ["Scienze dell'economie", text as printed] – LM 56. 2) Specialisation courses and/or professional masters for Destination Manager pursuant to Law 4/2013 and DPCM 14/10/2021;

- **Professional Experience**

1) Possession of proven work experience and specific professionalism for a period of at least 2 years, even non-continuous, rendered in a field pertinent to the position covered by the selection (promotion, events, marketing, territorial marketing), in managerial or comparable positions, e.g. heads of structures in bodies such as DMOs, Tourism Districts, Tourism Promotion Consortia and/or Public or Private Bodies or Public or Private Companies;

The selection will be carried out by a special Commission appointed for the purpose.
