## Point by point — accusation vs. response

Each cell ends with a 📖 link that jumps to the **full passage** in the original speech (opens the raw text at that line).

| Topic | ⬅️ Giovanni Meli — accusation | ➡️ Tiziana Cascio — response |
|---|---|---|
| **Wastewater plant (depuratore)** | The administration lied by saying there was no funding. Ministerial Decree No. 191 of 17 May 2022 (M2C4, Inv. 4.4) opened €600M nationally / €61.6M for Sicily. Collesano could have had a new ~€1.409M plant at no cost to the town instead of paying ~€250k locally for upgrades. The project was submitted through ATI; the money was available. A payment dispute and an unresolved urban-planning designation issue caused Collesano to lose the chance — and now the upgrades must be paid with local money.<br>📖 [Full text — L25][me1] | The plant is old (1975) but legal, functioning, and under no EU infringement. The 2021 design was not build-ready — it still needed opinions/authorizations (ATO, regional utilities, ASP, heritage, civil engineering). The services conference was closed over unresolved issues (incl. planning conformity), then reopened and **concluded positively in March 2024**. The project was updated to the new price list and procurement code, verified, approved and submitted for a funding line. **There was no lost financing decree** — only a call for project proposals, not secured funding (backed by audio from a technical official).<br>📖 [Full text — L86][ca1] |
| **Legal dispute with the designer** | The studio that prepared the larger project asked to be paid; the administration refused, the studio filed an injunction, and the municipality spent yet more public money hiring a lawyer. Documents show the work had been officially requested.<br>📖 [Full text — L37][me2] | The designer asked for ~€28k beyond the original fee. The offices appointed a lawyer to protect the municipality — normal caution, not a scandal. The case was **settled for ~€19k**, and the municipality immediately obtained the updated project needed to apply for funding.<br>📖 [Full text — L100][ca2] |
| **Urban plan (PRG / PUG)** | ~10 years of work and ~€100k of public money; the draft reached the council before he left office. The deadline under the transitional regime was 31 Dec 2025. He warned publicly on 11 Sept 2025 and the minority raised it in council, but the administration let the deadline pass. The old plan is lost; the town must restart under the PUG with ≥€150k of new costs and years more work.<br>📖 [Full text — L41][me3] | The PRG dates from 2008 and needs updating; the update began in 2012 **under Meli**, with a preliminary framework approved in May 2023, but was not finally approved by the 31 Dec 2025 deadline. She **accepts political responsibility** for the missed deadline — but rejects "did nothing": the administration immediately launched the PUG (executive resolution, new urban-planning office, procedure manager, public notices/observations, meetings with professionals, request for regional funding).<br>📖 [Full text — L33][ca3] |
| **Community House (Casa di Comunità)** | The most serious loss — only two Community Houses in all Sicily were lost, and Collesano was one. The Region had identified Collesano; it could have had 15 clinics and 24-hour care. ASP-appointed technicians requested building documents to design it, proving it was real; a feasibility project existed with **€500k** allocated. If that was too little, the mayor should have gone to the Region for more. Instead it was neglected and suppressed by decree, and the funds were redistributed to other municipalities.<br>📖 [Full text — L51][me4] | She never denied Collesano was in the regional plan — she denied that **direct funding was assigned to the Municipality**; implementation belonged to the health authority. The amount (**~€342k**) was not enough to adapt the former small hospital, and documents show the intervention was cancelled as **technically/economically impracticable** (structural & seismic issues), not through negligence. The municipality secured an alternative: a **35-year free-use agreement with ASP Palermo** for health services (specialist clinics, community services, PUA single access point, territorial care, continuity of care). Dr. Casiglia confirms Collesano will still get essential services (diabetology, cardiology, pulmonology, geriatrics, nursing, booking, home care, telemedicine).<br>📖 [Full text — L50][ca4] |
| **Opera Pia / social-health services** | The administration presents this as its own success, but it came from his mandate: Decree No. 440 of 9 Dec 2022, ~€1,999,245, with Collesano as lead municipality alongside Gangi and Gratteri, funding ~30 months of support for the elderly and vulnerable.<br>📖 [Full text — L69][me5] | Meli conflated several distinct projects. She separates them: (1) the **ATS social-infrastructure** project (started ~2021–2022, already active, ~200 citizens — elderly, disabled, fragile, home assistance, special-needs children); (2) a social-health district project for minors and families; (3) support for disabled children and families. She says Meli mixed them together and presented them inaccurately.<br>📖 [Full text — L74][ca5] |
| **"Projects presented as their own"** | Many projects the administration claims — public lighting, roads, the sports field, rockfall protection, Opera Pia — were actually prepared under his mandate. He cites a €3.5M hydrogeological safety project for the rocky slopes/historic center that the administration failed to follow until he raised the alarm.<br>📖 [Full text — L65][me6] | She distinguishes a genuinely funded project from one merely placed in a ranking or submitted. She lists many works started, continued, funded or completed by her administration (nursery ~€560k PNRR, roads, water network & drought response, waste center, former slaughterhouse, schools, ceramic museum ~€760k SNAI, cemetery, public lighting, Palazzo Fatta…) — and **apologizes** for one campaign claim that called a sports facility "funded" when it had only been admitted to a ranking. Results belonged to the whole team: "he was not mayor alone."<br>📖 [Full text — L108][ca6] |
| **Betrayal / relationship** | He supported the current team, chose not to run again, and campaigned for them expecting they would continue his projects. After they won he felt betrayed and abandoned, and had to warn them for months via videos, posts and the minority councillors because the administration would not talk to him.<br>📖 [Full text — L19][me7] | She had supported him, served as council president, and was part of his administration. She rejects the betrayal narrative — after becoming mayor there were still messages and contacts, including advice and reminders. Many "his" projects were team efforts: "he was not mayor alone, just as she is not mayor alone."<br>📖 [Full text — L23][ca7] |
| **Democracy & priorities** | The administration focuses on events, ceremonies, flags, festivals, Targa Florio and Carnival while neglecting the structural projects that shape daily life. People are even afraid to speak or like posts for fear of pressure or retaliation.<br>📖 [Full text — L153][me8] | She rejects the "incompetent / anti-democratic / negligent" labels and objects to being called "less democratic than Putin." She challenges the fear/retaliation claim — if anyone truly suffered it, they should report it formally, not use it as a slogan — and defends cultural events (Targa Florio, Carnival, ceramics, the Madonna feast) as legitimate governance, not a distraction from roads and water.<br>📖 [Full text — L15][ca8] |

> **Note:** Each summary reports the claims, accusations and reconstructions as expressed by the speaker in the respective video. They are positions stated publicly, **not** an independent verification of the facts or documents cited. Where the two accounts disagree on figures (e.g. €500k vs €342k for the Community House allocation), both are shown as stated.
