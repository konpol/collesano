## 1. What this is
**Madonie Nomad Residency** (first edition: 4–6 Sept 2026, Castelbuono) is a festival on digital nomads and territorial regeneration, part of the two-year **Madonie Smart Tourism** project whose **lead municipality (ente capofila) is Collesano**, coordinated by Gioacchino Di Garbo. Site: https://www.madonienomads.com/ · Videos: [Day 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KRLPbhvIyBQ) · [Day 3](https://www.youtube.com/live/4pTL012ZOY4)

## 3. Why this dossier exists
It connects the **political dispute** to the **community-development narrative**: the administration's strongest public story (territorial promotion, nomad residency, Targa Florio) is precisely what critics cite as evidence of skewed priorities. Keeping both in one place lets a reader weigh them.
