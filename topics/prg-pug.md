# PRG / PUG — urban master plan

> 🇮🇹 **Versione italiana → [prg-pug-IT](prg-pug-IT.md)**

**Status:** deadline missed (undisputed); responsibility disputed · **Money at stake:** ~€75–100k spent on the update (wasted); ≥€150k estimated for the new PUG · **Key date:** 31 December 2025 (transitional-regime deadline, missed)

## 1. What everyone agrees on
The PRG (Piano Regolatore Generale) dates from 2008; its update began around 2012 under Meli. Sicily's 2020 law replaced the PRG with the PUG (Piano Urbanistico Generale) but allowed advanced updates to finish under a transitional regime. Collesano joined via a council acknowledgment in **April 2023**. The **31 December 2025 deadline was missed** — the mayor herself accepts political responsibility. The town must now produce a PUG.
