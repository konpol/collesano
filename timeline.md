# Timeline — Collesano's contested three years (and their roots)

One chronological spine for all topics. Each entry: event → source voice(s) → topic dossier. Claims are *as stated* by the linked voice, not independently verified.

## Before the 2023 election
- **2008** — current PRG approved → [prg-pug](topics/prg-pug.md)
- **~2012** — PRG update begins (Meli mandate; all voices agree)
- **2020** — Sicilian law replaces PRG with PUG; transitional regime for advanced updates
- **2021** — larger depuratore project designed (Meli) → [depuratore](topics/depuratore.md)
- **~2021–22** — ATS social-infrastructure project starts (per Cascio, still active, ~200 citizens) → [opera-pia-social](topics/opera-pia-social.md)
- **17 May 2022** — DM 191: €600M national / €61.6M Sicily wastewater line (per Meli: Collesano's chance; per Cascio: a call, not secured funding)
- **9 Dec 2022** — Decree No. 440, ~€2M social-health funding, Collesano lead municipality (Meli mandate)
- **April 2023** — council presa d'atto joins PRG transitional regime (Cascio then council president)

## 2023 — mandate change
- **2023 election** — Cascio administration wins with 39.62% of votes cast; Meli didn't run and campaigned for the winning team (all three voices agree on the support; its aftermath is disputed)
- **July 2023** — last administration→Region PRG communication for ~2 years (per opposition records access) → [prg-pug](topics/prg-pug.md)
- **Sept 2023** — opposition files formal question on school-building safety after falling plaster → [schools](topics/schools.md)
- **Nov 2023** — opposition requests SP9 agenda item; PRG standstill begins (per opposition)
- **Dec 2023** — council unanimously approves SP9 clarification request to Metropolitan City

## 2024
- **Jan 2024** — opposition letters on family-doctor vacancy; meeting with Dr. Casiglia → [schools](topics/schools.md)
- **Feb 2024** — national digital-nomad visa introduced (context: [nomad-residency](topics/nomad-residency.md))
- **March 2024** — depuratore services conference concluded positively (per Cascio)
- **May 2024** — administration announces SP9 delivery "by June" (per opposition: nothing for 8 months)
- **11 Sept 2024** — opposition amendment: €17,658.46 to fully cover school-transport costs — voted down
- **Nov 2024** — Region sets final PRG deadline: **31 Dec 2025**

## 2025
- **Feb 2025** — SP9 works only partial (markings missing) yet celebrated (per opposition)
- **31 March + June 2025** — opposition formal questions on castle accessibility; partial opening Aug, full ~Nov
- **15 April 2025** — opposition formal question on Casa di Comunità; funding subsequently lost → [casa-di-comunita](topics/casa-di-comunita.md)
- **May 2025** — municipal-kennel motion (€27k/yr, 14 dogs) — voted down
- **June 2025** — Region letter: depuratore upgrade only after "homework" incl. PRG (per opposition)
- **26 June 2025** — formal question: Madonna della Neve trail abandoned after public spending
- **11 Sept 2025** — Meli's public warning on the PRG deadline
- **Oct 2025** — formal question: waste-centre relocation
- **Nov/early Dec 2025** — administration resumes contact with Region after opposition records-access request (per opposition)
- **22 Dec 2025** — Region's last letter still asks for digital documents (per opposition)
- **23 Dec 2025** — completed documentation re-sent, days before deadline (per opposition)
- **31 Dec 2025** — **PRG deadline missed** (undisputed; Cascio accepts political responsibility)
